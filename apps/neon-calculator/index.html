About this app

Neon Calculator is a bright, playful calculator with glowing neon keys, big buttons that are easy to hit, and a fast, lightweight interface that works fully offline.

It is free to download and supported by a small banner ad and an occasional full-screen ad. There is no sign-up, no account and no in-app purchase.

Questions

Is Neon Calculator free?

Yes - it is free to download and use. It is supported by a small banner ad and an occasional full-screen ad. There is no sign-up and no in-app purchase.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The calculator itself works fully offline, so it works without Wi-Fi or mobile data. Ads need a connection to load, and the app keeps working without them.

Is it suitable for all ages?

Yes. It is a general-audience calculator with big, easy-to-tap keys. There is no sign-up, no chat and no user-generated content.
