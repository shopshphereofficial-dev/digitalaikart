About this app

Neon Calculator is a bright, playful calculator built for kids and anyone who wants maths to feel fun. Every key glows in neon, the buttons are big and easy to hit, and the whole app works fully offline with no ads and no sign-up.

It is small, fast and free — the perfect first calculator on a child's phone or tablet.

Yes. Neon Calculator works fully offline, so it works without Wi-Fi or mobile data.
