01.Who is Arcee AI?

Arcee AI is a San Francisco company founded in 2023 by Mark McQuade, who previously worked at Hugging Face. For its first couple of years, Arcee focused on post-training Ã‚” taking existing models and adapting them for specific customers. 02.The raise: unicorn status, with Wipro at the table

On September 16, Arcee announced a Series B led by Vista Equity Partners, Cambium Capital and Emergence Capital, with participation from AI10 Ventures, Hitachi, IAG, Microsoft's M12, P7 Ã€œ and, notably for us, Wipro. 03.Trinity Large: big model, clever architecture

The flagship is Trinity Large, a 400-billion-parameter sparse mixture-of-experts model with only about 13 billion parameters active per token. In plain English: the model is huge, but it only wakes up the specialists it needs for each task, which keeps inference cheap. According to the company, it was the first frontier open-weight model developed end-to-end in the United States since Meta stopped releasing Llama models in early 2025 â€” a gap that, until Arcee, was being filled mostly by Chinese labs.
