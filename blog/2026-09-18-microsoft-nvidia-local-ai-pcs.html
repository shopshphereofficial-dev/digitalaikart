Microsoft Nvidia October 7 Event: Local AI PCs Explained

TL;DR

  • Microsoft hosts its biggest Windows and Surface event since May 2024 on October 7 in San Francisco — 10:30 PM IST for us
  • Satya Nadella and Jensen Huang share the stage; the theme is local AI on the PC
  • Nvidia's RTX Spark platform promises up to 1 petaflop of AI compute and 128GB unified memory
  • The Surface Laptop Ultra is expected to run AI models with roughly 120 billion parameters entirely offline

Here's some news that matters if you're planning your next laptop purchase. The Microsoft and Nvidia October 7 event is confirmed for San Francisco, and it's shaping up to be the most important Windows hardware moment in years. Satya Nadella, Jensen Huang, and Windows + Devices chief Pavan Davuluri will all be on one stage, talking about a single idea: AI that runs on your PC instead of someone else's cloud.

01.What's actually happening on October 7?

Microsoft has sent out invites for a Windows and Surface event on October 7, starting 10 AM Pacific — that's 10:30 PM for anyone watching from India. The speaker list is the interesting part: CEO Satya Nadella, Pavan Davuluri (who runs Windows and Devices), and Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang. When two CEOs of companies this size share a stage, real products get announced, not just slideware.

This is Microsoft's first major Windows-focused event since May 2024, when it launched the Copilot+ PC idea with Qualcomm's Arm chips. Back then, the pitch was thin-and-light laptops with small NPUs for gentle tasks like live translation. This event is about a much heavier class of machine. Reports also suggest a Windows 12 announcement is unlikely — the focus stays on Windows 11 and local AI.

02.RTX Spark: a petaflop on your desk

The heart of the event will be Nvidia's RTX Spark platform for Windows PCs. It's built around the GB10 Grace Blackwell superchip, which fuses a Blackwell RTX GPU and a 20-core Grace CPU onto a single module with unified shared memory. The headline numbers: up to one petaflop of AI compute and up to 128GB of unified memory — enough to run AI models with roughly 120 billion parameters completely on the device, with no internet connection needed.

To put that in perspective, most of us today chat with models that live in a data centre somewhere in the US. An RTX Spark machine flips that — the model sits in your laptop. Native CUDA support means the enormous ecosystem of AI tools built for Nvidia hardware just works.

03.The Surface Laptop Ultra takes on the MacBook Pro

The device expected to headline the show is the Surface Laptop Ultra, which Microsoft teased earlier this year as its answer to the MacBook Pro. It runs on a custom Nvidia RTX Spark SoC with the Blackwell GPU, and it's still officially pre-release — final availability is pending regulatory approval, and pricing hasn't been announced. October 7 is widely expected to be when Microsoft finally tells us how much it costs and when we can buy it. There's also a Surface RTX Spark Dev Box aimed at developers who want to test AI agents in-house instead of renting cloud GPUs.

And it's not just Microsoft's own hardware — Dell, Lenovo, and ASUS have already committed to building laptops around the RTX Spark platform, so this is an ecosystem play, not a single fancy machine.

04.Windows 11 is being tuned for it

On the software side, Microsoft has spent the year refining Windows 11 for this hardware. Two things stand out: a Workload Profile Scheduling system that spreads background tasks across the chip's 20 CPU cores, and an improved Prism emulation layer that keeps older x86 applications running smoothly on Arm-based silicon. We covered Microsoft's other big AI moves this week — like ChatGPT landing inside Microsoft Word — and this event is the hardware counterpart to that software push. Nvidia's growing role in PCs also extends to cloud infrastructure, as we saw when Google and Nvidia announced their data centre partnership.

05.Why local AI matters for Indian users

Honestly, what surprised me most about this whole shift is how well it fits Indian realities. Local AI means your documents never leave your device — that's a big deal for CAs handling client financials, lawyers with case files, or doctors with patient records who'd rather not send everything to a foreign server. It means no cloud subscription bills piling up in dollars every month, and it means the AI keeps working when the broadband doesn't. Anyone who's had a "network issue" right before a deadline knows exactly what that's worth.

There's a cost angle too, and it's not obvious. Running everything in the cloud is convenient until you see the bill — if you're building AI workflows, knowing where to spend and where to save is a skill in itself. Key Takeaways

  • Microsoft's October 7 event (10:30 PM IST) is its biggest Windows event since May 2024
  • Nadella, Davuluri, and Jensen Huang present together — local AI on the PC is the theme
  • RTX Spark: up to 1 petaflop of AI compute, 128GB unified memory, ~120B-parameter models offline
  • Surface Laptop Ultra pricing and availability expected; Dell, Lenovo, ASUS building RTX Spark laptops
  • No Windows 12 expected — Windows 11 refinements and the local AI roadmap instead
