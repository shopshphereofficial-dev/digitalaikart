Meta One Launched: Paid AI Subscription for WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook

TL;DR

  • Meta One bundles AI features across Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp and Meta AI into one paid subscription
  • Global pricing starts at $2.99 (~₹250) for single products, $7.99 for Core and $19.99 for Premium per month
  • 15 million subscriptions and trials already happened during the phased rollout
  • The core experience and everyday Meta AI usage stay free — subscriptions unlock more AI generation limits

For years, the deal with Facebook and Instagram was simple: the apps are free, you pay with your attention. On Tuesday, Meta quietly changed the terms of that deal. It launched Meta One, a subscription service that packs more AI capabilities and premium features across Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp and Meta AI. And here's the number that made me sit up: the service has already crossed 15 million subscriptions and trials during its phased introduction.

01.What is Meta One, exactly?

Meta One is a bundle of subscription plans that sit on top of the apps you already use every day. Meta launched the single-product plans earlier this year — Instagram Plus, Facebook Plus and WhatsApp Plus — and Meta One now combines those with something new: higher AI usage. Subscribing gets you more media generation with Meta AI, including creating and editing images and generating videos powered by Meta's Muse models, plus more use of in-app AI tools like Restyle on Instagram.

The pitch is straightforward. The free experience, built for billions of users, has limits. Pay a subscription, and you get expanded creative freedom — more generations, more Restyle edits on your Stories, more voice effects. Meta is rolling it out with more than 50 features across its platforms, with plans to expand to the Edits app and AI glasses over time.

02.The plans and pricing

Global pricing starts at $2.99 a month for single products (roughly ₹250 at current exchange rates), $7.99 for the Core individual bundle (around ₹680), and creator and business bundles from $14.99. The top consumer tier, Premium at $19.99 a month (about ₹1,700), includes everything in Core with the most room to create with Meta AI. Meta hasn't announced India-specific pricing yet, so expect localised numbers when it reaches us fully — Indian pricing usually lands at an adjusted tier, not a straight dollar conversion.

What surprised me most is how quickly people are paying. Market intelligence firm Appfigures estimated Instagram's average daily worldwide revenue hit $1.2 million during the week of September 9 — a 475% jump from the prior week — while Facebook's hit $528,000, up 143%. That was before Meta One even formally launched. Clearly, AI features, not badge customization, are what's opening wallets.

03.Why Meta is charging for AI now

Here's the thing nobody's talking about: this isn't really about features like a blue tick or a "bold follow button". It's about compute costs. Generative AI — especially image and video generation — is brutally expensive to run at scale. Meta monetised attention through ads for two decades, but AI generation doesn't slot neatly into an ad model. Putting AI usage behind a metered subscription is the same playbook OpenAI, Anthropic and Google use to recover training and inference costs.

Analysts estimate subscriptions could add around $13.5 billion to Meta's revenue by 2028 if momentum holds. That's the real story here: Meta is teaching a billion-plus people that AI has a monthly price tag, the same way Netflix taught us about streaming. If the model works, expect every platform you use — Snap, X, TikTok — to follow within a year.

04.What this means for creators and small businesses

The creator and business bundles include professional tools like higher rankings in search results, a verified badge, monitoring for fake impersonation accounts and audience insights. Meta also says it will keep adding agent skills — end-to-end marketing help, business operations support, content creation and optimisation — directly into the apps where your audience already lives.

For an Indian small business running a WhatsApp catalogue or an Instagram shop, that direction matters. Imagine describing your product and having an AI agent draft the campaign, create the visuals and schedule the posts — all inside WhatsApp. That's where this is heading. 05.Is free Meta AI going anywhere?

No — and Meta has said this explicitly. The core experience across Meta's apps and Meta AI stays free. Everyday use of Meta AI, normal posting, messaging, Reels — none of that is being paywalled. What subscriptions unlock is more: more generations, more creative tools, more professional features. It's a usage-based upgrade, not a hostage situation.

Still, read the fine print on those AI usage limits. Meta hasn't published exact caps, saying they can vary by "country, surface and system conditions". If you create content professionally, that vagueness is worth watching before you commit to a plan.

Key Takeaways

  • Meta One launched September 15 with 50+ features across Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp and Meta AI
  • Global pricing: $2.99 single product, $7.99 Core, $19.99 Premium; creator and business bundles from $14.99
  • 15 million subscriptions and trials already happened during phased rollout
  • Subscriptions unlock more AI media generation (Muse models, Restyle, voice effects) — core experience stays free
  • Appfigures data showed Instagram's daily revenue jumped 475% in the week before launch, driven by AI features
