AI to Drive Growth Over Next Decade: Infosys Co-Founder

#AIIndia#Infosys#TechLeadership#AIBusiness

TL;DR

  • Infosys co-founder SD Shibulal projects artificial intelligence to be the primary engine of economic and enterprise growth across the coming decade.
  • The outlook emphasizes the shifting baseline of the global technology services sector toward automated and intelligent systems.
  • Indian professionals and enterprises are urged to align capabilities with long-term technological transformation.

01.A Long-Term Growth Horizon Led by AI

Artificial intelligence is positioned to serve as the defining catalyst for enterprise and economic expansion over the next ten years, according to Infosys co-founder SD Shibulal. The observation comes at a transformative juncture for the broader technology ecosystem, where intelligent automation and machine learning models are transitioning from experimental tools to foundational operational infrastructure across industries.

This decade-long perspective highlights that current AI initiatives are not merely short-term efficiency plays. Instead, the integration of advanced intelligent systems represents a structural shift in how businesses create value, optimize workflows, and build scalable global products.

02.Implications for the Indian IT Ecosystem

For India, where the information technology sector contributes substantially to national economic output and employment, such projections carry strategic importance. Industry pioneers like Shibulal helped build the foundational offshore delivery model that defined Indian tech over past decades, and the pivot to AI marks the next evolution of this legacy.

Global clients increasingly seek technology partners capable of implementing cognitive capabilities, autonomous workflows, and predictive systems. Indian service providers must therefore integrate artificial intelligence deeply across traditional service lines to sustain their competitive advantage and drive continued international growth.

03.Signals for Students and Tech Professionals

For engineering students and working professionals across India, a decade-long growth runway provides clear direction for career development. Traditional software maintenance and routine technical tasks are steadily being automated, making domain expertise in machine learning, data engineering, and system integration essential.

Investing in continuous upskilling and gaining familiarity with foundational AI concepts will be critical for workforce mobility. Those who learn to work alongside AI tools and build solutions on top of emerging intelligent platforms are likely to find themselves at the forefront of hiring demands throughout this expansive growth cycle.

04.Preparing Businesses for the Next Wave

Entrepreneurs and business leaders must interpret this long-term outlook as a mandate for enterprise modern deployment. Adopting artificial intelligence requires clean enterprise data architectures, reliable infrastructure, and a deliberate approach to organizational capability building.

Rather than treating technological shifts as temporary market cycles, Indian enterprises must embed intelligent tooling into their primary business roadmaps to capture the compounding advantages projected over the coming decade.
