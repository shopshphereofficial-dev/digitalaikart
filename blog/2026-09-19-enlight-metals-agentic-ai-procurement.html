Enlight Metals Raises $1.5M for Agentic AI Procurement

#AgenticAI#Procurement#StartupFunding#B2BAI

TL;DR

  • Pune-based Enlight Metals has raised $1.5 million from US-based Exar North Group at a $10 million valuation
  • Its agentic AI platform has cut supplier matching time from 5-7 hours to under three minutes
  • The company claims 75% faster transactions, 30% lower inventory costs and 60% lower operational overheads
  • It's targeting around ₹1,200 crore revenue in the next financial year

While everyone argues about chatbots, a Pune startup is using agentic AI to fix one of the least glamorous — and most expensive — corners of Indian industry: buying steel. Enlight Metals, an agentic AI-enabled metal procurement platform, announced on Friday that it has raised $1.5 million from US-based investment firm Exar North Group at a valuation of $10 million.

01.Steel meets software agents

If you've never worked in manufacturing, here's what procurement actually looks like: dozens of calls to suppliers, price quotes over WhatsApp, hours of haggling, and then a pile of paperwork to match purchase orders with deliveries and invoices. For a mid-sized factory buying steel every week, this is a full-time job for several people — and every delay directly hits production.

Enlight Metals' answer is an agentic AI platform that handles the sourcing and distribution flow — matching buyers with suppliers, chasing quotations, and processing transactions end to end. The investment combines Enlight's steel sourcing expertise with Exar North's capital, technology and operational support. The transaction is expected to be completed in the coming weeks.

02.The numbers Enlight is claiming

According to the company, the platform has reduced end-to-end transaction processing time by 75 per cent, inventory costs by 30 per cent, and operational overheads by 60 per cent. The number that jumped out at me: supplier matching time has fallen from 5-7 hours to less than three minutes.

Think about what that means for a small manufacturer in Bhiwandi or Coimbatore. The owner currently spends a good part of the day just finding the right supplier at the right price. If an agent can shortlist options in minutes, that's time redirected to actually running the business.

Enlight is targeting revenue of around ₹1,200 crore in the next financial year, with growth expected from customer acquisition, geographic expansion, new dark stores, and wider adoption of AI-led operational efficiencies. Vedant Goel, Director at Enlight Metals, said the partnership would provide resources and technology support as the company expands operations.

03.Why procurement is a perfect AI agent use case

Procurement is exactly the kind of work AI agents are built for: structured, repetitive, rule-bound, and drowning in documents. Compare prices across suppliers, check specifications, raise the purchase order, track the delivery, flag the mismatch. Every step is a decision a well-designed agent can make — with a human approving the final spend.

This is part of a bigger shift we've been tracking. Hostinger's new agent now runs websites for its customers, and we wrote about that launch here. Indian companies are also placing serious bets on the AI layer itself — Ola's Krutrim committed ₹100 crore to an AI cloud for Indian developers. Enlight Metals is the applied-India version of the same story: take the agent capability, point it at a real, painful, invoices-and-steel problem, and charge for the outcome.

That's also why this deal is instructive for anyone building in this space. Jay Joshi, Founder and CEO of Exar North Group, said data-driven procurement could meaningfully improve steel sourcing and distribution — an area where most Indian SMEs still run on phone calls and Excel sheets.

Key Takeaways

  • Enlight Metals (Pune) raised $1.5 million from Exar North Group at a $10 million valuation
  • Claims: 75% faster transaction processing, 30% lower inventory costs, 60% lower operational overheads
