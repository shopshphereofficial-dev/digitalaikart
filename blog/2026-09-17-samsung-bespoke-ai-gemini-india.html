Samsung Bespoke AI India Launch: A Gemini-Powered Fridge, AI ACs and the Connected Home

TL;DR

  • Samsung unveiled its 2026 Bespoke AI appliance range in India at 'The Brief Gurugram' event on September 16
  • The Family Hub refrigerator gets AI Vision built with Google Gemini — it recognises food and suggests recipes
  • Bespoke AI WindFree Pro AC is priced at ₹69,990; the Infinite 1-Way Cassette AC starts at ₹1,60,000 with an AI Energy Mode that cuts energy use by up to 30%
  • Everything connects through SmartThings, with Bixby Voice ID, Knox security and up to 7 years of software updates

Samsung just wrapped its 'The Brief Gurugram' event on September 16, and the headline is hard to miss: the company's 2026 Bespoke AI appliance range has launched in India — headlined by a refrigerator with Google Gemini built into it. Yes, Gemini. Inside a fridge. We've officially reached the point where the most boring appliance in your kitchen is getting an AI brain, and honestly, some of it is more useful than it sounds.

01.A fridge that actually runs on Gemini

The star of the show is the Family Hub refrigerator, which now carries a 32-inch touchscreen that Samsung positions as a central hub for family schedules, notes and entertainment. The interesting part is AI Vision built with Google Gemini: the fridge can recognise the food items inside it and suggest recipes through the AI Food Manager. There's also a Video-to-Recipe feature that turns those endless cooking videos we all watch into actual step-by-step recipes, plus a 'Now Brief' function that serves up personalised updates like your schedule and the weather. Samsung has also localised Family Care and Pet Care features for Indian households.

Think about that for a second. The most common kitchen problem in most Indian homes — vegetables forgotten in the back of the fridge until they turn into a science experiment — is now a target for AI. Whether Gemini actually fixes it is a review-day question, but the direction is clear.

02.The ACs are the quiet stars of the Bespoke AI lineup

Everyone will talk about the fridge, but I found the air conditioners more interesting, mainly because they attack an actually painful Indian problem: summer power bills. The Bespoke AI WindFree Pro AC is priced at ₹69,990 and comes with AI-enabled controls plus PM1.0 purification. The Infinite 1-Way Cassette AC, designed specifically for Indian homes, uses 23,000 micro-holes for draft-free cooling and ships in 3.5 kW, 5.2 kW and 7.1 kW capacities starting at ₹1,60,000. Its AI Energy Mode, Samsung claims, can reduce energy use by up to 30%. For a country where the AC is the single biggest line item on the electricity bill for four months a year, that number deserves attention.

03.One ecosystem, seven years of updates

None of these products live alone. Through SmartThings, the appliances are designed to understand routines and manage energy use together — the upgraded Bixby assistant now supports Voice ID so it can tell family members apart and personalise responses. There's a Bespoke AI Laundry Combo that identifies fabric types and adjusts wash cycles on its own, plus the AI Home screen that lets you monitor everything from one interface. Samsung says the range is protected by Knox security and will receive software updates for up to seven years through SmartThings — which is a smartphone-style promise being made about a washing machine. That's genuinely new territory for appliances.

It also continues a theme we've been tracking all week: AI is moving from apps into the physical home. Google recently turned its Home platform into a hub for AI agents, and we covered that shift in detail earlier. Samsung's version of the same bet is hardware-first — and it's arriving on Indian shelves right now.

04.My take: premium prices, but the direction is right

JB Park, President and CEO of Samsung Southwest Asia, said something at the launch that stopped me: "Every second Samsung appliance sold in India today is a Bespoke AI appliance." That's a striking stat for what's supposed to be a premium line. Reality check, though — ₹69,990 for an AC and ₹1,60,000 for a cassette unit are renovation-and-new-home budgets, not impulse purchases. What surprised me most isn't the fridge; it's that AI features are clearly on their way to becoming default rather than deluxe. Give it two upgrade cycles and the recipe-suggesting fridge stops being a novelty.

And here's the thing nobody's talking about: the same "AI quietly optimising costs for you" logic applies to how you use AI at work. Most people bleed money on AI tools simply because nobody ever taught them cost optimization — which is exactly the chapter in our GPT-6 Astra — Zero to Hero Guide that readers tell us pays for the book first.

What This Means for You

If you're mid-renovation or furnishing a new home, this range is worth a serious look — the energy-saving claims alone could matter over an Indian summer. If you're a student or professional watching from the sidelines, don't get distracted by the shiny fridge; the real signal is that AI is becoming ambient, woven into everyday objects.
