Jaishankar Calls AI Mother of All Tech, Urges Inclusiveness

#AIPolicy#IndiaAI#DigitalIndia#TechInclusion

TL;DR

  • S Jaishankar has labeled artificial intelligence the mother of all technologies.
  • A central demand highlighted is the vital need for global and domestic AI inclusiveness.
  • The emphasis reflects India's broader push to prevent digital divides in emerging capabilities.
  • For Indian developers and businesses, inclusiveness underscores broad access to foundational tools.

01.Recognizing the Mother of All Technologies

External Affairs Minister S Jaishankar has termed artificial intelligence the mother of all technologies, putting a clear spotlight on the sheer scale of disruption that advanced computing systems represent. Unlike narrow technological developments that transform isolated industries, AI represents an overarching architectural shift capable of altering every layer of the global economy, scientific research, and national administrative systems.

By categorizing AI at this fundamental tier, the statement underscores that artificial intelligence cannot be viewed merely as another commercial software upgrade. It serves as the foundational substrate upon which future industries, services, and strategic capabilities will be built, making high-level diplomatic and national attention essential.

02.The Core Imperative of Inclusiveness

Central to the minister's observation is the critical call for AI inclusiveness. As artificial intelligence advances rapidly, a key global concern remains the concentration of compute, elite datasets, and intellectual property within a small handful of dominant geographies and corporations. A drive for inclusiveness asserts that the benefits and applications of machine intelligence must be distributed broadly rather than restricted to privileged sectors.

In practical terms, genuine inclusiveness requires lowering the barriers to entry for developing nations and diverse communities. It involves ensuring that AI models are designed to understand varied cultures, languages, and socioeconomic contexts, preventing the emergence of technological divides that widen inequalities between advanced economies and emerging markets.

03.Relevance for Indian Students and Professionals

For students, engineers, and professionals across India, the emphasis on inclusive AI aligns closely with domestic priorities. India possesses one of the world's most vibrant talent pools and developer ecosystems, yet sustained long-term success requires open access to foundational models, computing infrastructure, and practical educational tools.

An inclusive approach directly benefits local problem solvers who are applying technology to everyday national challenges. When emerging tools are accessible and inclusive, developers in smaller cities and non-traditional hubs can construct localized solutions across public services, multilingual digital interfaces, agriculture, and healthcare without facing restrictive technological monopolies.

04.Shaping the Future of Digital Governance

As global discussions around standard setting, data access, and safety frameworks continue to evolve, high-profile calls for inclusiveness signal the direction needed for sustainable policy. Framing AI as the preeminent technology of our era means governance frameworks cannot be shaped solely by proprietary market interests.

Instead, global dialogue must balance innovation with widespread access and ethical deployment. Ensuring that advanced systems serve collective human progress rather than deepening divisions will remain the central test for institutions and policymakers steering this technological shift.

Frequently asked questions

Why was AI termed the mother of all technologies?
Because its impact spans every sector, serving as the foundational infrastructure upon which future economic, scientific, and societal systems will be constructed.

What does AI inclusiveness mean in a policy context?
It refers to ensuring widespread access to AI resources, preventing extreme technological concentration, and making models relevant and accessible to diverse global populations.

How does this focus benefit India's technology ecosystem?
It supports broader access to tools and infrastructure, enabling students, builders, and entrepreneurs across diverse regions to innovate without encountering prohibitive barriers.
