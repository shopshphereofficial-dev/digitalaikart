HiDream V1: New Omnimodal AI Video Model Debuts

#HiDream#AIVideo#Omnimodal#GenerativeAI

TL;DR

  • Beijing-based HiDream.ai has launched HiDream V1, a native omnimodal video generation model that accepts text, images and video as input
  • It generates 1080p videos of 5–20 seconds with audio, character consistency and physical plausibility
  • It debuted at No. 4 on the Artificial Analysis Image-to-Video Leaderboard (with audio) and No. 8 on Arena.ai — on day one
  • The launch came alongside a Series C+ funding round from New Micro Capital, Jiaozi Capital and ICBC Capital

Every week a new AI video model shows up promising Hollywood-quality output, and most weeks I shrug. This week was different. HiDream.ai, the Beijing foundation-model company, just unveiled HiDream-O1-Video-1.0 — HiDream V1 for short — a native omnimodal video generation model. And instead of the usual vague "our demos look great" claims, it arrived with third-party benchmark rankings that immediately put it among the world's best. For anyone in India making content for a living, this launch deserves ten minutes of your attention.

01.What does "omnimodal" actually mean here?

Most video generators do one thing: text in, video out. HiDream V1 is natively multimodal in both directions — you can feed it text, images and video, and it treats all of them as first-class inputs. Want to animate a product photo you shot on your phone? Give it the image. Want to extend or restyle an existing clip? Give it the video. The model plans the output as a single creative task rather than bolting a video stage onto a text-to-image engine — which is exactly why the results hold together better across a shot.

02.What it can produce

The specs are concrete: high-fidelity 1080p videos ranging from 5 to 20 seconds, with enhanced narrative planning, character consistency, physical plausibility and audiovisual synchronisation. That last pair matters more than the resolution. Anyone who has tried current AI video tools knows the two heartbreakers — your character's face morphing between shots, and objects that behave like they've never met gravity. Character consistency and physical plausibility are precisely the two things HiDream says it optimised for, along with syncing the audio to what's happening on screen instead of pasting a soundtrack on top.

03.The benchmark debut that raised eyebrows

Here's the part that separates this launch from the daily flood of press releases. On debut, HiDream V1 ranked No. 4 globally on the Artificial Analysis Image-to-Video Leaderboard (with audio) and No. 8 on the Arena.ai Image-to-Video leaderboard. Those are two independent evaluation platforms — one does standardised benchmark testing, the other uses anonymous head-to-head comparisons voted on by real users. Artificial Analysis is the same leaderboard tracker that has become the de facto scoreboard for model buyers worldwide. A top-five debut puts HiDream in the same conversation as the big-name video models from major US and Chinese labs, and it notably expands China's already strong presence in video generation alongside teams like Seedance and MiniMax.

04."Physics is the next frontier"

HiDream's CTO Yao Ting made a comment that I think will define the next year of video AI: "The next generation of video models will not be defined solely by higher resolution or longer duration. What matters is whether a model can genuinely understand a creator's intent and how objects interact in the real world." That lines up with what we're seeing everywhere — the resolution race is basically over, 1080p is table stakes now, and the differentiator is whether the model understands that a glass of water falls off a table, spills, and doesn't un-spill. The launch essay logic is the same one driving Google DeepMind's world-models push — we covered the $700 million Emulate raise for exactly this kind of research earlier this week.

And the endgame of all this video work is already visible — the first fully AI-generated feature film is out.

05.Backed by serious Chinese capital

Alongside the launch, HiDream announced a Series C+ round backed by New Micro Capital (the corporate VC arm of Shanghai New Micro Technology Group, which manages nearly RMB 10 billion), Jiaozi Capital (a state-backed platform under Chengdu Jiaozi Financial Holding) and ICBC Capital. The money is earmarked for native omnimodal research, computing infrastructure and industry applications. Translation: this is not a research paper looking for a product — it's an industrial group with patient capital building a world-model platform.

06.My honest take — and the caveats

What surprised me most is how quickly the video model hierarchy is shuffling. A company that most Indian creators had never heard of a month ago is now sitting above several household names on a neutral leaderboard. Two honest caveats, though. First, HiDream V1 is currently in internal testing with public availability "in the near future" — so nobody outside the company has stress-tested it yet, and leaderboard positions at launch have a way of settling lower once thousands of users get their hands on a model. Second, benchmarks measure clips, not campaigns. Whether HiDream V1 can hold a character through a 20-second ad with synced dialogue is exactly the thing to verify before betting your client pitch on it. But if you write AI video off because "the leaderboards never change", you're reading yesterday's news.

Key Takeaways

  • HiDream V1 is a native omnimodal video model — text, image and video inputs in one model
  • Output: 1080p, 5–20 seconds, with character consistency, physical plausibility and audiovisual sync
  • Debuted No. 4 on the Artificial Analysis Image-to-Video Leaderboard (with audio), No. 8 on Arena.ai
  • Series C+ backing from New Micro Capital, Jiaozi Capital and ICBC Capital — patient, industrial-scale money
  • Still in internal testing; wider availability expected "in the near future"
