Claude Docs and Slides: Anthropic Merges Everything Into One Claude

#Claude#Anthropic#DocsAndSlides#Productivity

TL;DR

  • Anthropic merged Claude chat and Cowork into a single interface — Claude now picks the right mode itself
  • New beta tools: Claude Docs and Claude Slides, with exports to Word, Google Docs, PowerPoint and PDF
  • Rollout starts with Pro and Max plans on web, desktop and mobile; Team and Free plans come later
  • Agentic work burns way more tokens than chat, so heavy users should watch their usage

If you've ever opened Claude, started typing a message, and then paused wondering whether this task belongs in plain chat or in Cowork — Anthropic just made that decision for you. On Wednesday, the company announced it is combining Claude chat and Cowork into a single interface, and launching Claude Docs and Claude Slides in beta. The visual design tool, Claude Design, is being folded into the main interface too. In short: there is now just one Claude, and it figures out what your task needs on its own.

01.One Claude: the end of tab confusion

Here's the honest backstory. Anthropic built Cowork as a separate place for "bigger work" — multi-step, agentic tasks — and Design for visual work. But users kept getting stuck at the same question: which one do I open? Anthropic admitted this directly, saying people told them the frustrating part was deciding where a task belonged. So instead of a toggle you have to think about, Claude now decides whether your request needs a quick answer, a document, a deck, a design, or a longer-running task — and routes it inside the same conversation.

02.Claude Docs is a real Google Docs rival

This is the part that made me sit up. Claude Docs isn't just "generate a file and download it" — it's a collaborative working document. You ask Claude to draft sections, it asks you clarifying questions, and it comments on choices like a colleague would. Documents start private, then can be shared with teammates who can edit at the same time, with comment threads that look a lot like Google Docs. Everything lives at one shareable link that opens on your phone. And when you need to move out of Claude, you can export to Microsoft Word or Google Docs, with more export destinations planned.

03.Claude Slides: decks straight from a chat

Same idea for presentations. Ask for a deck in an ordinary conversation and Claude drops the slides right into your chat. You can edit individual slides, present directly from Claude, or download the whole thing as a PowerPoint or PDF — which, let's be honest, is still what every client and every professor in India actually wants. There's a neat cross-device touch too: start a document on your desktop and check Claude's progress from the mobile app while you're stuck in traffic.

04.What happens to Cowork — and your bills

The Cowork toggle simply disappears as the rollout reaches your account. Cowork only launched in January 2026 as a research preview, so it had a short life as a brand. But here's the thing nobody's talking about enough: agentic work is expensive. Fortune pointed to a Stanford Digital Economy Lab study that found agentic tasks can consume roughly 1,000 times more tokens than simple chat reasoning. If your casual questions start getting routed through tool-calling machinery by default, bills can climb — so keep an eye on usage if you're on a metered plan. If you want a proper framework for keeping AI costs under control, the cost-optimization chapter in our GPT-6 Astra — Zero to Hero Guide applies to any agentic tool, Claude very much included.

05.The superapp race just got tighter

This launch is really a chess move in the race to build the AI "everything app". OpenAI started folding ChatGPT, its Codex coding agent, and potentially its Atlas browser into a single superapp back in July with ChatGPT Work — and it has been busy on the monetisation side too, as we saw with the ChatGPT Ads rollout we covered yesterday. Google, meanwhile, has spent the year pushing Gemini deeper into Docs, Sheets and Slides, where it enjoys native distribution. Anthropic's answer is to make Claude itself the workspace. Notably, Claude Code — the developer favourite — stays separate for now, and enterprise admins get at least 30 days' notice before the changes hit their organisations. It also fits neatly into Anthropic's broader global expansion, including the Singapore office it just opened for Asia-Pacific.

Key Takeaways

  • Claude chat, Cowork, Artifacts and Claude Design now live in one interface — Claude routes tasks automatically
  • Claude Docs and Claude Slides are in beta, with real-time collaboration and exports to Word, Google Docs, PowerPoint and PDF
  • Pro and Max subscribers get it first over the coming weeks; Team and Free plans follow
  • Agentic tasks can burn ~1,000x more tokens than plain chat — usage discipline matters more than ever
  • The move is Anthropic's answer to ChatGPT Work and Gemini-in-Workspace in the race for the AI superapp

What This Means for You

If you're a student or working professional in India, this quietly removes the biggest excuse for not using AI daily — the friction of switching between tools. Your reports, project decks, and client proposals can now be drafted, discussed, and edited inside one window, then exported to whatever format your college or office demands. For freelancers and small agencies, that's hours saved per week. My suggestion: don't just wait for the rollout to reach your account. Learn how to direct agentic tools properly now — prompting, permissions, and cost control — because that's the skill this new interface rewards.
