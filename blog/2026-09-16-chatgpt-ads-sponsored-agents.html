ChatGPT Ads Goes AI-First: Sponsored Agents, Shopify Integration and What It Means for Indian Businesses

TL;DR

  • OpenAI rolled out a big ChatGPT Ads update on September 16 — including "Sponsored Agents" you can actually converse with after clicking an ad
  • Advertisers can now create and manage campaigns using plain-language prompts in ChatGPT Work and Ads Manager
  • HubSpot is the first CRM partner and Shopify the first ecommerce partner — the Shopify app goes international on September 23
  • For Indian D2C brands, this could become the cheapest "marketing team" they've ever had

OpenAI just made its biggest advertising move yet. On September 16, the company announced a stack of new AI-powered features for ChatGPT Ads — and honestly, this is the update that turns ChatGPT from a place where people ask things into a place where businesses can actually sell things. If you run a small brand in India, this one deserves ten minutes of your attention.

01.What OpenAI actually announced

There were four pieces to the announcement. First, OpenAI is testing Sponsored Agents. Second, ad creation is getting dramatically easier — you can now build campaigns by simply writing a few prompts inside ChatGPT Work. Third, Ads Manager is getting AI creative tools that suggest copy and imagery based on your landing page. And fourth, ChatGPT Ads is plugging directly into the tools businesses already use, with HubSpot as the first CRM partner and Shopify as the first ecommerce partner.

02.Sponsored Agents: ads you can talk to

This is the part that made me sit up. With Sponsored Agents, when someone clicks an ad in ChatGPT, they land in a conversation with a business-sponsored agent instead of a static page or a chatbot script. Think about how you currently shop — you see an ad, click it, get bounced to a website, hunt for the price, give up, DM the brand on Instagram anyway. Now imagine the ad itself answering your questions: does this come in size M, is there COD, what's the delivery time to my pincode?

For a country where half our shopping conversations already happen on WhatsApp, this format feels oddly familiar. The difference is that the agent is right there at the point of curiosity, before the customer gets distracted.

03.Ads Manager now builds ads with you

The second big change is for the people making the ads. Advertisers can use natural-language prompts to create, update, and analyse campaigns directly through the Ads Manager plugin in ChatGPT — turning a website or a brief into a campaign, checking performance, and getting recommendations on what to do next. When drafting an ad, the system suggests copy and images based on your landing page and campaign objective, and you stay in control — you review and edit before anything goes live.

There's also an opt-in "AI-powered text customization" that adapts your headlines and descriptions to fit the context of a conversation and automatically translates ad copy into the user's preferred language. One ad, many languages — no small thing in a market like ours.

Here's where I'll add a small plug that's actually relevant: the gap between "I have a product" and "I have a working ad campaign" is now mostly a prompting problem. That's exactly the kind of skill our GPT-6 Astra — Zero to Hero Guide trains you on — the prompting fundamentals carry straight into tools like Ads Manager, and the guide's cost-optimization chapter will save you from the classic beginner mistake of burning budget on lazy campaigns.

04.Shopify and HubSpot plug straight in

The integrations matter because adopting a new ad channel usually means rebuilding your workflow — and most small businesses simply won't. So OpenAI went where the businesses already are. HubSpot users can now connect a ChatGPT Ads account, create ads, track performance, and follow up on leads right inside HubSpot. Shopify merchants in the US can install the ChatGPT Ads app from the Shopify App Store, and since products are already integrated through Shopify Catalog, campaigns can go live almost immediately. The app rolls out internationally in markets where ChatGPT Ads is available from September 23 — the date Indian Shopify sellers should circle on the calendar.

05.Why this matters for Indian sellers

Here's the thing nobody's talking about: advertising on ChatGPT is still cheap relative to Meta and Google, because the auction isn't crowded yet. Every Indian D2C founder I know complains about rising CPMs on Instagram — the same creative, the same audience, costing more every quarter. A new channel with AI-native ad creation, multilingual copy generation built in, and Shopify integration landing just before the festive season? That's not a coincidence, that's OpenAI aiming squarely at ecommerce.

Early movers always get the arbitrage. The brands that learn conversational ad formats first will pay less for attention than the ones who show up in 2027 when everyone's bidding. If you've been meaning to get serious about AI for your business anyway, this is a decent excuse.

Key Takeaways

  • Sponsored Agents let users chat with a business-sponsored AI agent right after clicking an ad in ChatGPT
  • Ads can now be created with plain-language prompts in ChatGPT Work and Ads Manager, with AI-suggested copy and imagery
  • AI text customization auto-translates ad copy into the user's preferred language
  • HubSpot (first CRM partner) and Shopify (first ecommerce partner) integrations are live; the Shopify app goes international September 23
  • Indian D2C brands get a fresh, less-crowded ad channel right before the festive season
