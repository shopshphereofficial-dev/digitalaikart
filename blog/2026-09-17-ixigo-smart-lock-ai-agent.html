ixigo Smart Lock: AI Agent That Hunts Cheap Flights

#ixigo#TravelAI#AIAgents#FlightDeals

TL;DR

  • ixigo launched Smart Lock — an agentic AI that monitors eligible flight fares 24×7 in the background against your target budget
  • When it finds a match, it locks the fare for 4 hours and notifies you to complete the booking
  • The Smart Lock fee starts at ₹99; if no matching fare is found, the full fee goes back to your ixigo money account
  • Currently limited to domestic one-way flight bookings in India

If you have ever refreshed a flight-booking app fifteen times a day hoping fares will drop before your trip, ixigo has some news for you. On Thursday, the Gurugram-based travel platform launched ixigo Smart Lock — an agentic AI feature that hunts for cheap flight fares on your behalf, around the clock, and locks them in within your budget. The fee starts at just ₹99, which makes this one of the most affordable and practical uses of agentic AI I have seen from an Indian consumer app all year.

01.What exactly is ixigo Smart Lock?

Smart Lock is what ixigo is calling an industry-first agentic AI feature. The difference from a fare alert is the whole point: an alert only tells you that a price changed. Smart Lock actually goes hunting. You share your travel preferences and desired budget, and the AI agent monitors eligible flights in the background, continuously, comparing fares across airlines until it finds one that fits what you are willing to pay.

When it finds a matching fare, it locks that fare for four hours and notifies you — giving you a window to complete the booking before the price moves again. For now, the feature covers domestic one-way flight bookings in India, but the direction of travel is obvious: ixigo wants the waiting and watching to be the AI's job, not yours.

02.How the ₹99 fare-hunting agent works

The flow is simple. Open the ixigo Flights & Hotels app, head to the 'Your AI Agents' section on the home screen, enter your destination, preferred travel dates and budget, and pay the Smart Lock Fee — which starts at ₹99. That is it. The agent goes to work in the background.

You can track everything under the My Trips section: the current fare, historical price movements, your target price, and the live search status. So you are not handing over money and praying — you can see the agent working, which honestly makes it feel less like a black box and more like a very patient assistant who never sleeps.

ixigo's co-founders Rajnish Kumar and Aloke Bajpai framed it well: "Travellers often spend significant time checking the same route repeatedly, hoping that fares will fall before they rise again. With Smart Lock, we are shifting that effort from the traveller to AI agents." The company describes this as another step towards what it calls an "AI-native Peace-of-Mind Travel App" that understands intent and works behind the scenes for travellers.

03.What happens if it can't find your fare

Here is the detail that decides whether this feature is worth trying. If the Smart Lock agent is unable to find a flight matching your preferences within your desired budget, the full Smart Lock Fee is credited back to your ixigo money account — and that balance is 100% redeemable on future flight, hotel and bus bookings.

Note the wording: it is a credit to your ixigo wallet, not a refund to your bank account. It is a retention play, and a smart one. But from a user's point of view, the downside is capped at zero as long as you plan to travel again anyway. For frequent travellers, that makes the experiment almost risk-free.

04.Agentic AI is quietly going mainstream in India

Here's the thing nobody is talking about enough: this launch is part of a much bigger pattern. "Agentic AI" — AI that takes actions instead of just chatting — has been the buzzword of 2026, and it is now showing up in everyday Indian products, not just San Francisco demos. Earlier this week we covered Amazon's Alexa+ launch in India, an assistant designed to shop and book for you. Jio Haptik has been building Solo, an AI marketing team for small businesses. Now ixigo has put a fare-hunting agent inside a travel app your entire family probably already has installed.

When agents move from keynote slides into apps with crore-level user bases, the game changes. Most people will not even realise they are using an AI agent — they will just notice that flights got cheaper and booking got less stressful.

05.Why ixigo is betting big on agents

The context matters here. ixigo, founded in 2007 by Aloke Bajpai and Rajnish Kumar and listed on the NSE and BSE, is India's leading AI-based travel platform by user base — over 57 crore annual active users in FY2026 across its ixigo, ConfirmTkt and Abhibus apps. When a company that size ships an agent-first feature, it is not a gimmick; it is a product strategy.

There is real business logic too. Every Smart Lock that ends in a completed booking is a transaction ixigo captured instead of losing to a rival app, and the fee model adds a fresh revenue layer on top of engagement.
