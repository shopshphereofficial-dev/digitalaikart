ChatGPT Is Now Inside Microsoft Word — and Even Free Users Get It

TL;DR

  • ChatGPT for Word launched September 17 — a sidebar that drafts, edits, proofreads and reformats right inside your document
  • It works on every ChatGPT plan, including Free (usage limits apply), and the same add-in covers PowerPoint and Excel
  • Business and Enterprise customers get a free GPT-5.6 Sol preview in Word from September 17–30, outside their usage limits
  • It turns on by default from October 1 — workspace admins can switch it off

For years, the routine was the same everywhere: type something in Word, open a browser tab for ChatGPT, copy, paste back, fix the formatting that broke in transit. On September 17, OpenAI killed that dance. ChatGPT is now inside Microsoft Word — a sidebar that can draft, edit, proofread and reformat your document without you ever leaving it. And here's the part nobody expected: it's available on every ChatGPT plan, including Free.

01.What actually launched

ChatGPT for Word is an add-in you install from the Microsoft Marketplace, open from the Word ribbon, and sign into with your existing ChatGPT account. Once it's running, it reads the document you have open and can operate on text you select. Ask it to turn your notes and source material into a first draft, rewrite a clunky paragraph, reorganize sections, or tighten headings and numbering — and it does the work in place, instead of handing you a wall of text to re-paste.

02.Yes, free users get it too

This is the headline for most of us. OpenAI confirmed the Word integration works across every ChatGPT plan, Free included, subject to each plan's usage limits. The same Microsoft add-in also works in PowerPoint and Excel — OpenAI already had add-ins for those, so Word completes the full Office trio. Between a student in Kota writing assignments and a chartered accountant in Mumbai drafting audit reports, an enormous amount of Indian work happens in these three apps. Free access to a capable AI layer inside them is a genuinely big deal.

03.What it can do inside your document

The design targets the most annoying weakness of browser-based writing assistants: moving text back and forth between Word and a chat window destroys formatting and creates version chaos. ChatGPT for Word instead works on the open document itself. It can draft an executive summary of your report, shorten a section for a specific audience, rewrite selected passages in a different tone, and flag inconsistent terminology across a long file. With the right plan and permissions, it can also pull in context from connected services like Outlook, SharePoint, Google Workspace and Dropbox.

One honest note: the output quality still depends on how well you ask. "Make this better" gets you mush; a specific, structured prompt gets you something you can actually send to your boss. That's exactly why the GPT-6 Astra — Zero to Hero Guide puts prompting before everything else — the tool is now sitting inside your document, but the skill of directing it is still yours to learn.

04.The fine print worth knowing

It's not magic, and OpenAI's own documentation is refreshingly honest about that. Complex formatting, charts and tables may still need manual correction. The add-in can work with the document that's open in Word, but it can't go rummaging through unrelated files on your laptop. Conversations you have through the add-in stay separate from your main ChatGPT history, and your ChatGPT memory doesn't carry over. Also note: ChatGPT for Word will be enabled by default from October 1 — workspace admins can turn it off via OpenAI's admin console, and Microsoft 365 admins control whether employees can install it at all.

05.Two weeks of Sol, without the meter running

Business and Enterprise customers are getting a sweetener: a free preview of GPT-5.6 Sol in Word from September 17 through September 30, with preview activity excluded from normal usage limits. That matters because Office integrations are context-hungry — a long report means the model processes the source file, your instructions and the revisions, so a full-power model outside your quota is a real preview, not a demo. For Business, Enterprise and Edu accounts, inputs and outputs are not used to train OpenAI's models by default. Curious about the model itself? We wrote up the GPT-6 Sol story here.

06.ChatGPT and Copilot, in the same document

Here's the awkward part: Microsoft sells its own Copilot assistant inside Word, and the models behind Microsoft 365 Copilot come from... OpenAI. Now OpenAI has shipped its own ChatGPT-branded sidebar into the same product. The model partnership survives, but the interface war has moved into the document itself — a company can standardize on Microsoft Office and still end up with ChatGPT sitting right beside the text. It mirrors what Anthropic did this week by folding Cowork into one Claude and launching Docs and Slides — read our coverage of that here. Every AI company has reached the same conclusion: stop making people visit the chatbot, move into the tools where the work already happens.

Key Takeaways

  • ChatGPT for Word is live — a sidebar that drafts, edits, proofreads and reformats inside your open document
  • Works on every plan including Free, within usage limits; the same add-in covers PowerPoint and Excel
  • Limitations: charts and tables may need manual fixes, no access to unrelated local files, no memory carryover
  • GPT-5.6 Sol preview free for Business and Enterprise users September 17–30, outside usage limits
  • On by default from October 1 — admins on both OpenAI and Microsoft 365 sides can control it
