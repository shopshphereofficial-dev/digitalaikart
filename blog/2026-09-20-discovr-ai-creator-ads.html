Discovr AI Launches AI-Native Creator Advertising Platform

#Discovr#AIAdvertising#CreatorEconomy#AdTech#AIMarketing

TL;DR

  • Six-month-old Indian startup Discovr AI has launched an AI-native creator advertising platform
  • It combines creator discovery, AI agents, campaign governance, brand safety and real-time measurement in one place
  • Already works with 10,000+ creators and brands like Adani Wilmar, Kotak Neo and Groww
  • The bootstrapped startup is targeting $25M+ in creator ad spend over the next 12 months, with a US entry planned

While everyone argues about frontier models, the quieter AI story is playing out in Indian marketing rooms. This week, Discovr AI officially launched its AI-native creator advertising platform — a product that wants to do for creator campaigns what Google Ads did for search: make them data-driven, automated and measurable. And the team isn't thinking small. They're targeting more than $25 million in creator advertising spend facilitated through the platform over the next twelve months.

01.What exactly is Discovr AI selling?

Founded just six months ago, Discovr AI sits at the intersection of two very Indian realities. One, creator marketing has exploded — every brand from fintech to fairness creams wants influencers now. Two, actually running a creator campaign is still painfully manual: finding the right creators, negotiating, checking brand safety, tracking whether any of it worked.

The platform bundles all of it together — creator intelligence for discovery, AI agents that execute campaigns at scale, campaign governance, brand safety monitoring, and real-time analytics that connect creator spend to actual business outcomes. Think of it as an execution engine rather than another influencer discovery database.

02.The brands already on board

Here's the part that surprised me most: a startup younger than most YouTube channels is already working with Adani Wilmar, Wispr Flow, Kotak Neo and Groww, and has enabled thousands of creator-brand collaborations across fintech, technology, consumer, beauty, lifestyle, SaaS and e-commerce. It has worked with more than 10,000 creators so far, and claims to be facilitating a few million dollars in creator spend this year already.

For context on how fast the advertising side of AI is moving, we covered ChatGPT's move into sponsored results and ads earlier — Discovr is riding the same shift, just from the creator side of the table.

03.Why founders Rosanlal Behera and Achyuta Chand believe now is the moment

"Creator advertising is rapidly evolving into a mainstream media channel, but brands still face challenges in scaling campaigns intelligently and connecting it to measurable business outcomes," said co-founder Rosanlal Behera. Co-founder Achyuta Chand's argument is simpler: creators are one of the most influential channels for consumer engagement, yet the category remains underpenetrated compared to search, social and performance marketing.

That gap is exactly what AI agents are good at closing. If a machine can shortlist creators, negotiate rates, flag unsafe content and generate performance reports overnight, the cost of running a serious creator campaign collapses — which is precisely how smaller Indian brands end up competing with the big ad budgets. It's the same automation logic behind the AI agents making phone calls we wrote about earlier — agents quietly doing operational work humans used to bill hours for.

04.Bootstrapped, revenue-first, and now eyeing the US

No venture round, no splashy announcement — the company says it is currently bootstrapped with strong revenue traction. The plan now is to deepen enterprise adoption, strengthen the AI workflow and measurement stack, expand managed execution, and enter international markets, including the United States.

Honestly, that's the detail worth sitting with. Indian startup folklore says you raise big or you die. A bootstrapped creator-tech company from India telling US brands it will manage $25 million of their creator spend is a different kind of ambition — the kind built on invoices, not decks.
