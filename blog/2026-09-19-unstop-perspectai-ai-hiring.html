Unstop Buys PerspectAI: Games Will Test Your Job Fit

TL;DR

  • Unstop, India's largest campus hiring platform, has acquired PerspectAI in an all-equity deal
  • PerspectAI uses game-based, AI-scored assessments to measure employability, role fit and "future potential"
  • Its clients already include Tata Sons, Swiggy, Aditya Birla Capital and Motilal Oswal — and it was profitable in FY26
  • For students, talent profiles may soon go beyond resumes to include behavioural and cognitive signals

If you're a student who has ever panicked the night before an aptitude test, here's some news worth your attention: Unstop, the platform behind half the hackathons and case competitions on Indian campuses, is acquiring PerspectAI — a company that measures job fit through games scored by AI. The deal was announced this week and it quietly changes how campus hiring in India is going to work.

01.What just happened

Unstop, which calls itself India's largest talent engagement and hiring platform and has a community of more than 32 million students and professionals, announced it has entered into an agreement to acquire PerspectAI. The transaction is structured as an all-equity swap with no cash consideration — Unstop has picked up 100 percent of the company, covering its full business, technology platform, intellectual property, data, client relationships and revenue.

And here's the detail that made me sit up: PerspectAI was profitable in FY26 heading into the deal. This isn't a struggling startup looking for an exit — it's a profitable, operating business with paying enterprise customers joining a bigger platform to scale faster.

02.What PerspectAI actually does

PerspectAI works at the intersection of what it calls Game Science, Data Science and People Science. In plain English: instead of a multiple-choice test that checks what you know, you play carefully designed games grounded in psychometrics, cognitive science and behavioural science — and AI turns how you play into signals about how you think, learn, communicate, solve problems and respond to real-world situations.

Those signals become indicators of Employability, Role Fit, Future Potential and workplace performance — things a resume simply cannot show. The company's enterprise clients include Aditya Birla Capital, Tata Sons, Swiggy and Motilal Oswal, across banking, education, manufacturing and consumer tech. Co-founder and CEO Jignesh Talasila described the founding idea simply: "Can we really understand human potential using games and AI?" After the acquisition closes, Talasila will lead Unstop's expansion into Education and Government, while co-founder and CTO Suraj Vanka joins Unstop's product leadership.

03.Why resumes aren't enough anymore

Two forces are colliding in Indian hiring right now. First, AI-generated resumes and cover letters have made paper credentials cheap — when anyone can sound impressive on paper, employers start hunting for signals that can't be faked. Recruiters are already fighting AI-enabled cheating and impersonation in online assessments, which is why AI-powered proctoring has been booming.

Second, the skills companies need are shifting faster than syllabi. AI-related hiring in India's IT sector rose 16% year-on-year in June, even as overall IT recruitment fell 3% — companies are hiring for AI capability and potential, not just experience. Unstop founder and CEO Ankit Aggarwal's reasoning for the deal fits exactly into this gap: "PerspectAI is not a bet on a roadmap. It is a product that large, demanding enterprises already trust and pay for." The plan is to make these assessment tools available across Unstop's entire customer base — campus recruiters, lateral hiring teams and large enterprises. If you read our earlier piece on how AI is sawing off entry-level jobs, you'll see why companies are desperate for better ways to spot potential early.

Here's the thing nobody's talking about, though: every game you play generates behavioural and cognitive data about you, and that data will now flow into a platform used by thousands of employers. Used well, that's a fairer shot for candidates from tier-2 and tier-3 colleges with thin resumes. Used carelessly, it's a permanent, scored profile you never interviewed for. Data governance questions around behavioural assessment are real, and students should be aware of what's being collected and how it's used. 04.What changes for students and job seekers

For the 32 million-strong Unstop community, the biggest visible change will be richer talent profiles — combinations of demonstrated work, skills and scientifically derived indicators of capability, instead of just self-reported achievements and CGPA. In theory, a final-year student from a small-town college with strong problem-solving signals gets a fairer read against a metro kid with a polished resume. That's the promise, at least, and it's a good one.

The government is pushing in the same direction on skilling — we broke down the one-crore AI training target here — and assessment technology like this is what will decide who actually absorbed those skills.
