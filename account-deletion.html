The short version

Our Android apps do not create an account for you and do not store your personal data on our servers. Everything an app keeps — settings, downloads, recordings, saved chats — lives on your own phone, so you can delete it yourself in a few seconds. If you have ever emailed or messaged us for support, write to us and we will delete that too.

1. Delete app data on your device

  • Clear one app's data: open Android Settings → Apps → pick the app → Storage & cache → Clear storage. This removes its settings, history and saved files.
  • Remove the app completely: Android Settings → Apps → pick the app → Uninstall. All data the app held on the device is deleted with it.
  • Revoke a permission: Android Settings → Apps → pick the app → Permissions. You can turn off camera, microphone, storage, notification access or display-over-other-apps at any time.
  • TwinSpace device administrator: Android Settings → Security → Device admin apps → turn off TwinSpace, then uninstall.

2. Ask us to delete anything you sent us

If you contacted us — a support email, a WhatsApp message, an order query, a bug report with a screenshot — send a deletion request and we will erase it.

What we delete, and what we must keep

  • Deleted: support conversations, contact details you shared with us, screenshots or files you sent, and any note we made while helping you.
  • Kept: purchase records that Indian tax and accounting law requires us to retain (order value, date and payment reference from Razorpay) for the statutory period. These records do not include your card, UPI or bank details — Razorpay holds those, and we never see them.

How long it takes
