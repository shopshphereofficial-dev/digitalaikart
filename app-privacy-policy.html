Android App Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Operated by: UTILITYNEST, a registered micro enterprise (MSME / Udyam, Govt. of India), Qazibagh, Ichgam, Budgam, Jammu & Kashmir 191111, India. Digitalaikart is the brand name under which UTILITYNEST publishes these apps. Founder / authorised representative: Faisal Fayaz Lone.

This policy covers every Android application published by UTILITYNEST under the Digitalaikart brand and listed at digitalkartai.shop/apps/. It explains what the apps can access, what we do and do not collect, and how you can have your data removed. If you use the website as well, the website privacy policy also applies.

Summary

  • We do not run an account or sign-up system for these apps. You do not create a username or password, and we do not hold a profile about you.
  • We do not use third-party advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs in these apps. There are no ad networks and no trackers.
  • We do not sell, rent or share app data with anyone.
  • App settings and content you create stay on your own device.
  • Some apps ask for a specific Android permission in order to work. Each one is listed below with the reason, and every permission is optional — the app simply cannot do that one job without it.

Permissions used by each app

The permissions below are the ones declared by the current release of each app. You can review and revoke any of them at any time in Android Settings → Apps → (app name) → Permissions.

Neon Calculator

  • Internet, network state — used only to check whether a connection exists. The app works fully offline.

Neon Piano + Calculator

  • No permissions are requested. The app runs entirely offline.

Car Simulator

  • No permissions are requested. The game runs entirely offline.

GrabIt

  • Internet, network state — to load the pages and files you ask it to download.
  • Storage / all-files access (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) — to save downloads to the folder you choose and to show your own media files in the cleaner. The app only reads the files needed for the action you start.
  • Foreground service, wake lock — to keep a download running while the screen is off, with a visible notification.
  • Notifications — to show download progress and completion.

SoulStream

  • Internet, network state — to play and fetch the media you request.
  • Storage / all-files access — to list and play your own media files and save downloads.
  • Foreground service, wake lock — so playback or a transfer continues in the background with a visible notification.
  • Notifications — for playback and transfer status.

PulseChat

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO), audio settings — to record voice messages and make voice calls when you tap the microphone or call button.
  • Camera — to take photos or scan a code, only when you start the camera from inside the app.
  • Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT) — to find and connect to nearby devices for chat and calling.
  • Internet, network state, notifications — to deliver messages and notify you of new ones.

TwinSpace

  • Package visibility (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES) — the app must be able to see which apps are installed on the device so it can create and launch a second copy of the app you pick. It does not send your app list anywhere.
  • Device administrator — used only for the optional protection feature that prevents the second space from being uninstalled by someone else. You enable it yourself and can turn it off at any time in Settings → Security → Device admin apps.

VoltAura

  • Notification access — to read notification content so the app can show your alerts in its own dashboard. Notification data stays on your device.
  • Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW) — to show its overlay and dashboard on top of other apps.
  • Foreground service, notifications — to keep the dashboard running with a visible notification.

Data we collect

We do not collect personal information through these apps. Specifically:

  • No account, name, email or phone number is required to use an app.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDK is included.
  • Content you create (recordings, downloads, settings, saved chats) is stored in the app's own storage on your device, and is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.
  • If you contact us for support, we receive whatever you choose to send us — typically your email address or phone number and your message — and we use it only to answer you.

Third-party services

  • Download hosting — the Android packages are hosted as release files on GitHub. Downloading an APK from us is subject to GitHub's own privacy practices.
  • Google Play — once an app is distributed through Google Play, Google processes the install, update and (if you use it) payment data under Google's privacy policy. We only see aggregate install and rating information, never your personal details.
  • We do not share app data with any other third party.

Children

Our apps are general-audience apps, and some (such as Neon Calculator and Neon Piano) are made with children in mind. None of them collects personal information, shows advertising or includes in-app purchases or chat with strangers, so they are safe for children to use. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email or WhatsApp, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

The apps run locally on your device and do not transmit your personal data to our servers, so there is no store of your information on our side to be breached. Data you keep in the app is protected by your device's own security — screen lock, encryption and app sandboxing.

Data retention and deletion

Because the apps keep their data on your device, uninstalling an app or clearing its data in Android Settings removes that data immediately. If you have sent us a support email or message, we keep it only as long as needed to resolve your issue and delete it on request. Full instructions are on the data deletion page.

Changes to this policy

If an app starts using a new permission or a new third-party service, we will update this page and change the "last updated" date above before the new version is released.
