Delete your Caption Studio account

You can permanently delete your Caption Studio account and associated server-side data in either of these ways.

From the Android app

  1. Open Caption Studio and sign in.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Choose Delete my account.
  4. Review the warning and confirm Delete forever.

The app sends an authenticated deletion request. This removes your Supabase sign-in account and associated server-side profile, credit balance/grants, notifications, reports and usage/session records linked to that account.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email lonefaisal977@gmail.com with the subject Caption Studio account deletion request. Include the Google account email used to sign in. We may ask for reasonable verification before processing the request. Do not send passwords, API keys or other credentials.

What is not deleted by the server request

Drafts, edited captions, thumbnails and exported videos stored only on your phone are not deleted from the device by account deletion. Delete those separately in the app or from your device. Audio already sent to third-party AI providers is subject to their retention policies. Information may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention or legal obligations.

For related information, read the Caption Studio Privacy Policy.
