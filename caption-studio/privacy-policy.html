Privacy Policy

Caption Studio
Effective date: 9 November 2026

This policy explains what Caption Studio collects and how it is used when you use the Android app.

Information we process

  • Account information: when you sign in with Google, Supabase receives your Google account identifier and email address. We store account and credit information needed to operate the service.
  • Service activity: app version, Android platform, last-seen/session events, caption-generation duration, selected language, engine and credit usage.
  • Support reports: caption text you deliberately submit through the in-app report feature, plus report status and any support response.
  • Advertising: the free experience uses Google AdMob rewarded ads. Google may process advertising identifiers, device information and related signals as described in Google's privacy disclosures.

Videos, audio and captions

Your selected video is not uploaded as a video file. To generate captions, Caption Studio extracts audio on your device and sends audio data to our Supabase Edge Function. The service forwards it to the selected speech provider (Groq or Google Gemini) and returns the transcript. If you request translation or emoji assistance, relevant caption text is sent to our server and AI provider for that request. We do not promise immediate deletion from third-party AI provider systems; their retention and processing are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Local projects and exports

Drafts, caption edits, styles, thumbnails and exported videos are stored locally on your device. They are not synced to your Caption Studio account. They remain on your device after account deletion unless you delete them separately, clear app data or uninstall the app.

Service providers and sharing

We use Supabase for authentication, hosting and database services; Groq and Google Gemini for speech or text processing; and Google AdMob for rewarded advertising. We do not sell personal information. Providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Account-related records are retained while your account is active. You can request permanent account deletion from Settings → Delete my account inside the app. You can also request deletion externally at the account deletion page. Deleting your account removes the sign-in account and associated server-side profile, credits and linked service records. Local drafts and exports on your phone are not deleted by the server request. Data already sent to AI providers may be subject to their retention policies. Some records may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention or legal obligations.

Security

App-to-server communication uses HTTPS. Session tokens are stored in the app's private device storage. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

Caption Studio is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the service changes. For privacy questions or account-deletion help, contact lonefaisal977@gmail.com.
