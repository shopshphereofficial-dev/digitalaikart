Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Is this playbook official? Can I trust the numbers?

It is an independent guide, not a government publication. Every figure is taken from official sources — the MNRE scheme guidelines, Press Information Bureau releases, the National Portal at pmsuryaghar.gov.in, the Electricity (Rights of Consumers) Rules and public-sector bank rate sheets — and the guide tells you exactly where to re-check anything that can change.

I live in a flat. Is this for me?

Yes. The playbook has a dedicated chapter on solar for flats, housing societies and shared terraces — including the RWA route, where a society can claim Rs. 18,000 per kWp up to 500 kWp for common facilities.

How much subsidy can I actually get?

Under PM Surya Ghar, the central subsidy is Rs. 30,000 for 1 kW, Rs. 60,000 for 2 kW and Rs. 78,000 for 3 kW or larger — capped at Rs. 78,000. Several states add a top-up on top of this. The playbook explains the exact structure and how to check your state's offer.

Does the guide help me avoid scams?

Yes. There are full chapters on the 2026 scam landscape (fake approval SMS, lookalike portals, upfront-fee fraud, non-ALMM equipment substitution), vendor red flags, and the exact grievance escalation ladder — from the portal's grievance system to the MNRE helpline, CPGRAMS and the consumer helpline.
