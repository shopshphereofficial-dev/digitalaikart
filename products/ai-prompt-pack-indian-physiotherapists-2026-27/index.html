Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Is this pack written for Indian practices?

Yes. Every prompt assumes an Indian setting: home visits in traffic, WhatsApp as the main channel, package billing in sessions, family members in the consultation, Hindi and Hinglish alongside English, and patients who ask whether insurance will pay.

Does it work with a free AI account?

Yes. The prompts work on the free tiers of ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude. Part 1 lists the current paid tiers and India prices in case you later want one, but nothing in the pack needs a paid plan.

Will the AI diagnose my patients?

No, and the pack is built so it cannot pretend to. The prompts draft structure, language, checklists and options. Every prompt carries a safety line that forbids inventing diagnoses, dosages, guideline citations or statistics, and a verify line that tells you what to check before anything reaches a patient.

Is it safe to paste patient details?

Not raw. Part 2 gives a two-minute de-identification routine and two prompts — one that rewrites your note without identifiers, one that audits a brief before you paste it. Part 1 sets out the DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025 timeline in plain dates.

I am a home-visit physiotherapist with no clinic. Does it still fit?

That is the setting the pack was written around. There are dedicated prompts for travel-time planning, home safety, caregiver training, the first phone call, the family briefing and equipment substitutions when there is no plinth.
