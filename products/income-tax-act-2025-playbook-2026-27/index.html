Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Did my income tax actually change because of the new Act?

Your tax did not change. The Income-tax Act, 2025 replaced the 1961 Act on 1 April 2026, but rates, slabs and deduction limits were left intact — the Government called it revenue-neutral. What changed is the language, the section numbers and the forms. The guide shows you exactly what moved and what stayed the same.

I am a salaried employee. Is this guide for me?

Yes. It covers the new regime and old regime comparison with worked examples, the Rs 12 lakh rebate and the marginal-relief cliff, your new Form 130, the perquisite changes, HRA proof rules and a step-by-step action plan for salaried taxpayers. Freelancers, business owners, senior citizens and NRIs are covered too.

Is every fact verified from official sources?

Yes. Every rate, slab, form number and section mapping comes from official sources — the Income-tax Act, 2025, the Income-tax Rules, 2026, the Finance Act, 2026, Press Information Bureau releases, CBDT FAQs and the incometax.gov.in e-filing portal — current as of October 2026. Where a number could change in a future budget, the guide says so.

Which return do I file this year — old law or new?

For income earned up to 31 March 2026 you still file under the old 1961 Act, for AY 2026-27, using the old forms and old section numbers. The new Act governs Tax Year 2026-27, which you file in 2027. The guide explains the transition timeline clearly so you never mix the two.

Will this help me choose between the old and new tax regimes?

That is one of its core jobs. It gives you the full comparison at every income level, four fully computed case studies, and a clear rule of thumb, so you can see in minutes which regime leaves you with more money.
