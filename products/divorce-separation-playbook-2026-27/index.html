Why This One Is Different

Three things almost no free article on divorce in India gets right

It names the right section

Christian couples need two years of separation under section 10A — not one. A court marriage is governed by the Special Marriage Act even if both of you are Hindu. Getting this wrong costs months, and most online guides get it wrong.

It refuses to invent a formula

There is no statutory percentage for alimony, and courts have said so repeatedly. Instead of a made-up 25%, the book shows you the factors a court actually weighs and how to present need and capacity so a judge can use them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a substitute for a lawyer?

No, and the book says so on its second page. It is an information guide: what the statutes say, what the Supreme Court has held, what things cost and where people go wrong. It will not draft your petition. It will make you a client who can tell a careful advocate from a careless one, and who does not sign a settlement with an open-ended clause in it.

Which laws does it cover?

All five that govern marriage in India — the Hindu Marriage Act 1955, the Special Marriage Act 1954, the Divorce Act 1869 for Christians, the Parsi Marriage and Divorce Act 1936, and Muslim personal law including the 1939 Dissolution of Muslim Marriages Act and the 2019 Act on triple talaq. It also covers the Uttarakhand Uniform Civil Code, in force since 27 January 2025 and amended twice since.

How current is the law in it?

Every section, time limit and judgment was checked against the statute or the court's own order in October 2026. That includes the Supreme Court's 2 February 2026 order in Misha Somani v. Rituraj Somani on waiving the cooling-off period, and the Allahabad High Court's October 2026 directions on enforcing maintenance orders.

What if my spouse and I already agree?

That is the case it was written for. Part Two is a complete mutual-consent system: the sixteen terms your settlement must nail down, the two motions step by step, what goes into a waiver application that actually works, the documents that decide whether your file moves, and what it costs.

Does it cover money and property?

Yes. Part Four covers the four separate doors to maintenance, the affidavit of disclosure both parties must file, how quantum is argued, how to enforce an order when the money stops, stridhan and the joint home loan trap, and the tax treatment of lump-sum versus monthly alimony.
