Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

How much security deposit is legal in India?

In most of India there is no statutory cap — the deposit is whatever you agree. Only Assam and Uttar Pradesh cap homes at two months and Tamil Nadu and Andhra Pradesh at three months, plus a few union territories. In Bengaluru, Mumbai and Delhi there is no cap.

Is the Karnataka two-month deposit cap real?

No. The Karnataka Rent (Amendment) Act, 2025, in force from 8 January 2026, only decriminalised offences and raised penalties. It did not cap deposits, and the eight- to ten-month Bengaluru deposit remains lawful. The playbook explains the correct position — and how to negotiate the number down anyway.

Does it cover tax?

Yes. It covers the HRA exemption under the old regime (including the four new 50% metro cities from FY 2026-27), the landlord PAN rule above Rs 1,00,000 a year, and TDS on rent above Rs 50,000 a month under the Income-tax Act, 2025.

How do I get my deposit back if the landlord refuses?

The playbook sets out the full escalation ladder — reminder, registered-post demand letter, legal notice, then the Rent Authority in MTA states or a civil suit elsewhere, with a three-year limitation. It includes a ready-to-send demand letter.

Is this legal advice?

No. It is general information and a practical system, built from official and primary sources, not legal advice for your specific situation.
