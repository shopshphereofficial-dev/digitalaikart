Who is this playbook written for?

Small and first-time exporters — a manufacturer shipping a first container, a proprietor selling through a marketplace's global programme, a craft business sending courier parcels, a trading house doing merchant exports, or a services firm invoicing overseas clients. It assumes you have a buyer and a product, and no compliance team.

Is the content current for 2026-27?

Yes. Every rule is tied to a primary document as it stood in November 2026 — the FEMA (Export and Import of Goods and Services) Regulations 2026 in force from 1 October 2026, RoDTEP continued to 31 December 2026 by DGFT notification 41/2026-27, the courier export value cap removed from 1 April 2026, and the 2026 GST refund mechanism changes.
