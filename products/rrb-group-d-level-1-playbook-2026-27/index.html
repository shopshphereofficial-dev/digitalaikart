The Fact That Changes How You Prepare

Three times the vacancies are called to the PET. One per vacancy reaches document verification.

At 22,195 posts, roughly 66,585 candidates are called for the Physical Efficiency Test. Document Verification is then held at one candidate per vacancy. Even if every shortlisted candidate cleared the PET, at most one in three could go further. The PET is qualifying, so clearing it adds nothing to your score — your rank is your CBT mark and nothing else.

Most candidates find this out after the shortlist. It is the reason this book puts the physical and medical standards before the syllabus, and the reason the ten-week plan starts now rather than the week the result appears.

Everything you need to know before buying

Is this a mock test series or a study guide?

Neither, and that is deliberate. It is a process playbook: how the four selection stages actually work, how the shortlists are built, what the physical and medical standards say in plain words, and which decisions you cannot undo. There are worked examples in the CBT chapter and a study plan, but it is not a question bank.

Is the information official?

Every figure traces to a named official document: CEN No. 09/2025 as published on the RRB domains, the Railway Board's Annual Recruitment Calendar 2026-27 issued on 12 December 2025, the Indian Railway Medical Manual Volume I Chapter 5, and the Eighth Central Pay Commission's own site. The sources list at the back names all eight documents. Where something is an expectation rather than a published fact, the book says so.

My CBT result just came out. Is this still useful?

It is the most useful part of the book for you. Part 7 has the exact PET standards, the sandbag technique comparison, a ten-week plan and a compressed four-week plan for candidates who were shortlisted today. Part 8 decodes the medical standard for your post, and Part 9 is the document verification file.

I am waiting for the next notification. Where do I start?

Parts 3, 4 and 5, in that order, then Part 11. The choices you make in the application form decide which medical standard you are judged against a year later, and the certificates you need take four to eight weeks to obtain, so they are the right thing to start this month.

Does it tell me the exam date or how many posts?

No, and no honest book can. The Railway Board calendar places Level-1 posts in the October to December 2026 quarter with a draft notification targeted for October 2026, and as of 13 November 2026 no Level-1 CEN for 2026-27 has been published. The book explains exactly what is confirmed and what is press expectation, so you can tell the difference when you read a headline.
