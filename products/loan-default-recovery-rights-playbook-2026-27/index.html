What You'll Learn

Six things almost nobody knows before they need them

🕗 The 8-to-7 rule

Recovery contact is allowed only between 8:00 am and 7:00 pm, only with the borrower or guarantor, and never on a bereavement or a medical emergency.

📱 The 30/60 phone rule

A financed phone can be locked only after 30 days past due, never blocking incoming calls, SMS or SOS, and must be unlocked within an hour of payment. Wrongful locking carries Rs 250 per hour.

⚖️ The SARFAESI clock

60 days on the demand notice, a mandatory reasoned reply in 15, then 45 days to the tribunal from each enforcement measure. Every period, in one table.

🧾 The charge audit

Since January 2024 a penalty cannot be a penal interest rate and cannot earn interest. A charge not in your Key Facts Statement cannot be levied without consent.

🤝 Settlement done properly

The five documents to demand before you pay a rupee, what the letter must say, and what happens to your credit report for the next seven years.

🆓 The free escalation ladder

Lender's grievance officer, then the RBI Complaint Management System at cms.rbi.org.in. No fee, no lawyer, and up to Rs 1 lakh for harassment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

I have already missed EMIs. Is it too late to use this?

No — it is written for exactly that position. Section 1 tells you which of the five default stages you are in, and the options narrow as the day count rises, so the playbook is most valuable in the first 89 days. It also covers what to do after a Section 13(2) notice and after settlement.

Does this tell me how to avoid paying my loan?

No. A loan is a contract and this book says so plainly. What it does is put the rules, the timelines and the paperwork in one place so you negotiate from knowledge instead of fear — the restructuring options, the settlement process, the recovery conduct limits and the free escalation routes.

Is the new RBI recovery framework covered?

Yes. Section 7 sets out what changes on 1 January 2027 — certified recovery agents, the published agency list, the named grievance officer, six months of call recordings and the compensation clause — and section 5 sets out the conduct rules already in force today.

My loan is with an NBFC or a housing finance company, not a bank. Does this apply?

Yes. The recovery conduct directions, the Key Facts Statement rules, the penal charges circular, the digital lending directions and the RBI Ombudsman scheme all cover NBFCs and housing finance companies as well as banks. Where a rule applies only to one type of lender, the playbook says so.

I already settled. Is there anything in here for me?

Sections 25 to 28. They explain what a 'Settled' status does to your file for the next seven years and the three-step route to have it changed to 'Closed' once the balance is cleared, plus the 12-month rebuild sequence.
