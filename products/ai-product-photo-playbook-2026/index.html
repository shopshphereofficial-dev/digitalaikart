Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Do I need a designer or Photoshop?

No. The playbook is built for sellers and creators who are not designers. You shoot one clean photo on your phone, paste a prompt, and finish the image in a free editor. Every step is written in plain, simple English.

Which AI tools does it cover, and are they current?

It covers the tools that shipped in October 2026 — Google Nano Banana 2.1, Nano Banana Pro, OpenAI GPT Image 2.5 (Sunburst and Flare) and Black Forest Labs FLUX 3 Image — plus free editors like Photoroom, Canva and PhotoCraft. Every model name and price was verified from the provider's own page in October 2026.

Will AI images get my marketplace listing suppressed?

Not if you follow the rules. Chapter 5 gives you the exact requirements for an Amazon main image — pure white background (RGB 255,255,255), the product filling about 85 per cent of the frame, at least 1,000 pixels, and no text or logos — plus the sizes for Shopify, Etsy, Instagram and WhatsApp. The book's core rule is simple: AI drafts, you verify against the real product.

Is this useful right now, in the festive season?

Yes. Chapter 6 is a ready festive calendar from Karwa Chauth and Diwali through Christmas and New Year, with the image set to make for each occasion and when to publish. You can dress a whole catalogue for the season in one afternoon.

How much do AI product images cost?

About ₹3 per 1K image and around ₹10 per 4K image on Nano Banana 2.1, with GPT Image 2.5 at roughly ₹3 to ₹12. A 10-product catalogue of seven images each works out to a few hundred rupees of generation — against a studio shoot that starts in the tens of thousands. Chapter 7 shows the full maths.
