Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Do I really need an FSSAI licence to sell food from home?

Yes. The Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 applies to anyone who manufactures, stores, sells or distributes food, with no exemption for home kitchens. Basic Registration costs about ₹100 a year.

What changed in the April 2026 FSSAI reform?

From 1 April 2026 FSSAI raised the Basic Registration turnover ceiling from ₹12 lakh to ₹1.5 crore, moved the State Licence band to ₹1.5-50 crore and the Central Licence above ₹50 crore, and made registrations and licences perpetually valid. A home kitchen can now scale to ₹1.5 crore a year on the same ₹100 registration.

Do I need GST to list on Swiggy or Zomato?

Not automatically. Restaurant service through these apps is a Section 9(5) supply, which is excluded from the mandatory e-commerce registration rule under Section 24(ix). You register only when turnover crosses ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh in special-category states), though platforms may ask for a GSTIN or an exemption declaration as a commercial condition.

Is this only for tiffin services?

No. It covers the three home models — a tiffin or dabba service, a home bakery, and a delivery-only cloud kitchen — and the same legal, pricing and channel playbook applies to all three.

Is the content up to date?

Yes. Every rule, fee and threshold is drawn from official sources — FSSAI, FoSCoS, CBIC and the CGST Act, the MSME Ministry, MoFPI, MUDRA and the Income-tax Act — current as of October 2026.
