Two Things Most Aspirants Never Find Out

Both are published. Neither is explained.

UCEED Part B is a gate, not a score

The Part B qualifying cut-off is 15 out of 100 for the open category. A candidate scoring 190 out of 200 in Part A and 14 in Part B receives no rank at all — the Part A brilliance is discarded. Chapter 14 explains why this inverts most people's preparation.

NIFT's CAT is worth more than everything else

GAT 30 per cent, CAT 50 per cent, Situation Test 20 per cent. The GAT — the part with past papers, mock series and coaching batches — is the smallest slice. Chapter 16 shows two candidates where the one who drilled the GAT loses by eleven marks.

The seat arithmetic

245 UCEED seats across seven IITs. 3,423 NIFT B.Des seats. Nine seats per discipline at each NID campus. Chapter 3 puts the three side by side, which is why nobody sensible prepares for one exam.

The MSQ maths

On UCEED's multiple-select questions, partial credit pays for certainty and punishes curiosity. Chapter 12 works out the exact confidence threshold for adding one more option — and the expected value of guessing on the MCQs.

Which exams does this book cover?

All four design entrances for the 2027-28 cycle: UCEED for B.Des at seven IITs, CEED for M.Des and PhD, NIFTEE for B.Des and B.F.Tech at twenty NIFT campuses, and the NID Design Aptitude Test. Each is decoded separately, with its own dates, fees, pattern and marking scheme.

I am in Class 12. Is CEED relevant to me?

No. CEED is the postgraduate route and requires a three-year degree or diploma. If you are in Class 12, your exams are UCEED, NIFTEE and NID DAT, and Chapter 2 explains exactly which door belongs to which candidate.

Is the content up to date?

What if the registration deadlines have already passed?

Chapter 48 is written for exactly that. It covers NIFT's Artisan category and NLEA routes, the 47 result-sharing institutes that accepted UCEED scores in the 2026 cycle, and the dates to plan the next cycle against.
