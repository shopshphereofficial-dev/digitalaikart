Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Is this for students applying for the 2027 intake?

Yes. It is built around the rules in force in November 2026 for January, May and September 2027 intakes — including the CAD 23,448 living-cost requirement for applications submitted from 1 September 2026, the 2026 cap arithmetic, and the 2027-2029 Immigration Levels Plan published on 5 November 2026.

Where do the facts come from?

Every figure is traced to an official source and dated: IRCC pages, the Canada Gazette, the Immigration and Refugee Protection Act and Regulations, Statistics Canada tuition tables, the Federal Court and provincial designation rules — all checked on 10 November 2026. Where IRCC has not yet published something, such as the 2027 provincial allocations, the book says so instead of guessing.

Is this legal or immigration advice?

No. It is research and process guidance. Immigration officers decide individual applications and no book can change that. What it does is put every requirement, number and deadline in one place, in the order you need them, with the official page named so you can verify each one yourself.

Does it cover the postgraduate diploma route?

In detail. Chapter 3 compares master's degrees, postgraduate diplomas and bachelor's degrees on the four things that change your outcome: whether you need a provincial attestation letter, whether your spouse can get an open work permit, how long your post-graduation work permit lasts, and whether your program must be on the eligible field-of-study list.
