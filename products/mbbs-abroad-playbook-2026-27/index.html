The Two Gates Nobody Compares

Entry is sold hard. The exit decides everything, and it is decided by rules published years before you need them

19.99 lakh vs 1.40 lakh

NEET-UG 2026 candidates against the MBBS seats in the NMC's 2026-27 matrix: 64,121 government and 76,543 private. The arithmetic that pushes families abroad is real, and the book starts there.

12.78% passed

The overall FMGE pass rate for June 2026, from the Health Ministry's reply to Lok Sabha question 3375. Among first-time candidates it was above 35%, and 88% of the hall were repeaters. That gap is the whole argument for planning early.

Regulation 4(b)

The clause requiring a licence in the country of study at par with its own citizens. In 2026 the NMC answered three times on whether junior-doctor status in Georgia satisfies it, and left the question open. Chapter 3 walks the timeline.

54 + 12, one institution

Fifty-four months of course and twelve months of internship, in the same institution, in English, finished within ten years. A transfer can quietly void the whole degree - the Delhi High Court upheld exactly that in September 2026.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything worth knowing before you buy

Does this book tell me which college to join?

No, and deliberately not. It names no university, agent or consultant as safe. The National Medical Commission has stated that it does not endorse any list of foreign medical institutions, so any claim of an "NMC approved university" is describing something that does not exist. What the book gives you instead is the rule text, twelve verification steps, the exact emails to send, and the standard of evidence to accept.

How current is the research?

Research closed in the third week of November 2026. Every rule is quoted from a published source and dated: the FMGL Regulations 2021, the NMC advisories of 19 May 2025 and 5 November 2026, the NMC replies of 24 September and 13 October 2026 on the Georgia licensing question, the FMGE October 2026 information bulletin, and parliamentary answers of 7 August 2026 and 4 December 2025.

My child is already enrolled abroad. Is the book still useful?

Yes. Chapter 9 is written specifically for enrolled students: how to establish which rule regime your admission date puts you under, how to audit your own college against the seven criteria, what can and cannot be fixed, and the four complaint routes that produce a written record, including the PM Grievance Portal route that produced three dated NMC replies in 2026.

Will this help me pass FMGE?

Chapters 11 and 12 give you the exam as published - 300 questions, 150 to pass, no negative marking, no grace marks, three sessions a year - the 2026 change to video-based questions, and an eighteen-month and a six-month plan built around retrieval practice rather than re-reading. It is a plan, not a question bank.

Is this legal or medical advice?

No. It is research. Every rule is quoted from a published Indian government source with its date, and where a rule is under examination by the regulator the book says so rather than guessing the outcome. Regulations are amended and reinterpreted, so re-verify anything that decides money before you act on it.
