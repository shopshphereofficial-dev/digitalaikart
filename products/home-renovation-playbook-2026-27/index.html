Why This Book Exists

Three things happen in almost every Indian renovation

The scope drifts

Band 2 becomes band 4 by pointing at things during site visits. Nobody recalculates the total, and the bill arrives at double the conversation you thought you were having.

The quote hides its gaps

The cheapest quote is usually missing a heading — plumbing, electrical, waterproofing or debris removal. Per-square-foot rates cannot show you which.

Nothing is written down

No BOQ, no variation note, no retention. When the work does not match the render, there is no document to point at. In October 2026 a consumer commission in Hyderabad awarded a flat owner about ₹4.25 lakh in exactly that situation — because she had kept the renders, the quotation and every payment proof.

Frequently Asked Questions

What buyers ask before downloading

Is this useful if I have already signed with a contractor?

Yes. Parts 4, 6 and 7 are written for a contract that is already running — the variation protocol, the payment and retention structure, the snag list and the escalation ladder all work from where you are today.

Does it tell me what a renovation costs per square foot?

It tells you why that number is unreliable — published Indian estimates for 2026 span roughly ₹500 to ₹4,500 per square foot for what people call "renovation" — and then teaches you to build your own estimate from three itemised quotes for an identical scope. Chapter 4 walks through the method line by line.

Is it specific to India?

Entirely. GST on works contracts after the September 2025 rate changes, sections 24(b), 80C, 269ST and 194M of the income-tax law, the Consumer Protection Act 2019 with its 2021 jurisdiction rules, the construction and demolition waste rules in force from 1 April 2026, and Bureau of Indian Standards specifications for the materials you will be asked to approve.

Is it legal or tax advice?

No. It tells you which rules exist, what they say, and which questions to put to a chartered accountant or a lawyer before you sign something large. It makes you a better-informed client rather than replacing one.
