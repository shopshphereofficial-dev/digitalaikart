Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Is this medical advice?

No. It is an educational playbook. It does not diagnose anything and it does not replace a registered dentist who has examined you. What it does is show you what procedures cost in India, what the government schemes already pay for, and the questions that separate a treatment plan from a sales plan.

Where do the prices come from?

Two places. Published 2026 price lists from private clinics in Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Lucknow, Indore and Patna give the metro and tier-2 ranges. The CGHS procedure rate list revised on 13 October 2025 gives the government benchmark for the same procedures, and every CGHS figure in the book is taken from that document, with its revision date printed next to it.

Will this help if I already have a quote from a dentist?

That is exactly who it is written for. Chapters 2, 11, 12 and 30 take a written quote apart: what should be inside the number, the twelve red flags of an over-aggressive plan, the fifteen questions to ask, and a printable sheet for comparing two clinics line by line.

Does it cover Ayushman Bharat and CGHS?

Yes, in detail. Chapter 3 sets out who CGHS and ESIC cover and at what rates, what Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY actually pays for (oral and maxillofacial surgery, not fillings or root canals, quoting the official exclusion), and how to use a government dental college for large treatment plans.

Is it useful if nothing is wrong with my teeth right now?

That is the best time to read it. Part three covers the daily routine that has evidence behind it, sugar frequency, gum disease, children from zero to fourteen, pregnancy, diabetes and care after sixty. Prevention is where the money is saved permanently.
