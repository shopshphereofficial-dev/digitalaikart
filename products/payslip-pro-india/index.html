The Problem It Solves

Salary slips are five minutes of work and three hours of fiddling.

Every month the same thing happens in a small business. The salary sheet is in one Excel file, the slip format is a Word document from four years ago, and the numbers have to be copied across by hand for each employee. Someone's paid days change because they took three days off. The PF figure has to be capped again. The professional tax slab is remembered from a conversation with the CA. Then the slips have to be saved as separate PDFs and sent out — and by the time the last one goes, the month's register still has not been built.

PaySlip Pro puts that loop into one screen. Your employee master is entered once. Each month you change only what actually changed — paid days, an incentive, an advance — and the app produces the slip, the register and the numbers behind them. It runs from a single file on your laptop, so there is no monthly bill, no per-employee pricing and no salary data leaving your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Is this an ebook or a working app?

A working app. The ZIP contains one HTML file that opens in your browser as a complete payroll desk — employee master, monthly pay run, salary slips and the salary register. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

Does it work without the internet?

Yes. Once the folder is on your device the app works with the internet switched off. The file loads nothing from the web, and your employees' salary data never leaves your computer.

Does it calculate income tax (TDS)?

No. PaySlip Pro does not estimate income tax. You enter the monthly TDS figure your CA advises, and the app deducts it, prints it on the slip and carries it into the register. That is deliberate: tax estimation depends on each employee's declarations and regime choice, and getting it wrong on a slip is worse than not guessing.

Does it file PF, ESI, professional tax or Form 16?

No. It prepares salary slips and the salary register. It does not file PF ECR, ESI returns, PT challans, 24Q or Form 16, and it does not connect to any government portal. Most buyers run payroll here and hand the month's register and CSV to their CA for the filings.

How are PF, ESI and professional tax worked out?

PF is 12% of basic, capped at the PF wage ceiling (₹15,000 a month by default, and you can switch the cap off). ESI is 0.75% employee and 3.25% employer when the month's wages are within ₹21,000, rounded up to the rupee. Professional tax uses slab presets for Maharashtra, Karnataka, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, West Bengal, Gujarat and Madhya Pradesh, with a nil option for Delhi and a manual option everywhere else. Every one of those figures can be overridden for an individual employee in a given month.

What happens when someone takes unpaid leave?

Enter the paid days for that employee and every earning component is pro-rated by paid days over total days. The loss-of-pay days and the reduced figures show on the row, on the slip and in the register. Half days are allowed.

Can I print the slips or send them as PDF?

Both. Each slip is laid out for A4 with your business header, earnings and deductions side by side, and the net pay in figures and words. Print all slips at once — one per page — or choose Save as PDF in the print dialog to email them.

How many employees can I add?

There is no limit in the app. Small businesses typically run anywhere from two to a hundred. The pay run is a table that recalculates as you type, and the register scrolls sideways on a phone.

Where is my data stored, and can I lose it?

In your browser's local storage on your own device. Clearing browsing data, switching browsers or resetting the device will empty the app, so it includes a one-click JSON backup and a matching restore. Download a backup at the end of every month and keep it in Google Drive — that is the whole safety net, and it takes five seconds.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Copy the file to your phone, open it with Chrome, and use the browser menu to add it to your home screen. The layout adapts to a small screen, though entering salary structures is faster on a laptop.

What if I run two businesses?

One purchase covers one business. For two separate sets of books, buy a second copy, or keep one file per business in its own folder with its own backup — never open the same file from two folders and expect one set of books.

No. One payment of ₹249, no monthly or yearly fee, no per-employee charge, no free-plan limits and no expiry.
