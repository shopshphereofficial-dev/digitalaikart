Is this book for complete beginners?

Yes. It starts from the very first step — getting comfortable speaking out loud — and builds up to interviews and meetings. If you can read English, you can use this book.

How long until I notice a difference?

Most people feel more confident within four to six weeks of consistent daily practice, and hear a clear change in their recordings by week six to eight. The habit research points to about 66 days for a new behaviour to feel automatic.

Do I need a teacher or a paid course?

No. The 30-day routine, a voice AI partner and your phone recorder cover the essentials for free. Coaching later helps most with pronunciation feedback, but you can start today without it.

Will this remove my accent?

No, and it is not meant to. The aim is intelligibility — being understood clearly the first time. A handful of sound and stress fixes achieve that, and your accent can stay.
