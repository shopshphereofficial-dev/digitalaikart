The Problem With the Paper Khata

Three things the book in the drawer cannot tell you

It does not tell you how old a due is. You can read that Harpreet owes ₹6,400. You cannot read that ₹4,800 of it has been outstanding since July, because the page is a column of dates and amounts and nobody adds up the ageing of a page while a customer is waiting. So the oldest money stays the quietest, and the fresh credit keeps going out on top of it.

It does not add up after a part payment. A customer pays ₹1,600 against ₹4,800. The book shows a new balance, but which ₹1,600 was that — the July stock or the September stock? A shopkeeper knows the answer in his head and forgets it by the next visit. KhataLedger Pro settles payments against the oldest unpaid amount first, every time, and the ageing report then shows what is genuinely still old.

It does not survive the reminder. Asking for money is awkward, so it gets postponed, and the amount gets larger, which makes it more awkward. The app's answer is not a stronger message but a faster one: the reminder is already written, with the balance, the days pending and your UPI ID, and it opens in WhatsApp with one tap. You still press send. It just takes four seconds instead of four days of thinking about it.

How the Ageing Is Calculated

The same rule a careful shopkeeper applies in his head

Each customer's unpaid amounts are held in the order they were given. When a payment comes in, it is applied to the oldest unpaid amount first, then to the next, until the money runs out. Whatever remains is aged from its due date — or from its own date, if you left the due date blank — and placed in one of five buckets.

BucketWhat it meansWhat a shop usually does
Not dueThe due date has not arrivedNothing. Do not chase a customer who is inside the agreed date.
1–30 daysJust overdueOne polite WhatsApp reminder, with the statement attached as a PDF.
31–60 daysGetting oldReminder plus a printed statement handed over. Ask for a date.
61–90 daysSeriousStop new credit on that khata. Agree an amount and a date in writing.
90+ daysRecovery at riskDecide: a written promise, a part payment, or treat it as a loss. Waiting rarely improves it.

The app does not judge and does not chase anyone by itself. It only makes the age of each rupee visible, which is the part the paper book hides.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this an ebook or a working tool?

A working tool. The ZIP holds an HTML application, its JavaScript and stylesheet, a printable Start Here guide, a readme, a licence and a sample book. You open index.html and the khata runs in your browser.

Do I need to install anything or create an account?

No. Unzip the folder, double-click index.html, and the app opens in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. There is no sign-up, no login, no activation key, and no internet connection needed after the download.

Is it a subscription?

No. One payment of ₹299. No renewal, no per-customer fee, no per-message charge, and nothing stops working after a year.

Where is my data stored, and can it be lost?

In the local storage of the browser you open it with, on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded. It can be lost if you clear browsing data, use a private window, reset the browser profile or change computers — so the app has one-click JSON backup and restore. Take a backup weekly and keep the file in your email or Drive. The Start Here guide makes this the first habit it teaches.

Does it send WhatsApp messages automatically?

No. It writes the message — balance, days pending, your UPI ID and a payment link for the exact amount — and opens WhatsApp on your phone with it filled in. You press send from your own number. No bulk messaging, no Business API, no risk of your number being flagged.

How does the ageing calculation work?

Payments are applied to the oldest unpaid amount first, the way a paper khata works. Each remaining amount is aged from its due date, or from its own date when no due date was set, and lands in one of five buckets: not due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90 and more than 90 days.

Can I print a statement for a customer?

Yes. Open the customer's khata and press Print statement. It prints on A4 with your shop name, address, phone and GSTIN if you saved one, every entry with a running balance, the closing balance in figures and in words, and signature lines. The same print dialog saves it as a PDF you can send on WhatsApp.

Will it work on my phone?

The layout adapts to a phone and the reminders open WhatsApp on the phone, but the files live on the computer you unzipped them on, and each browser keeps its own copy of the book. Keep the computer as the master; if you want to look at the book on a second device, restore a backup file there.

Does it create GST invoices?

No. It is a credit ledger, not billing software: no tax invoices, no GSTR reports, no e-way bills. If you need GST invoices, GST Invoice Generator Pro is the separate Digitalaikart product for that, and the two sit comfortably side by side.

Can two people use it at the same counter?

Not at the same time. There is no sync and no cloud, by design. If two counters must write to one book live, use a cloud billing app. If one person keeps the book and another only reads it, export the CSV or restore a backup on the second machine.
