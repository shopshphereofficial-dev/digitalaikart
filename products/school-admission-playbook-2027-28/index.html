The Indian School Admission Playbook 2027-28

Four admission routes, one calendar, and the rules that decide each of them

This book maps all four, with the dates, the documents, the points systems and the lottery orders, verified from the DoE, KVS, NVS and NTA documents themselves.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Which admission systems does this cover?

Four: private unaided schools through their published points system, the RTE 25% free-seat quota, Kendriya Vidyalaya, and Navodaya or Sainik School through the JNVST and AISSEE entrance tests. The Delhi private-school and RTE chapters are the most detailed because Delhi is the one large system with a fully published, centrally run process; the Kendriya Vidyalaya, Navodaya and Sainik chapters are national.

My child is not in Delhi. Is this still useful?

Yes. Three of the four routes are national — Kendriya Vidyalaya, Navodaya and Sainik School — and the RTE chapter explains the statutory right that applies in every state, plus how to find your state's portal and check the three things that differ. Only the points-system chapter is Delhi-specific, and it is there because no other state publishes its criteria.

Is the 2027-28 admission calendar already announced?

Partly. Two things are fixed: the JNVST 2027 exam on 28 November 2026, and the AISSEE 2027 exam on 31 January 2027. Delhi's 2027-28 circular and the AISSEE 2027 bulletin had not been issued when this was written on 28 October 2026, and the book says so plainly instead of inventing dates. Where a cycle is awaited, you get the previous cycle's day-by-day dates as the pattern to plan against.

How is this different from free articles online?

Free coverage of these admissions is full of fabricated notification dates, recycled last-year schedules and fee tables that were never announced. Every date, fee and eligibility rule here comes from a government document or portal read on or before 28 October 2026, and every fact that could not be verified was left out. The source list is printed at the end of the book.

Does it include practice questions?

Yes. Appendix A is a 40-question practice set written to the JNVST 2027 syllabus and Class V level — 20 arithmetic questions and 20 mental ability and EVS questions — with a full answer key and a one-line explanation for every answer.
