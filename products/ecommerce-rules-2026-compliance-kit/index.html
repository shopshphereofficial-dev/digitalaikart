The Indian Online Seller's Consumer Protection Compliance Kit

The rules change on 1 January 2027. This is the 56-day plan that gets you ready — without a lawyer on retainer.

On 9 September 2026 the government notified the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) (Amendment) Rules, 2026 — G.S.R. 789(E). From 1 January 2027 every Indian online seller carries new duties: a grievance officer who acknowledges in 48 hours, a National Consumer Helpline registration, a real 30-day prior price on every discount banner, a yearly dark-pattern self-audit with a certificate displayed on your site, and a GSTIN or MSME number on every listing. This 41-page kit turns all of it into a checklist, with the exact text to paste, the audit to run and an eight-week calendar that ends on 31 December.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Does this apply to me if I only sell on Amazon or Flipkart?

Yes, as a seller. Rule 6(5)(d) requires you to publish country of origin, best-before, return, refund, exchange, warranty, delivery, cost of return shipping and payment modes, and Rule 6(5)(j) requires your GSTIN or MSME registration number on the listing. The Rule 4 duties sit on the marketplace, but it will ask you for the new data because its own compliance depends on your accuracy.

When do the new rules come into force?

The Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) (Amendment) Rules, 2026 were notified on 9 September 2026 as G.S.R. 789(E) and come into force on 1 January 2027. No transition window has been announced.

Is this kit legal advice?

No. It is a practical compliance kit written from the notified rule text, the regulator's own orders and the Consumer Protection Act, 2019. It is not legal advice and does not create a lawyer-client relationship. If your turnover is large or you are raising money, have your counsel read the rule-by-rule section alongside it.
