Is it safe to use AI in a medical practice in India?

Yes, within clear limits — and the pack explains them. The Compliance Primer covers the NMC Telemedicine Practice Guidelines 2020 (AI platforms may not counsel or prescribe), the NMC Professional Conduct regulations on advertising and confidentiality, and the DPDP Act 2023 on patient data. Every prompt is written to stay inside those limits.
