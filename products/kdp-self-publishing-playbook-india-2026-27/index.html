Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Do I need a company or GST to start publishing on Amazon KDP?

No. You can publish as an individual using your PAN and your own Indian bank account. A company and GST registration become relevant later, once your India sales approach the ₹20 lakh GST threshold or your income is large enough to warrant structuring.

Can I publish on KDP from India without a US bank account?

Yes. Amazon pays Indian authors by direct bank transfer to an Indian account. You complete a tax interview and give your PAN as your tax identification number; you do not need a US account or PayPal.

Why does my KDP withholding show 30%?

Because you have not claimed the India-US treaty benefit, or your W-8BEN has expired. Complete the tax interview, select India and treaty benefits, and enter your PAN, and the rate drops to 15% on US-source royalties.

Is the content up to date and India-specific?

Yes. Every royalty rate, fee, tax rule and policy is checked against official sources — Amazon's KDP Help Centre, the Income-tax Act 2025, the Raja Rammohun Roy National Agency for ISBN and the GST law — and dated where it can move, current as of October 2026.
