How It Works

From unzip to a copied prompt in about two minutes

  1. Unzip and open. Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser with all 120 prompts already loaded. Nothing to install, nothing to sign into.
  2. Find the job. Search for the word in front of you, or tap a category. The count at the top tells you how many prompts match.
  3. Fill the boxes. Open a prompt and type your product, city, price and audience into the inputs. The prompt rewrites itself below as you type, and the word count updates.
  4. Copy and paste. Tap Copy prompt, paste into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or Copilot. Pick Hindi or Hinglish first if you want the answer in that language.
  5. Improve the first answer. Reply with one line: “Cut 25% of the words and remove anything you cannot support.” Then save the prompt to your stack for next time.
