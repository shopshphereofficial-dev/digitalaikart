The Problem It Solves

A rejected PF claim is not a free retry. It is three weeks of your money sitting still.

📵

You resigned and the money is stuck

The claim says "date of exit not marked", your old HR has stopped replying, and every video you find was made for rules that stopped existing in July 2026. Chapter 5 explains the wait, Chapter 7 fixes the defect, Chapter 11 makes someone answer.

🔁

Your claim keeps getting returned

Name mismatch, bank not validated, PAN unverified. Nine defects account for almost every rejection, and eight of them can be found and fixed before you press submit. There is a checklist for exactly that.

🧾

You are about to resign

The cheapest claim you will ever file is the one you prepared for while you still had a job. The 20-point exit file and the twelve-month calendar are the two pages worth printing.

👨‍👩‍👧

You are filing for someone who has died

Three separate claims, three separate funds, and families routinely receive one and never claim the other two. Chapter 10 covers the ₹2.5 to ₹7 lakh insurance cover nobody tells them about.

Is this book about the old PF rules or the new ones?

The new ones. Every rule is taken from the Employees' Provident Funds Scheme 2026, the Pension Scheme 2026 and the EDLI Scheme 2026, notified on 29 June 2026 and in force from 1 July 2026, plus the wage ceiling notification of 17 September 2026 and EPFO's own releases up to 1 November 2026. Paragraph numbers are printed next to the rules so you can check them yourself.

Can this tell me exactly how much I can withdraw?

It tells you the two rules that decide it: the 25% minimum-balance floor, which caps any partial withdrawal at 75% of your corpus, and the purpose ceilings. Your actual rupee figure comes from the portal's eligible-amount screen, which shows the maximum before you submit. The book shows you where to read it and how to do the arithmetic yourself.

My PF claim was rejected. Will this help?

Yes. Chapter 7 lists the nine defects behind almost every rejection with the exact fix and the time each takes, and explains the difference between a returned claim and a rejected one. Chapter 11 is the escalation ladder for when your employer will not file the exit date or answer email.

It does. Chapter 9 works the EPS 2026 pension formula with five worked examples, early and deferred pension tables, the withdrawal-benefit wait and the Scheme Certificate decision. Chapter 10 covers EDLI, the ₹2.5 lakh to ₹7 lakh cover most families never claim.

Yes. A printable pre-claim checklist, a document matrix showing which claim needs which paper, a 20-point exit file for before you resign, and three letter templates: to your employer, to the Regional PF Commissioner, and an RTI application.
