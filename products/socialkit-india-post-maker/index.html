Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Is this an ebook or a tool?

It is a tool plus a design pack — no ebook. You get a small web app (plain HTML and JavaScript, no install, no account) that renders 48 ready-made social graphics with your business details, and exports them as PNG or SVG. The folder also includes all 48 designs pre-exported as SVG files, so you can use them even if you never open the app.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything runs inside the browser on your own device. There are no external fonts, no image links, no analytics and no server. Once the ZIP is on your laptop you can switch off the internet, open index.html and export every design.

Do I need Canva or Photoshop?

No. The app is the editor. If you want to go further, the SVG exports open in Inkscape (free), Illustrator, Figma or Canva, where the text stays editable and you can drop in a product photo.

What sizes do I get?

Three: 1080 × 1080 square (feed posts), 1080 × 1350 portrait (feed posts that take more height) and 1080 × 1920 story (Instagram Story, WhatsApp Status). Each layout is designed at one of these sizes and exported at exactly that pixel count.

Will my Hindi or regional language text work?

Yes. Devanagari, Tamil, Bengali, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Gujarati, Punjabi and Urdu text all render with your device's own fonts and export correctly in both PNG and SVG.

Are the designs watermarked?

No. There is no watermark, no branding strip and no per-post limit. The files are yours to use on as many posts as you like, for as many businesses as you own or manage.

The sample business name 'Sharma Studio' — is that what I get?

That is placeholder content, used to show what a finished post looks like. You type your own business name, phone, address, hours and price list into the app once, and every template updates. The instructions cover exactly which fields to replace.

Can I use it for my client's business?

Yes. The licence covers any business you own or are paid to manage, with unlimited posts. What you may not do is resell or redistribute the template files themselves as a template pack.
