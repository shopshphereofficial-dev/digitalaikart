Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

I am a fresher on my first support job. Is this kit for me?

Yes, and it is where most buyers start. Part 1 explains what changed in Indian support hiring this year, Part 3 teaches the six-line prompt structure, and Module 1 covers the twelve cases that fill a new agent's queue. You can use the first prompt within ten minutes of downloading.

My company does not allow AI tools on the floor. Is this still useful?

Yes. Every prompt works on a personal phone during a break, and the patterns — the six-block skeleton, the pre-send check, the macro library, the escalation note — are useful even if you never paste a prompt into a tool. Many readers use the kit to build macros and knowledge-base articles in their own system.

Does it cover Hindi and regional languages?

Module 3 has eight prompts: Hinglish in Roman script, formal Hindi in Devanagari, and a language-agnostic prompt for Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Kannada and Gujarati. Each one keeps product names, amounts, dates and policy terms in English so nothing gets mistranslated.

Is the data current?

Every external statistic is dated and sourced in Part 13, checked on 12 November 2026 — including the FY27 hiring data, the Nasscom–Krisp voice AI readiness report and the DPDP Rules timeline. Tool plan details were verified in the first week of November 2026, and the kit tells you to re-check them because free tiers change.

Will using AI get me into trouble with quality audits?

Unedited AI output will, which is why Part 11 lists the fifteen habits that get agents pulled up — invented policy, template phrasing, over-promising, pasting customer data. The prompts include a mandatory CHECK block that makes the model list missing facts instead of guessing, and P41 audits your draft before you send it.
