The Problem It Solves

The roster lives in a notebook, a WhatsApp group and one person's memory — until someone does not turn up

📓

The same three people always get Sunday off

Whoever writes the roster by hand falls into a pattern. The favourites get the good days, the newest hire gets every closing shift, and the resentment arrives long before the resignation does.

📵

Leave is approved in one place, forgotten in another

The leave is in a diary, the roster is in Excel, and the holiday list is in someone's head. The clash is discovered on the morning it happens, at the counter, with a queue.

🧮

Nobody knows the hours until the salary is due

Twenty-six days, six hours a day, minus one break — worked out on a calculator at month end, differently each time. The argument is never about the roster, it is about the money.

💸

Software wants a monthly fee per employee

Rostering tools in India are priced per user per month — commonly ₹99 to ₹499 a head. For eight staff that is ₹800 to ₹4,000 every month, for a grid you fill once a week.

What DutyRota Does

One file, one month, one page — and the rules are applied in a fixed, explainable order

⚡ Generate a month in one click

Thirty-one days, every person, every shift. The generator applies your rules in order: pinned cells, then leave and holidays, then weekly offs, then the consecutive-day limit, then the weekly cap, then fair distribution.

🗓️ Weekly off is never forgotten

Give each person a fixed weekly-off weekday and that day stays free every week of the month, including the weeks that start mid-month.

🏖️ Leave and holidays come first

Enter a leave range for one person, or a holiday date for the whole business. The generator works around both instead of overwriting them.

⚖️ Fair rotation, not favouritism

Shift types are handed out by how often each person has already had them, so morning, evening and full-day shifts spread across the team instead of settling on the same names.

🛡️ Limits you set are enforced

Maximum days in a row and a weekly shift cap per person. The generator will give someone an extra day off rather than break either limit.

🔍 Coverage check under every day

Each shift shows how many people you rostered against how many it needs — 2/2 in green, 1/2 in red — for all 31 days at once, plus a per-shift total for the month.

🖨️ Prints on one A4 page

Business name, month, legend, the full grid, the coverage line and a sign-off block for the manager and the owner. Landscape, 100% scale, no shrinking.

💬 A WhatsApp message in one press

Each day on one line: shift code, who is on it, who is off. Copy it into the staff group instead of typing it out at midnight.

📊 Hours and a CSV

Days worked, hours scheduled, off days and leave days per person, and a CSV with one row per person per day for your attendance sheet or payroll.

🔒 Offline, and yours

No account, no upload, no analytics. Backup downloads the whole plan as one JSON file; Restore loads it on another computer.

What each cell means

M, E, F are your shift codes — the ones you type in the Shifts panel, so they can be Morning, Evening, Night, General or anything else, one or two letters.

— is a day off: a weekly off, an extra off day because there were more people than slots, or a rest day after the consecutive-day limit.

L is approved leave and H is a paid holiday. On a closed holiday the whole row reads H and nobody is rostered.

Under the grid, 2/2 means two people rostered against two needed. A red 1/2 is a shift that is short — visible before it happens, not after.

How It Works

From unzip to a roster on the wall in about ten minutes

  1. Unzip and open. Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser and loads a worked example so you can see a full month before you change anything.
  2. Describe your team. Name, role, weekly-off weekday, maximum shifts per week, and optionally the shift they prefer. Eight staff takes about three minutes.
  3. Describe your shifts. Name, one or two letter code, start and end time, break minutes, and how many people the shift needs on a normal day and at the weekend.
  4. Add leave and holidays. A date range per person, and the dates the business is closed. Both are respected the next time you generate.
  5. Generate, adjust, print. Press Generate roster. Click any cell you want to change — the change is pinned. Print, or copy the WhatsApp text.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this an ebook?

No. You get a working application — one HTML file you open in your browser — plus a manual, a worked example, a sample printout and a licence. There is nothing to read before you can use it.

Do I need internet, an account or an install?

No. The app is one file on your computer. No sign-up, no login, no server, no analytics, and it works with the internet switched off. Your staff names and shifts never leave your device.

How many staff and shifts can it handle?

There is no built-in limit. The grid is comfortable to about 25 staff and 6 shifts, which prints on one A4 landscape page. Past that the page still prints but the type gets small. Most small businesses sit far inside that range.

Does it record attendance or calculate salary?

No. DutyRota plans the roster. It does not record who turned up, mark late or absent, or calculate wages, overtime, PF or ESI. It shows scheduled hours per person and exports a CSV so your attendance sheet or payroll tool can use the plan.

Will the roster be legally compliant?

The app applies the rules you set — a weekly off, a maximum number of consecutive days and a weekly shift cap. Whether those numbers satisfy your state's Shops and Establishments Act, the Factories Act or a contract is for you to confirm. It is a planning aid, not legal advice, and it does not know your state's limits.

Can I use it for more than one shop or branch?

Yes. The licence covers one business including all its own outlets and departments. Keep one backup file per branch, or put the branch in the role field so you can see who belongs where.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The layout stacks and the roster scrolls sideways in mobile Chrome, Safari and Firefox, so you can change a cell while standing in the shop. Printing from a phone needs a printer app; most people print from a computer.

What happens when a shift cannot be covered?

The Coverage check shows the shortfall per shift and the Checks panel names the days, instead of quietly leaving a gap. You then lower the headcount for that shift, add a person, or move a weekly off. The app does not invent staff.

Can I change the roster after it is generated?

Yes. Click any cell to cycle it through shift, Off, Leave, Holiday and blank; right-click steps back. An edited cell is pinned and is never overwritten when you press Generate again.

After a successful payment you land on a thank-you page with a download button for the ZIP. The ZIP holds the app, the manual, the quick-start note, the worked example, the sample printout and the licence.
