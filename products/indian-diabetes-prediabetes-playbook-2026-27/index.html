Six Facts That Change What You Do This Week

All verified from Indian sources, current to November 2026

101 million and 136 million

The ICMR-INDIAB national study puts diabetes at 11.4% of Indian adults and prediabetes at 15.3% — 101 million and 136 million people.

Only 7.7% hit all three targets

36.3% control their HbA1c, 48.8% their blood pressure, 41.5% their LDL. Just 7.7% manage all three together.

The millet swap alone doesn't work

The 2025 Nature Medicine analysis of 18,090 Indians found swapping refined cereal for millet flour, at the same total carbohydrate, did not lower diabetes risk. Adding protein did.

Semaglutide fell to ₹750–1,300 a month

The patent expired in March 2026 and at least seven Indian manufacturers launched generics within a day. The full price table, and what to ask before switching, is in Part Five.

Insurance rules changed in your favour

IRDAI cut the pre-existing disease waiting period to a maximum of 36 months and the moratorium to 60. Portability now carries the credit with you.

Free care you may not be using

Screening for everyone over 30, free diabetes medicines, and teleconsultation at 1.87 lakh Ayushman Arogya Mandirs — plus ₹5 lakh cover under PM-JAY.
