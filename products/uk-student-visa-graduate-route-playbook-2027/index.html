UK Student Visa & Graduate Route Playbook 2027

The Rules Changed Twice This Cycle. Here Is What They Cost You.

A 50-page working playbook on the UK Student visa as it stands in November 2026. It starts with the two changes most applicants have not caught up with: from 30 November 2026 the money you must show rises to GBP 1,570 a month in London and GBP 1,203 outside London, and from 1 January 2027 the Graduate Route drops from two years to 18 months — with a two-year exception for graduates of "high compliance" universities that the Autumn Budget announced but has not yet defined. Then the arithmetic, worked four ways with the 28-day and 31-day clocks mapped onto a calendar, in rupees at Rs 123.5 to the pound. Then the application: the document set, ATAS, the TB certificate, the B2 English rule, the IHS calculation, the credibility interview questions, and the ten refusal reasons with the fix for each. Then what happens after you land — the eVisa, the first fourteen days, the 20-hour work condition and its self-employment trap, the Graduate Route timing that buys six months, the GBP 33,400 new entrant route to Skilled Worker, and four letters that ask a university for money.

The Numbers You Get

Checked against GOV.UK on 18 November 2026

GBP 1,570 / 1,203

Monthly maintenance from 30 November 2026 in London and outside London — up from GBP 1,529 and GBP 1,171.

18 months

The Graduate Route for applications made on or after 1 January 2027. Two years if you apply before then; three years for PhD graduates.

GBP 558 + 776

The Student visa fee, and the student Immigration Health Surcharge for each year of the visa.

28 days

How long the money must sit still, ending no more than 31 days before you apply — counted backwards from the closing balance.

GBP 33,400

The new entrant salary floor for Skilled Worker, or 70 per cent of the going rate, for a maximum of four years.

GBP 937

The Graduate visa fee, plus GBP 1,035 for each year of that visa — roughly GBP 1,552 for an 18-month grant.
