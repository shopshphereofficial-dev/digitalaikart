Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Can I work abroad with a GNM diploma?

Yes, in several markets. The UAE (DHA and DOH), Qatar, Bahrain and Oman accept a GNM as a Registered Nurse, usually with about two years' post-registration experience. Saudi Arabia's SCFHS generally classifies a GNM as Nurse Technician. The UK, US, Australia and Canada assess the qualification itself rather than the title. The playbook explains the pay-band consequences of each.

Is the content up to date for 2026-27?

Yes. Every fee, rule, date and timeline is verified from official sources — NCSBN, the NMC, GOV.UK, NMBI, Ahpra and NMBA, ANMAC, NNAS, the Nursing Council of New Zealand and TruMerit, the German Federal Employment Agency and GIZ, SCFHS and Prometric, the US Department of State Visa Bulletin, IDP, OET, the Ministry of External Affairs, eMigrate, the Indian Nursing Council and AIIMS — current as of October 2026.

Does it cover the current decline in overseas nurse hiring?

Yes, plainly. The playbook states that Indian nurse immigration clearances fell from 16,594 in 2023 to 11,581 in 2025, that UK international intake fell to about a third of its 2023-24 peak, and that Saudi Arabia and the UAE are localising nursing jobs. It then shows the routes that are still genuinely open — Germany, Australia, Ireland, Canada, New Zealand and the Gulf — and how to plan around the constraint.

Yes.
