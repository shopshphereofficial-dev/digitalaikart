What Makes This Different

Six things no free article will tell you

📅 The 5th-day rule

PPF interest is calculated on the lowest balance between the 5th and the month end. Depositing on the 6th instead of the 5th costs about ₹2.4 lakh over fifteen years.

🧾 The tax truth

80C is now Section 123 and lives only in the old regime. The Section 156 rebate makes tax nil up to ₹12 lakh. So what is the deduction actually worth to you?

📊 After-tax, not headline

All nine schemes ranked at the nil, 5%, 20% and 30% slabs. At 30%, the best taxable scheme returns 5.74% while PPF returns 7.10%.

⚠️ Every penalty, in rupees

SCSS closed at 18 months: ₹45,000 on ₹30 lakh. SSY closed for the wrong reason: 4% instead of 8.20%. RD after four defaults: discontinued.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Are the interest rates in this playbook current?

Yes. Every rate is the one notified by the Department of Economic Affairs for 1 October to 31 December 2026 and circulated to post offices as SB Order No. 12/2026 dated 30 September 2026 — PPF 7.10%, Sukanya Samriddhi 8.20%, SCSS 8.20%, NSC 7.70%, KVP 7.50%, the 5-year time deposit 7.50%, RD 6.70% and the Monthly Income Account 7.40%. The book also shows you how to update it when the board moves in January.

I am in the new tax regime. Is this still useful to me?

Especially then. The 80C deduction is now Section 123 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 and exists only in the old regime, and the Section 156 rebate makes tax nil up to ₹12 lakh of total income in the new regime. The book explains why the deduction is worth zero for most salaried savers and shows what to choose instead, with a full after-tax comparison of all nine schemes at the nil, 5%, 20% and 30% slabs.

Does it cover the Mahila Samman Savings Certificate?

Yes, and it tells you the truth about it: the scheme stopped accepting new deposits after 31 March 2025 under SB Order No. 03/2025, and Budget 2026 did not revive it. The book covers what happens to a live certificate and what to use instead.

Is this a collection of blog posts?

No. It is a single 49-page manual with 20 numbered tables and 8 printable worksheets, built scheme by scheme from the actual notifications — the PPF Scheme 2019, the Sukanya Samriddhi Scheme 2019, the SCSS Scheme 2019, the NSC Scheme 2019, the RD and MIS Schemes 2019, the Time Deposit Scheme 2019, the Kisan Vikas Patra Rules 2014 and the Government Savings Promotion General Rules 2018.
