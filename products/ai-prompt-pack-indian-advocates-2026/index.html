Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Is this pack safe to use given the Supreme Court's ruling on AI citations?

That ruling is exactly why this pack exists. In July 2026 the Supreme Court held that citing AI-generated judgments without verification is professional misconduct, while making clear that the judgment has no bearing on the rightful use of AI. The whole pack is built around that line: AI drafts, you verify. Every prompt is written to keep the AI on language work and to force it to say VERIFY instead of inventing a citation.

How many prompts are in the pack?

105 copy-paste prompts across ten legal jobs — legal research, notices and replies, pleadings and petitions, contracts, court filings and submissions, client communication, evidence and document analysis, translation, compliance and opinions, and practice management. Each one is written to be used as it stands, with the square brackets filled in from your own matter.

Does it cover the new criminal laws?

Yes. Chapter 8 is a BNS, BNSS and BSA transition cheat-sheet with the mappings advocates use most often — IPC 302 to BNS 103(1), IPC 420 to BNS 318(4), IPC 498A to BNS 85, CrPC 438 to BNSS 482, Evidence Act 65B to BSA 63 and more — plus the date-of-conduct rule and the correct way to write the pleading line.
