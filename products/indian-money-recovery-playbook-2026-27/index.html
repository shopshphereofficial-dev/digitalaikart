Questions

The five things people ask before buying

Is this legal advice?

No, and the book says so on its second page. It is a procedure manual: it tells you what the statute says, what the deadline is, which forum hears it and what the letter should contain. For a contested claim, a criminal complaint or anything above your comfort level, engage an advocate — and arrive with the file this book helps you build.

Does the MSME route apply to my business?

It applies if you are a micro or small enterprise with a Udyam registration dated before the supply. Medium enterprises are outside the protection entirely. The book prints the classification limits in force since 1 April 2025 and the two registration checks to run before you spend a rupee on filing.

My cheque bounced last month. Is it too late?

Probably not, but the clock is short: 30 days from the bank's return memo to send the demand notice, 15 days for the drawer to pay, then one month to file. Part 5 walks the sequence with a worked calendar, and Worksheet 7 is a fill-in timeline.

How current are the numbers?

Everything was verified on 14 November 2026 — including the RBI bank rate of 5.75 per cent, which sets the 17.25 per cent statutory interest under Section 16, and the 15 October 2025 cut-off after which the MSME ODR portal became the only route for new delayed-payment references.
