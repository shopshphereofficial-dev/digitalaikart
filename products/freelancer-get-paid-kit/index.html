Everything you need to know before buying

I'm a freelancer without a GST registration. Is this kit still for me?

Yes. The kit covers three invoice paths — domestic GST, export under LUT, and simple non-GST invoicing below the ₹20 lakh services threshold — so you use the one that matches your situation today and know exactly when registration becomes compulsory.

Are the legal rules current?

Yes. Every fact — the MSME 15/45-day payment rules and their monthly-compounded interest, the TDS ₹50,000 threshold effective FY 2025-26, presumptive-taxation limits, advance-tax dates and the FY 2026-27 new-regime slabs — was verified against official sources in September 2026, and the kit ends with its full source list.

Does the contract template work for foreign clients too?

The fill-in contract is written for Indian freelancers and covers both domestic and overseas clients, including export payment terms. Chapter 8 separately explains the export paperwork — the LUT, e-FIRA and purpose code P0802 — that applies when you invoice abroad.

Can I really recover money with MSME Samadhaan?

The kit explains exactly how it works: register on Udyam before the contract (registration must exist when work starts), then the 45-day payment rule and monthly-compounded interest give you real leverage at samadhaan.msme.gov.in. It is a genuine, free, government-backed route — not a magic button, so the kit also covers demand letters, cheque-bounce notices and civil suits.
