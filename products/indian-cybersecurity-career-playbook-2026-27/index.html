The same DSCI-SANS survey that shows a 73% talent shortage also shows automation reducing entry-level roles. Of 1,30,000 active tech openings in November 2026, 16,000 were entry level. Chapter 1 opens with that, not with a success story.

💰 Real prices, from the awarding bodies

Security+ at $439, ISC2 CC at $199, CEH at $1,199 or $950 depending on the voucher route, OSCP at $1,699 or $1,749 for the bundle, CISSP at $749, ISACA at $575 or $760. Read from the vendors' own stores in November 2026, with the renewal and retake costs nobody quotes.

📅 A deadline, not a trend

📜 The regulations, quoted properly

CERT-In's directions: six-hour reporting, 180-day logs kept in India, twenty reportable incident types, a named point of contact. SEBI's CSCRF: category thresholds, annual cyber audit, Market SOC. Chapter 22 covers the IT Act sections that end careers.

🎯 Four routes, ranked by who actually hires

Services SOC, empanelled audit firm, compliance and privacy, adjacent-role pivot — each with what gets you in, what it costs you, where it leads and who it suits. Not one generic roadmap.

✅ A plan with an output at every step

Six phases over 180 days, and each phase ends in something a stranger can inspect: twenty lab rows, two published reports, a cloud write-up, a booked exam, forty tracker rows, three interview debriefs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

I have no IT background at all. Is this for me?

Yes, and Chapter 5 routes you deliberately. If you come from commerce, law, BBA or any non-engineering degree, Route 3 — the compliance and privacy door — is the fastest realistic entry, because the DPDP Rules create work that is reading, mapping and evidence rather than terminals. Chapter 7 then lists the eleven foundation competencies with a plain statement of what enough looks like for each, so you are not guessing whether you are ready.

Is this another motivational book about cybersecurity being the future?

No. Chapter 1 states plainly that the same survey showing a 73% talent shortage also shows automation reducing entry-level roles, and that of 1,30,000 active technology openings in November 2026 only 16,000 were entry level. The book is built around that constraint rather than around it, which is why it spends more pages on evidence and sequencing than on encouragement.

Which certifications does it recommend, and what do they cost?

Chapter 14 gives the published prices, read from the awarding bodies in November 2026: CompTIA Security+ at $439, ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity at $199, EC-Council CEH at $1,199 for the Pearson VUE voucher or $950 for the remote-proctored one, OffSec OSCP at $1,699 for the exam alone or $1,749 for the course and cert bundle, ISC2 CISSP at $749, and ISACA CISM or CISA at $575 for members and $760 for non-members. It also explains why CEH should not be your first purchase, why the ISC2 CC is no longer free after the One Million Certified programme closed in May 2026, and which annual maintenance fees continue after you pass.

Where do the hiring and salary numbers come from?

Naukri JobSpeak for August, September and October 2026; Xpheno's Active Tech Jobs Outlook for November 2026; Quess Corp's India GCC Tech Talent Landscape for Q2 FY27; the DSCI-SANS Indian Cyber Security Skilling Landscape Report published in May 2026; ISC2's 2026 India findings; ManpowerGroup's Q4 2026 employment outlook; and Indian salary studies published in 2026, including reporting in the Hindustan Times and Mint. Every figure names its month, and Appendix C lists all of them.

Does it cover the legal side, so I do not get into trouble?

Chapter 22 does, in detail: sections 43, 43A, 66, 66B, 66C, 66D, 66F, 69 and 70B of the Information Technology Act, what written authorisation must contain before testing starts, the bug-bounty scope trap that catches beginners, and why an FIR under section 66 ends a security career in India before it starts.

What is the 178-day deadline in the chapter list?

India's DPDP Rules were notified on 13 November 2025 with a phased start. Consent Manager registration opened on 13 November 2026, and the main obligations for every data fiduciary — notice, security safeguards, breach intimation with a 72-hour detailed report to the Board, retention and erasure — begin on 13 May 2027. From the date the book was written that is 178 days, and it is the reason compliance and security hiring in India is being pulled forward now.
