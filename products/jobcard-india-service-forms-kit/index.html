The Disputes This Prevents

Almost every service-business argument is about a fact nobody wrote down

“The remote was in the box”

The most common handover dispute in appliance repair. A ticked accessory list, read back to the customer at intake, settles it in one line.

“I never agreed to ₹2,400”

An estimate signed before work begins — or marked "approved on call, 4:20 pm" — is the difference between a paid invoice and a discount you did not plan.

“It was working when I gave it to you”

A condition note and four photographs at intake, recorded on the same page the customer signs, protects you from damage you did not cause.

“The part you fitted failed in a month”

A warranty certificate that separates your workmanship warranty from the manufacturer's part warranty makes the answer obvious instead of negotiable.

“The unit is not covered under AMC”

Equipment listed, visits counted, exclusions ticked, response time stated. An AMC that says what is excluded is the only kind that survives a bad month.

“I already paid you”

A receipt with the mode, the UTR and the balance due is the record that outlives a WhatsApp chat and a changed phone.

The Eight Forms

Each is a complete A4 form with its own terms — not a blank grid

  1. 01 · Job Card / Service Request

    Customer and contact, item received with brand, model and serial, the complaint in the customer's own words, an accessory checklist (power cable, remote, battery, memory card, cover, manual, mounting kit), condition at intake, estimate given, advance taken, technician assigned, and five terms covering data, storage charges and the workmanship warranty.

  2. 02 · Service Estimate / Quotation

    Seven line rows with HSN or SAC, quantity, rate and amount; sub-total, discount, CGST and SGST, round-off and total; advance required; a validity date; an "excluded unless written above" checklist; and a clause capping variation at 10 per cent without fresh approval.

  3. 03 · Work Order / Job Assignment

    What the technician is sent to do, parts issued with serial numbers, a pre-work safety checklist, completion details, and signatures from both the technician and the customer at the site.

  4. 04 · Delivery Challan

    Consignee, challan number, goods with HSN, quantity and serial numbers, reason for movement (repair return, job work, replacement, sale, approval), e-way bill reference, packages, declared value, and a remarks box for shortage or damage at delivery.

  5. 05 · Annual Maintenance Contract

    Equipment covered, preventive visits, response time, working hours, what is included and chargeable, an eight-point exclusion checklist, annual fee with GST, escalation, termination on 30 days' notice and jurisdiction.

  6. 06 · Warranty Certificate

    What is covered and for how long, what the warranty promises in plain words, an eight-point "what voids this" checklist, and a claim process with the numbers to call.

  7. 07 · Work Completion & Handover Note

    Work carried out, parts replaced with their warranty, an eight-point test checklist, billing, advance adjusted, balance received with mode and UTR, a customer remarks box and acceptance.

  8. 08 · Payment Receipt

    Received from, against which job or invoice, amount in figures and words, mode (UPI, cash, card, NEFT, cheque), UTR or cheque number, total job value, balance due, next instalment with a due date, and notes.

Questions

Can I edit the forms in Word?

Yes. Every form is an unlocked .docx that opens in Word 2016 or later, Microsoft 365, LibreOffice, Google Docs, WPS Office and Pages. Change the business name, the logo, a clause, or the storage charge — the layout is built from tables, so it holds when you type.

Are these legally valid?

A form signed by both sides is useful evidence of what was agreed, and that is the point of the kit. The wording is a sensible starting point for an Indian service business — it is not legal advice, and it is not a substitute for your own lawyer's review if a dispute is likely to be significant. The guide says the same thing.

Is a delivery challan the same as a GST invoice?

No. A challan documents the movement of goods; a tax invoice documents a supply and charges GST. The challan in this kit states that on the form, and carries HSN, serial numbers and an e-way bill reference. If you need a GST invoice with line items and a UPI payment block, this kit is not that product — the estimate covers the quotation side, not the final tax invoice.

Can I print as many copies as I want?

Yes. Unlimited printing in your own business, for as long as you like. Duplicate or carbonless pads work well for job cards and receipts: one writing gives you an office copy and a customer copy.

Do the forms work for a business without GST registration?

Yes. Leave the GSTIN field blank, put a dash in the CGST and SGST rows on the estimate, and the rest of the form is unaffected. The guide explains which fields to skip.

Can I use them on a tablet instead of printing?

Yes. Open the PDF in Adobe Acrobat, Xodo or Samsung Notes, write with a stylus, then export a flattened PDF and send it to the customer on WhatsApp. Keep the flattened copy against the job number.
