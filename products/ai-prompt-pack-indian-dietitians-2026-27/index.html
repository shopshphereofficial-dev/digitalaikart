Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Do I need to know anything about AI to use this pack?

No. Every prompt is written to be pasted into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any modern assistant. You fill in the square brackets with the client's details and edit the result. The pack opens with a Master Context block you paste once, and a page of five habits that make every prompt work.

Will AI give my clients wrong nutrition or calorie advice?

It will if you let it — which is exactly what this pack is built around. Every planning prompt carries a hard rule against calorie, gram and macro figures, because models estimate Indian portions badly. Part 9 is a full troubleshooting section that deletes numbers rather than correcting them, and Appendix E is a 20-point checklist to run before anything reaches a client. AI drafts; you review.

Is this safe to use with real client data?

The pack treats client data as the serious issue it is. Part 8 covers consent and scope, records and retention, and a data-minimisation checklist that includes the re-identification trap most people miss — a city plus a profession plus an age can identify a person. Prompt 98 is the strip list to run before you paste anything, and the Master Context block tells the assistant to refuse identifying detail.

Are the GLP-1 prices and market figures current?

Yes. The figures reflect the Indian market after semaglutide lost patent protection on 20 March 2026, the generic launches that followed, and Novo Nordisk's price cut from 1 April 2026 — sourced from Reuters, Mint, Forbes India and the launch reporting of that period. Prices in this market are moving fast, so the pack presents every figure as indicative, dates it, and tells you to confirm current pricing before quoting it to anyone.

I am not a dietitian — is this for me?

It is written for registered dietitians, clinical nutritionists and qualified nutrition coaches working with clients in India. It assumes you are the professional whose name goes on the document. If you are a general wellness creator without a nutrition credential, this pack is not the right fit — several prompts deliberately exist to keep you inside a scope of practice.
