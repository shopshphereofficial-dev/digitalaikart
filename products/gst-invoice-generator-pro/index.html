What You Can Do With It

The everyday billing jobs, handled in one small tool

How It Works

Fill in your business name, address, home state, GSTIN (if registered), bank/UPI details and invoice number prefix.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Is this software or a book?

It is a small software tool, not an ebook. You get a folder with an index.html file; you double-click it and it opens in your browser as a working GST invoice and quotation maker. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once you have downloaded the folder, creating invoices, quotations, clients and PDFs all work with the internet switched off. Your data is saved in your own browser, not on any server.

How does it decide CGST, SGST or IGST?

You set your home state once in Settings and pick the place of supply on each document. If the place of supply is your own state the tool splits the tax into CGST and SGST; if it is a different state it charges IGST. The summary panel shows which one is being applied.

Can I save the invoice as a PDF?

Yes. Press Print / Save as PDF and in your browser’s print window choose Save as PDF. The print layout is a clean white A4 tax invoice — the coloured app interface is not printed.

Do I need to be GST-registered to use it?

No. If you are not registered you can leave your GSTIN blank and the tool still produces clean invoices and quotations. If you are registered, add your GSTIN and it prints on the document.

Does it file my GST returns?

No. This tool prepares invoices and quotations and helps you keep a client book and payment tracker. It does not file returns, generate e-invoices or connect to the GST portal. GST rates and rules can change, so verify the current position on the GST portal or with your CA before filing.

Is it a subscription?

No. It is a one-time purchase. There is no monthly or yearly fee, no per-invoice charge and no user limit inside the tool.
