The GCC Fresher Playbook 2026-27

How to get hired by a Global Capability Centre in India

IT services cut fresher hiring 11% in FY26. GCCs hired 1.4 to 1.5 lakh freshers in the same year, paid roughly Rs 3 lakh a year more, and grew intake another 12% in FY27. The door did not close - it moved to a different building. This is the 87-page playbook on how to walk through it: seven entry lanes, one defensible project, referrals that get replies, greenfield GCCs before the job is posted, the full interview loop, and internship-to-offer conversion.

Everything you need to know before buying

Is this book only for computer science students?

No. Chapter 5 maps seven separate entry lanes - software, data and analytics, AI operations, cloud and DevOps, core engineering and ER&D, BFSI operations and risk, and business and product. Each has its own skill bar, its own interview format and its own eight-week evidence plan. Commerce, economics, mechanical, electrical and science graduates all have a lane here.

I have no projects and no internship. Is it too late?

No, and the book is written for exactly that starting point. Chapter 28 is a 90-day plan that assumes you begin with nothing: two weeks choosing a lane and setting up your profile, six weeks building one defensible project, then applications and interview preparation. Chapter 10 gives five project briefs you can execute in that window.

Is the salary information verified?

The headline figures are sourced and dated: TeamLease research reported by the Economic Times in May 2026 projected GCC entry-level pay at about Rs 7.5 lakh against about Rs 4.5 lakh in IT services, and EY's Future of Pay 2026 puts GCC increments at 10.4%. The role-by-role bands in Chapter 7 are clearly labelled indicative, because no official source publishes them and the published ranges disagree.
