What's Inside

Seven documents that cover the whole client lifecycle

  1. 1 · Freelance Services Agreement

    Your master contract — scope, term, fees with GST and TDS, IP assignment on full payment, confidentiality, independent-contractor status, termination and kill fee, liability cap and arbitration.

  2. 2 · Mutual NDA

    Protect your ideas before you pitch or share work — mutual confidentiality with clear exclusions, a term and survival period, and return-or-destroy obligations.

  3. 3 · Project Proposal

    Win the client: restate the brief, list deliverables, show the timeline and milestones, present the investment table, and close with an acceptance block.

  4. 4 · Scope of Work (SOW)

    Attach it to the agreement as Annexure A. Deliverables, milestone payment schedule, out-of-scope list, assumptions, revision rounds and acceptance criteria.

  5. 5 · Change Request / Work Order

    The single best defence against scope creep — record the change, why it's out of scope, the extra time and cost, and get it signed before you do the work.

  6. 6 · Payment Receipt

    Acknowledge every payment cleanly — amount, mode, UTR, invoice reference, TDS and balance remaining — with a note on when a GST invoice is needed instead.

  7. 7 · Payment Reminder Scripts

    Four escalating email and WhatsApp templates — gentle, firm, hold-on-work and final notice — plus what never to do when chasing an overdue invoice.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Is this an ebook or a template pack?

It is a template pack, not an ebook. You receive a ZIP with seven ready documents as HTML files you fill in your browser and print to PDF, plus the same documents as editable Word (.docx) files, a start-here guide, a filled example and a licence.

Are these templates valid in India?

The templates are drafted for India — they refer to the Indian Contract Act 1872, GST, TDS under section 194J, and arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996, and use Indian conventions like PAN/GSTIN and lakh formatting. They are general-purpose drafting aids, not legal advice. Confirm tax and stamp-duty positions with a chartered accountant or advocate, and have a professional review anything high-value or complex.

Do I need Word, or any software?

No. Open the HTML files in any browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari), type into the highlighted fields, and print or save as PDF. If you or your client prefer Word, the same documents are included as .docx files for Microsoft Word, Google Docs, Pages or LibreOffice.

Does it work offline? Is my data uploaded?

Yes, it works fully offline after download. Nothing is uploaded anywhere — what you type is saved only in your own browser so you can close the file and come back to it.

Can I use these for my clients?

Yes. The licence lets you use the templates for your own business and for unlimited paying client projects, and modify them for each engagement. You may not resell or redistribute the template files themselves.

Will this guarantee I get paid?

No template can guarantee payment. What a clear agreement does is set expectations — scope, milestones, late fees and IP transfer only on payment — and give you a written record if a dispute arises. That, plus the reminder scripts, is what turns most late payments around.
