Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Is this guide useful if I am buying in the festive season right now?

Yes. It is written for the Dhanteras-to-Diwali window — Dhanteras on 6 November 2026 and Diwali on 8 November 2026. It gives you a six-week planner that starts from late October, so you can compare dealers calmly and take delivery on your muhurat.

Is the content up to date?

Yes. Every fact is checked against official sources as of mid-October 2026 — GST Council and CBIC notifications, Ministry of Heavy Industries PM E-DRIVE notifications, IRDAI circulars, the RBI's October 2026 policy, the Central Motor Vehicles Rules and Vahan registration data. It also corrects a common error: the electric two-wheeler subsidy was extended to 31 March 2028, not ended in July 2026.

Does it cover electric two-wheelers?

Yes. There is a full EV section — the AIS-156 safety standard, the PM E-DRIVE subsidy, a battery-warranty comparison across brands, the State-of-Health guarantee, and what voids a battery warranty.
