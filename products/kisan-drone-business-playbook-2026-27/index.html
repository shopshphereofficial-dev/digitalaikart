What's Inside

Twelve parts, no padding

💰 The subsidy slabs, line by line

Kisan Drone at 50% up to Rs 5 lakh for SC/ST, small and marginal farmers, women and the North-East; 40% up to Rs 4 lakh for others; the agriculture graduate route at 50%; CHC projects up to Rs 250 lakh; Farm Machinery Bank at 80 to 90%.

🌱 The demand subsidy nobody claims

Rs 2,000 per hectare for hiring drones from CHCs and women SHGs, up to 2 hectares a year, released by DBT after georeferenced verification. About Rs 810 an acre against a market rate of Rs 300 to Rs 700.

⚖️ Drone Rules 2021, reduced

UIN on Digital Sky, type certificate, remote pilot certificate at Rs 100 valid ten years, green and yellow zone limits, the 8 to 12 km airport rule, third party insurance under Part X, and the DGFT import gate.

🚁 Choosing the machine

What 5, 10, 16 and 40 litre machines actually cost in India, why a 16 litre is the default, the seven questions for the dealer, and the parts of the quotation nobody asks about — battery count, charger input, nozzle spectrum.

🎯 Spray numbers that keep customers

Height 1.5 to 2.5 m, speed 4.5 to 5 m/s, 20 to 25 litres per hectare, 250 to 350 micron droplets, wind under 3 m/s, plus the calibration routine and the spray log that settles disputes.

📊 Break-even before you borrow

A full model at real 2026 prices: Rs 6.6 lakh capex, subsidy, EMI, Rs 230 an acre variable cost, and roughly 64 acres a month to break even. Three scenarios with seasonality built in.

📞 Twenty customers, then forty names

Village clusters, the demo that converts, pricing by crop and plot size, FPO and input-dealer tie-ups, and the referral line that works better than any poster.

🧾 Ten printable worksheets

Eligibility, capital cost, per-acre cost, break-even, pricing, twelve-month cash flow, pipeline, spray log, battery register and season review — plus a 30-day launch plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything worth knowing before you buy

Is this useful if I have not bought a drone yet?

That is exactly who it is written for. Parts 1 and 7 are meant to be read before you spend anything: one tells you whether your district has demand, the other tells you how many acres a month the machine needs before it pays for itself.

Does it cover the subsidy paperwork?

Yes. The SMAM slabs from the guidelines revised in August 2026, the Custom Hiring Centre and Farm Machinery Bank routes, the Class 10 and trained-pilot conditions, the Rs 50 lakh DPR threshold, the Namo Drone Didi package and the Rs 2,000 per hectare hiring support are all covered, with the document each figure comes from.

Is the licensing part current?

It is built on the Drone Rules, 2021 as they stand in November 2026: remote pilot eligibility and the Rs 100 fee, UIN registration on Digital Sky, the green, yellow and red zones, third party insurance under Part X, the DGFT import gate, and the 2024 aviation law change and 2025 draft drone bill that shape what comes next.

Does it tell me what to charge?

Yes, with the reasoning behind each number: a rate band by crop and plot size, why hourly pricing punishes you for being fast, and how the Rs 2,000 per hectare support changes what a farmer can afford to pay in low mechanisation districts.

Are the worksheets printable?

There are ten, laid out as plain ruled tables: eligibility, capital cost, per-acre cost, break-even, pricing, twelve-month cash flow, customer pipeline, spray log, battery and maintenance register, and a season review. Print them or copy the columns into a notebook.
