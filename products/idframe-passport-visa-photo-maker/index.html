A Look at the Sheet

The layout the tool produces for 51 × 51 mm photos on A4 — three columns, five rows, gold guides on the first cell

What the guides mean

The band is the chin-to-crown head height the chosen size requires — 25–35 mm for a US visa, 32–36 mm for a Schengen photo. The two gold lines are where the top of the head and the chin should land. The dashed line is the approximate eye level. The dashed border is a 1.5 mm keep-clear edge so the head never touches the trim.

Guides are drawn on screen only. They never appear on the printed sheet or in the exported photo, and you can switch them off.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Is this an ebook?

No. You get a working tool — a folder of HTML, CSS and JavaScript you open in your browser — plus instructions and a licence. There is no book to read.

Do I need internet or an account?

No. The tool runs entirely from the folder on your own computer or phone. There is no sign-up, no login, no server and no analytics, so your photograph is never uploaded anywhere.

Which photo sizes are built in?

Eight presets: Indian passport 51 × 51 mm, the everyday 35 × 45 mm used by PAN, Aadhaar, exam and job forms, stamp size 20 × 25 mm, US visa 51 × 51 mm, Schengen 35 × 45 mm, UK 35 × 45 mm, Canada 35 × 45 mm, and a custom size where you type the millimetres yourself.

Will the passport office accept the photo?

The tool prints the size and head height that the issuing authority publishes, and it measures your background and resolution before you print. It cannot promise acceptance — that depends on the photograph itself, the light, your pose and the officer in front of you. Check the current rule on the form you are filling in.

Does it remove or change my background?

No. It does not cut out the background and it does not retouch the face, because most authorities prohibit altering facial features. Shoot against a plain, evenly lit wall instead — the tool tells you whether the corners of your photo look bright and even enough.

My printed photo came out the wrong size. What went wrong?

Almost always the printer scaled the page. In the print dialog set Margins to None and Scale to 100%, and switch off “Fit to page” or “Shrink oversized pages”. Print one sheet and measure a photo with a ruler before printing a stack.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The interface works in mobile Chrome, Safari and Firefox, and the sheet can be downloaded as a PNG to print elsewhere. Printing from a phone needs a printer app or a wireless printer, so most people export the PNG and print it from a computer or a shop.

Can I make a JPG for an online form?

Yes. “Download one photo (JPG)” exports exactly one photo at its pixel size, with a quality slider and a live file-size read-out, so you can get under a portal's KB limit. Most Indian portals accept 20 KB to 200 KB.

Which paper sizes can I print on?

A4, A5, 4 × 6 inch photo paper, 5 × 7 inch photo paper and US Letter, each with adjustable margin and gap. On A4 with a 6 mm margin you get 15 photos of 51 × 51 mm or 30 photos of 35 × 45 mm.
