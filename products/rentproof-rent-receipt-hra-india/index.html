The Problem With Rent Receipts

Every January, the same scramble

Payroll asks for twelve months of rent receipts. You have a rent agreement and a bank statement, and nothing that looks like the form they want. So you write receipts by hand, or hunt for a free website that wants your landlord's PAN typed into someone else's server, and then discover the receipt is missing the property PIN code, or the amount in words, or the receipt number payroll insists on.

Meanwhile the HRA calculation itself is quietly wrong for a lot of people. From tax year 2026-27 the 50% limit is no longer "the four metros" — Rule 279 of the Income-tax Rules, 2026 extends it to Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad and Bengaluru as well. Anyone still applying 40% in Bengaluru is leaving exemption on the table; anyone applying 50% in Gurugram is claiming too much and will see it reversed. RentProof gets both the paperwork and the arithmetic right, in one file that never leaves your laptop.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Is this an ebook or a working app?

A working app. The ZIP contains one HTML file that opens in your browser as a rent-receipt generator and HRA calculator — twelve months of receipts, the exemption working, 80GG, the declaration and the checklist. Nothing to install, no account to create.

What exactly does it produce?

Twelve rent receipts for the financial year you choose, April to March, two on each A4 page. Each carries the receipt number, tenant, landlord and PAN, the full property address, the period, the amount in figures and in words, the payment mode and reference, the date, a signature block, and a revenue-stamp box where one is needed. It also prints a one-page rent summary with the HRA working, and a landlord no-PAN declaration.

What is the HRA exemption rule it applies?

The least of three amounts: the HRA actually received, the rent paid minus one-tenth of salary, and 50% or 40% of salary. Salary means basic pay plus dearness allowance where DA forms part of your terms of employment — not gross salary, and certainly not CTC.

Which cities get the 50% limit?

From tax year 2026-27, Rule 279 of the Income-tax Rules, 2026 gives the 50% limit to Mumbai, Kolkata, Delhi, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad and Bengaluru. Everywhere else — Gurugram, Noida, Kochi, Jaipur and the rest — it is 40%. For tax year 2025-26 and earlier, only Mumbai, Kolkata, Delhi and Chennai get 50% under Rule 2A. Choose the financial year in the app and the right rule is applied; the note under the calculation tells you which one is in force.

Is HRA available under the new tax regime?

No. The Section 10(13A) exemption applies only under the old regime. The app computes the exemption figure; whether you can use it depends on the regime you have chosen, and it will not pretend otherwise.

When is the landlord's PAN required?

Once the rent paid in the year exceeds ₹1,00,000. The app tracks the annual total as you type, warns you the moment you cross the threshold, and checks that the PAN is in the right shape — five letters, four digits, one letter.

What if my landlord has no PAN?

The Documents tab prints a declaration for the landlord to sign, stating that no PAN is held or that an application is pending. That is what most employers accept in place of the number. It means nothing without the landlord's signature, so print it, get it signed and keep a copy with the receipts.

Is a revenue stamp needed?

For rent paid in cash above ₹5,000 a month, a Re 1 revenue stamp is affixed and signed across. The app prints a stamp box on exactly those months when you set the mode to cash, and no box at all for bank transfer, UPI or cheque.

My rent changed mid-year. Can I handle that?

Yes. Every month has its own editable amount. Set a month to zero for a month you did not occupy the flat, and the annual total, the HRA figures and the printed receipts all follow from it.

Does it work for the year I am claiming now?

It covers 2025-26, 2026-27 and 2027-28. Receipts run April to March, which is how payroll and the return both work, and the metro rule switches with the year you select.

Can I print to PDF instead of paper?

Yes. Every print button opens your browser's print dialog, where Save as PDF gives you a file you can email to payroll. Two receipts fit on each A4 page, so twelve months come to six pages; if a receipt spills onto a second page, set the print margins to Default or Minimum.

Does it work offline, and where is my data?

The file loads nothing from the internet and works with the connection switched off. Your entries are stored in your own browser on your own device under the key rentproof-v1. Nothing is uploaded. There is a one-click JSON export and a matching import, so you can move everything to another laptop or keep a backup in Drive.

Does it file my return or talk to my employer?

No. It does arithmetic on the figures you enter and formats the documents. It does not file anything, connect to any portal, or verify that rent was paid — that is what the signed receipts and your bank statement are for.

Is this tax advice?

No. It is a calculator and a document generator. Rules change — Rule 279 itself is new from 1 April 2026 — so confirm the current position with your chartered accountant before relying on a figure for a filing.
