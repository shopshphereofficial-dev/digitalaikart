Everything you need to know before buying

Is this useful if I have not registered for SNAP yet?

That is exactly who it is written for. Section 3 walks through the registration portal step by step, including the two separate fees most candidates miss, and section 4 helps you decide whether to book one, two or three attempts before the 25 November deadline.

Where do the dates and fees come from?

From the SNAP 2026 bulletin published by Symbiosis International (Deemed University), the official snaptest.org site, and the admission calendars published by SIBM Pune, SIIB, SCMHRD, SIMS, SICSR, SSBF and SCIT. Everything was checked on 28 October 2026. Where an institute has not published a figure, the playbook says so instead of guessing.

Does it cover the new Ethics, Morality and Values section?

Yes — two full sections. One teaches a five-step method for value dilemmas, and the other is a bank of 20 workplace and campus dilemmas with model reasoning, written because SIU has not published a chapter-wise syllabus for the new 10-mark section.

Yes. Sections 17 to 24 cover the part most guides skip: the 17 institutes and 32 programmes, programme registration deadlines that fall before the result, published fee structures, the 50 + 10 + 40 composite merit score, GE-PI preparation with 20 interview questions, and the exam-day checklist.

Is it written for one exam date or all three?

All three. The plan covers 28 October to 26 December, including what to do in the six days between tests and how to decide whether to sit a second or third attempt.
