What It Does

Not a form to fill and hope. Each document states who does what, when, and what happens if they don't

📄 Seven documents, not one

A residential 11-month leave and licence, a commercial one for shops and offices, a registrable 12-month lease, a paying-guest licence, a renewal addendum, both directions of notice letter, and a handover and deposit settlement sheet. You use the one that matches the arrangement instead of bending a single template to fit everything.

🧾 GST and TDS lines already in place

The commercial agreement carries a GST clause for a registered landlord, a TDS clause for a tenant that must deduct under the Income-tax Act, and a line that says plainly what happens when the landlord is not registered. The residential documents say the opposite, because residential rent for personal residence is exempt.

💰 Deposit rules that protect both sides

The deposit is capped at two months' rent for residential and six for non-residential, following the Model Tenancy Act, 2021. Refund is due on handover, deductions must be itemised in a written settlement statement, and the deposit cannot be quietly set off against the last month's rent.

⏱️ Notice with a date and a proof

Both notice letters state the premises, the agreement, the reason and the date possession is due, and come with a proof-of-service sheet: mode, date, postal receipt number, acknowledgement card. A notice you cannot prove you served is the most common way an eviction or exit goes wrong.

📋 A handover sheet that ends arguments

Room by room, item by item, with columns for condition at start, condition at exit and the deduction claimed — plus keys issued and returned, meter readings at both ends, and the final settlement statement both parties sign. Photograph the page and it is your evidence.

🏪 Built for how Indian letting works

Society NOC and shifting formalities, non-occupancy charges, parking, maintenance split, the 24-hour entry rule, joint intimation to a Rent Authority where the State has adopted the Model Act, and a note on police intimation where the State requires it.

✍️ Editable, not locked

Every document is a .docx with no password, no protected sections and no macros. Change a clause, add a clause, delete the ones that do not apply, and keep the numbering tidy. The same file opens in Word, Google Docs, WPS Office and LibreOffice.

🖨️ Prints correctly the first time

A4, 2 cm margins, numbered clauses, page numbers in the footer and repeated table headers — so a signature page does not land alone and an annexure does not lose its column headings across a page break.

How to Use It

From download to a signed agreement in about twenty minutes

  1. Pick the document. Page two of the guide is a one-table decision: residential or commercial, eleven months or a year, whole premises or one room, starting or ending.
  2. Open the .docx in Word or Google Docs and fill every line of underscores — names, parents' names, addresses, PAN, Aadhaar last four digits, rent, deposit, dates. Leave nothing blank; write "Nil" where there is nothing to write.
  3. Choose your options. Square brackets like [the Licensee / the Licensor] are choices: keep one, delete the other and delete the brackets. Add or delete clauses as you and the other party agree.
  4. Complete the annexures. Premises, payment details, opening meter readings and the inventory. This is the part people skip and the part that decides deposit disputes.
  5. Print, stamp, sign. Print on stamp paper or e-stamp paper of the value your State prescribes, sign both copies on every page and in the margin, and have two witnesses sign at the end.
  6. Record the start on day one. Fill document 07 on the day of possession, photograph every room, note the meters and both sign. Two minutes then saves the deposit argument later.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this an ebook?

No. You get seven working documents in Word and PDF, a seven-page filling guide and a licence. There is nothing to read before you can use it: open document 01, fill the blanks, print it.

Is this legal advice?

No. These are professionally drafted templates, not legal advice. Rent and tenancy law in India is State law — the Model Tenancy Act, 2021 is a model that each State adopts, changes or ignores in its own Tenancy Act. Where the amount at stake or the term is significant, have the completed document checked by an advocate in your State. That is exactly why the guide tells you which questions to ask locally instead of pretending one template fits every State.

Which document do I need for a normal 11-month flat rent?

Document 01, the residential leave and licence. Document 03 is the lease for twelve months or longer, or where a lock-in period is agreed — a lease for more than one year is registrable under the Registration Act, 1908.

Do I have to register it?

An 11-month leave and licence is not compulsorily registrable, though many parties notarise it and many societies ask for that. A lease for more than one year must be registered at the Sub-Registrar's office. Separately, where your State has a Tenancy Act based on the Model Tenancy Act, 2021, the agreement is to be intimated to the Rent Authority, and some States also require police intimation of a letting. The guide walks through each of these.

How much deposit can a landlord ask for?

Where a State Act based on the Model Tenancy Act, 2021 applies, a residential deposit cannot exceed two months' rent and a non-residential deposit cannot exceed six months' rent. Where an older Rent Control Act still applies, practice can differ. The documents use the two-month and six-month figures and leave the amount for you to fill in.

Does it cover GST and TDS on rent?

Yes, in the commercial agreement and in the guide. Residential rent for use as a residence is exempt from GST; commercial rent is generally taxable at 18% where the landlord is registered. TDS applies above the threshold in the Income-tax Act — for an individual or HUF payer not liable to audit, rent above ₹50,000 for a month under section 194IB at 2%. The documents carry the lines; your CA's advice governs the numbers.

Can I edit the clauses?

Yes, every document is an unlocked .docx. Delete what does not apply, add what you have agreed, keep the numbering tidy, and make sure both parties sign the same version.

Will it work in Google Docs?

Yes. Upload the .docx to Drive and open it with Google Docs; tables, footers and page numbers carry over. Export to PDF with File → Download → PDF Document and send that to anyone who only needs to read it.

Is the inventory sheet useful if the flat is unfurnished?

Yes. Floors, tiles, doors, locks, windows, grills, paint, electrical points, sanitaryware and the water pump all take damage, and all of them are on the sheet. Record "OK" or "minor wear" on day one, photograph the room, and the exit conversation becomes a comparison instead of a memory.

Can I use it for several properties?

The licence covers any number of agreements that you personally draft and sign as a party — your own tenancies, your own premises, your family's. It does not cover resale, republishing, or running a paid drafting service for clients.

What if the other side refuses to sign the handover sheet?

Fill it yourself, photograph everything in their presence if you can, and send them a copy by e-mail or WhatsApp the same day. A record they received and did not dispute is far stronger than no record at all. The proof-of-service page in document 06 works the same way for notices.
