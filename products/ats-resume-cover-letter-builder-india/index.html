Every feature is built for one job: getting your resume past the first screen

    Type on the left, see a real A4 page on the right. What you see is what prints — margins, spacing and all.

  2. 2 · Job-advert keyword check

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Is this a book or a tool?

It is a tool, not an ebook. You receive a ZIP containing one HTML file — the builder app — plus a bonus ATS reference guide, an example profile, a read-me and a licence. You double-click the HTML file and it opens in your browser. There is nothing to install.

Does it need an internet connection or an account?

No. The builder works fully offline after you download it, with no sign-up and no login. Your details never leave your device — the app does not upload anything, and any saved draft stays in your own browser's local storage.

What makes a resume ATS-friendly?

Applicant Tracking Systems parse plain, single-column text most reliably and often mis-read tables, multiple columns, text boxes and images of text. Every template in the builder is deliberately single-column with standard section headings and text-only content. That is a formatting property, not a promise of any particular score at any particular company — the bigger lift comes from matching your content to the job advert.

How does the resume score work?

The score is a transparent checklist, not a black box. It weighs your contact details, summary length, skills count, experience bullets, how many bullets contain a number, how many start with a strong action verb, education, and how many of the job advert's key terms appear in your resume. Each bar shows exactly what you scored and what to fix.

How many resumes can I make?

Unlimited. There is no per-download fee and no cap. Build and tailor a resume and cover letter for every application, and reuse the tool forever.

How do I get a PDF?

Click Print / Save as PDF and choose Save as PDF in the print dialog. Set the paper size to A4 and turn Background graphics on so the accent colour prints. The tool prints only the document you are viewing — no app interface and no watermark.

Can I use it for other people, or as a resume writer?

Yes. The licence lets you use the builder for yourself and for clients, including paying clients, with no limit on the number of documents. You may not resell or redistribute the tool itself.

Is the example profile real?

No. Every name, number and company in the example is fictional and clearly labelled as example data. Replace it with your own real details, and never claim experience or results you do not have.

Is it a subscription?

No. It is a one-time purchase. There is no monthly or yearly fee, and no limit on how many resumes and cover letters you create.
