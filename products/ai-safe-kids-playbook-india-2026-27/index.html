Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Is this for Indian parents specifically?

Yes. It is built around Indian law — the DPDP Act 2023 and the DPDP Rules 2025, the IT Rules as amended in February 2026, POCSO and the BNS — and around the reporting routes that exist in India: cybercrime.gov.in, 1930, 1098, 112 and the POCSO e-Box. The device settings are the same everywhere, but every legal and reporting chapter is India-specific.

My child is only 8. Is this too early?

No — Chapter 21 has a rule card for ages 6 to 9, and Chapter 15 covers supervised YouTube accounts, which is the main AI-adjacent risk at that age. If your child is under 10, read Chapters 2, 10, 11, 12, 13, 15 and 21 and leave the rest until they are older.

Will the settings be out of date in a few months?

The menu names may move; the logic will not. Chapter 31 tells you what to search for in any app to find the same four controls, and the book teaches the principle behind each setting rather than only the tap sequence. Every date, age limit and rule in this edition was checked in October 2026 and the source is listed so you can verify it yourself.

Does it cover ChatGPT, Gemini and other AI chatbots?

Chapter 18 has a table of eleven services — ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Meta AI, Character.AI, Snapchat My AI, Perplexity, Poe, DeepSeek, Grok and Claude — with each one's minimum age, its under-18 experience and whether it offers parental controls. It also explains plainly what no AI service currently lets a parent do, so you are not sold monitoring software that does not work.

What if something has already happened?

Go to Chapter 25. It has seven scenario cards — morphed images, sextortion, money taken, account takeover, bullying, grooming and self-harm disclosure — each with what to do in the first hour and what to do after. Chapter 26 is the reporting ladder with every Indian channel and how to write a complaint that gets acted on.

Is this legal advice?

No. It explains what the statutes and rules say, in plain English, with section numbers and sources so you can check them and speak to a lawyer or the police without being lost. For a live dispute you still need a lawyer.
