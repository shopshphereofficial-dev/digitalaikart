Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Is this medical advice?

No. The playbook deals only with cost, access and paperwork — medicine prices, government schemes, insurance claims, relief funds and worksheets. It does not recommend any drug, surgery or hospital, and every cost decision in it is meant to be taken back to the treating oncologist before you act on it.

Are the prices and scheme rules current?

Every figure, price, portal and phone number was checked against Government of India sources on 17 November 2026 — PIB releases, the NPPA order S.O. 5690(E), the NPPA citizen charter, NHA and PM-JAY pages, Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha answers, and state scheme portals. Chapter 24 lists every source so you can re-verify anything yourself, and flags which facts move fastest.

What exactly changed on 9 November 2026?

NPPA capped the trade margin on 137 non-scheduled anti-cancer drugs at 30% of the MRP, with revised prices effective 9 November 2026. By that date 85 companies had filed revised prices for 121 drugs, more than 650 formulations were down over 50%, and the highest reduction reported was 96.45%. The playbook explains what the order covers, what it does not, and how to check whether your pharmacy has applied it.

Will this tell me what my treatment will cost?

It will not print hospital price ranges, because the same diagnosis varies by a factor of five between institutions and a printed range anchors families to the wrong number. Instead it teaches you to get a written estimate for the whole planned course, in a specific format, and gives you the nine questions that change that estimate.

Does it cover my state's scheme?

PM-JAY is covered in full, and Chapter 11 covers Telangana's Rajiv Aarogyasri, Andhra Pradesh's Dr YSR Aarogyasri, Tamil Nadu's CMCHIS, Maharashtra's MJPJAY and Odisha's GJAY with their 2026 cover levels and eligibility. For every other state it gives you a five-minute method to find your scheme, its ceiling and its empanelled oncology hospitals.
