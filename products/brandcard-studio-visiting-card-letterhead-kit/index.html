Why Print Files Go Wrong

Three failures that cost a reprint, every week, in every city

The size drifts. A card designed in a phone app or a screenshot is not 90 × 54 mm. When the file reaches the press it gets scaled to fit a page, and 90 mm becomes 88 mm. The text no longer sits inside the trim, so the cutting blade shaves the top of your phone number. BrandCard Studio works in millimetres from the start and prints ten cards on an A4 sheet with 5 mm margins and 3 mm gaps, so there is space for the blade to be slightly off without touching your type.

The back does not line up. Two separate files, printed as two separate runs, almost never register. The studio builds the back sheet mirrored: the right-hand card on the front sits under the left-hand card on the back. Print the two pages as a duplex job with flip on the long edge and the backs fall into place.

Nobody adds the QR. In a market that pays by UPI, a card with no QR is a missed payment at the counter. Load your UPI screenshot once and the studio places it on the back at 16 mm — big enough to scan, small enough to leave the address readable — and on the A5 rate card at 18 mm.

Ten Cards on One A4 Sheet

The layout is arithmetic, not guesswork

A4 is 210 × 297 mm. Five rows of 54 mm is 270 mm; with 5 mm margins top and bottom and four 3 mm gaps between rows, the sheet uses 292 mm of its 297 mm. Two columns of 90 mm plus a 10 mm centre gap and 10 mm margins uses exactly 210 mm. That is ten cards per sheet, with a millimetre or two of slack at the foot — which is what you want, because a press that is a fraction off should waste a millimetre of paper, not a line of your address. At 85 × 50 mm it is still ten to a sheet; at 54 × 90 mm vertical it is nine, three across and three down.

The sheet carries light cut guides around every card. Leave them on for a local press that trims by hand, or switch them off if your printer uses a programmable cutter.

How It Works

Ten minutes to a file the press can use

  1. Open index.html. It loads with an example shop filled in, so you can see a finished card before you type anything.
  2. Type your details. Business name, tagline, your name and designation, phone, WhatsApp, email, website, two address lines and GSTIN. Everything except the business name is optional.
  3. Pick a template and an accent colour. The front and back update as you type. Long business names shrink automatically so they cannot overflow the card.
  4. Load your UPI QR if you want one on the back, and fill the service list if you are printing the rate card.
  5. Choose what to print — front sheet, front and back, back only, letterhead, or the A5 rate card — and click Print / Save as PDF with margins at None and scale at 100%.
  6. Send the PDF to the press. Ask for 300 GSM art card with matte lamination for the cards, 100 GSM bond for the letterhead. Test one sheet on plain paper first.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this an ebook or a working tool?

A working tool. The ZIP holds one HTML file that opens in your browser as a card studio, a printable start-here guide, a readme, a licence and an example profile. You type your details, the studio draws the card at real size, and the browser saves a print-ready PDF.

Do I need Canva, Photoshop or a designer?

No. The studio is a single file with six built-in templates, eight accent colours and your text. Everything runs in the browser with no account, no subscription and no internet connection after the first open.

Will the cards print at the correct 90 × 54 mm size?

Yes, if you leave the print dialog at 100% / Actual size and turn off Fit to page. The card is defined in millimetres, so the PDF carries the real size. The sheet places ten cards on A4 with 5 mm margins and 3 mm gaps, so there is room to trim.

How does duplex printing work?

Choose front + back and you get a two-page PDF. The back sheet is mirrored so the right-hand card on the front sits under the left-hand card on the back when the press flips on the long edge. Print one sheet on plain paper first and hold it to the light before approving a full run.

Can I put my UPI QR on the card?

Yes. Screenshot the QR from any UPI app, load it as a PNG or JPG, and it prints at 16 mm on the back of the card and 18 mm on the A5 rate card. Nothing is uploaded anywhere; the image stays in your browser.

What paper and GSM should I ask the press for?

300 GSM art card with matte or velvet lamination for the visiting cards and the rate card, and 100 GSM bond or maplitho for the letterhead. Prices vary by city, GSM and lamination, so ask your press for a quote on 100 cards and 200 letterheads rather than trusting a number here.

Does it work offline, and is my data sent anywhere?

It runs entirely on your own computer. There are no server calls, no analytics and no fonts loaded from the internet. Your business details and QR image never leave the browser, and your last session is remembered locally.

Can I save my details and reprint later?

Yes. Save profile writes a small JSON file with every field. Load it next year, change one phone number and print again without retyping the rest.

Does it work on a phone?

It opens on a phone, but the editor and the print sheet are built for a laptop or desktop, and phones do not print A4 reliably. Design and print on a computer.

Can I use my own logo file?

Not in this version. The templates use a two-letter monogram drawn from your business name, which keeps every layout balanced without a logo. Logo upload is the most requested change and is being worked on.

My text is too long and gets cut off.

The studio shrinks long business names automatically, but a 60-character name on a 90 mm card will still be tight. Shorten it — the name on the card does not have to be the full registered name — and put the GSTIN on the back rather than the front.
