Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Do I need a lawyer to use this playbook?

No. A consumer can file and argue in person at every tier of India's consumer commissions, and most everyday claims need no lawyer at all. The playbook shows you how to file online through e-Jagriti, what to write, and when a lawyer is actually worth paying for.

How much does it cost to file a consumer complaint in India?

Nothing for a dispute where you paid up to Rs. 5 lakh — the filing fee is nil. Above that, fees start at Rs. 200 and rise with the value of the goods or services, as set out in the Consumer Protection (Consumer Disputes Redressal Commissions) Rules, 2020.

Is the content current for 2026-27?

Yes. It is built on the latest rules, including the Reserve Bank Integrated Ombudsman Scheme 2026 (in force from 1 July 2026), the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) (Amendment) Rules 2026 (effective 1 January 2027) and the e-Jagriti consumer-commission platform. Every figure, fee, deadline and portal is drawn from official Government of India and regulator sources, current as of October 2026.

Which sectors does it cover?

E-commerce and quick commerce, banking, UPI, cards and loans, insurance, telecom and broadband, airlines, railways, food, real estate and builders, investments and securities, couriers, utilities, healthcare, education, and cyber financial fraud — each with the exact forum, deadline and first step.
