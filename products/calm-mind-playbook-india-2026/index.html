Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Is this playbook a substitute for therapy?

No. It is a self-help toolkit that teaches skills drawn from established, evidence-based approaches and points you to official Indian services. It is not a diagnosis, therapy or a replacement for a qualified professional. Chapter 10 helps you recognise when it is more than stress, and Chapter 11 shows you exactly how to get free and paid help in India.

What if I am in crisis right now?

Please reach out immediately. Tele-MANAS is the Government of India's free, confidential helpline on 14416 (or 1-800-891-4416), available 24×7 in 20 languages. Vandrevala Foundation is free on +91 9999 666 555. For any immediate danger to life, call 112. You are not a burden, and help is free.

Do I need a diagnosis to use this?

Not at all. Most people who buy this are dealing with ordinary but exhausting stress, exam pressure, work burnout or poor sleep. The tools work whether or not you have a diagnosis, and the book is honest about when to seek professional care.

Yes.
