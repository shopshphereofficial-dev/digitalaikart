Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Can this book delete my defaults or guarantee a score jump?

No — and any product or agent that promises that is a scam. Accurate data cannot be deleted by anyone. What this book does is fix genuine errors through the official free dispute process, stop the habits that are actively damaging your score, and sequence the levers that actually move it. No honest guide can promise point numbers, because bureau formulas are proprietary.

Is the content current?

Yes. Everything was verified in September 2026 against the RBI's Credit Information Directions (updated 1 July 2026), the December 2025 Amendment Directions that brought in weekly reporting, cibil.com's own dispute and pricing pages, and the September 2026 Mint report on why even a 780 score gets rejected. All sources are listed on the last page.

Do I need to pay for my credit report to use this book?

No. RBI rules give you one free full credit report with score from each of the four bureaus — CIBIL, Experian, Equifax and CRIF High Mark — every calendar year. The book is built around those four free reports, plus free score views on reputable apps. Paid CIBIL plans are only mentioned as an optional fallback.

My loan was just rejected. Will this help?

That is exactly who it's written for. Chapter 7 shows you how to find out what went wrong (wrong entries, overdues, high utilisation, too many enquiries or FOIR), and the 90-day plan repairs it in order. You'll also learn why the next application should wait 3-6 months — and how to make it count.

I have no credit history, only a rejected card application. Is this for me?

Yes. Chapter 13 covers the thin-file route: an FD-backed secured card, add-on cards and small EMIs to build a clean trail — plus the RBI rule that first-time borrowers cannot be rejected merely for having no history.
