The Problem This Solves

In India, the phone is the highest-intent channel you have — and the one you drop most

A person who calls you has a problem right now. When that call rings out, they dial the next number on the list — almost always a competitor within five kilometres who paid the same ad cost you did. Industry research in 2026 found 62% of inbound calls to Indian small businesses go unanswered, 85% of callers who reach no one never call back, and 38% of missed calls at clinics arrive between 7 PM and 11 PM, exactly when the doors are shut. An AI voice receptionist answers on the first ring, in the caller's language, at every hour — and this playbook shows you exactly how to set one up, what it costs, and what you legally must do.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Do I need a developer to set this up?

No. The playbook is written for owner-operators with no technical background. It shows you the self-serve route on India-first platforms where you build the agent in a dashboard, connect your calendar, and go live in about two weeks, one sitting a day.

Is the vendor pricing in the book current?

Yes. Every per-minute rate, inclusion and language list was taken from the vendors' own public pricing pages and India pricing indexes checked in October 2026. The book also teaches you how to re-check and compare rates yourself, because prices in this market change often.

Will this work for Hindi and Hinglish calls?

Yes. The playbook covers Indian-language support vendor by vendor, the Hinglish code-switching that most callers actually use, the accent problem and how to beat it, and includes ready call scripts in both English and Hinglish.

What about DLT, TRAI and the 140/160 series?

Part 6 explains exactly when the 140 and 160 series apply, how to register as a Principal Entity on a DLT portal, the fees and timelines, and the penalties for non-compliance. It also covers the DPDP Rules 2025, call-recording disclosure and retention duties, and the safe inbound-first way to start.

Is this for my type of business?

It is written for appointment-driven and lead-driven Indian businesses — clinics, diagnostics labs, dental chains, salons and wellness studios, coaching and test-prep institutes, real-estate brokerages, home and repair services, car-service workshops, gyms, restaurants and D2C sellers. Agencies and freelancers can use Parts 12 and 14 to price voice-agent setup as a service.
