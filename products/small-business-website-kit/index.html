Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Is this a book or a website?

It is a set of five ready-made website templates, not an ebook. You receive a ZIP folder of HTML and CSS files. You open a template in your browser, edit the text in any editor, and upload the folder to a host. There is nothing to install and no coding beyond copy and paste.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The templates are plain HTML and CSS. You change the words between the tags and set your details. A one-page cheat sheet and a read-me walk through every edit, and the colours are controlled by five values at the top of one stylesheet.

How do the contact forms work without a server?

The forms need no server or database. When a visitor submits one, their message opens in WhatsApp ready to send, or opens their email app addressed to you. You set your own WhatsApp number and email in one line of the template.

Where can I host the finished site?

Anywhere that serves static files. The read-me gives step-by-step instructions for two free options — Netlify Drop and GitHub Pages — and you can connect your own domain on either.

Are the business names and reviews in the templates real?

No. Every name, phone number, price and review in the templates is clearly labelled sample content. You replace them with your own details, and you should only publish reviews that are genuine.
