Everything you need to know before buying

I have never exported anything. Is this kit for me?

Yes. It assumes you have never filed a shipping bill and do not know what an AD Code is. Chapters 1 to 4 cover the entire registration sequence — entity, IEC, GST, LUT, AD Code — with the portal, the field people get wrong and the cost of each step.

Is the information current for 2026-27?

Every rule was checked against the issuing authority on 3 November 2026 — DGFT notifications, CBIC circulars, RBI's FEMA regulations and the GST portal's user guides. The kit covers the 2026 changes specifically: the 15-month realisation period, the removal of the courier export value cap, the RCMC de minimis, RoDTEP's 31 December 2026 end date and the inventory-based cross-border e-commerce framework.

Does it work for my product?

The kit is written for goods exporters of any product line — textiles, food, handicrafts, engineering goods, chemicals, leather, gems. Chapter 3 shows you how to check your product's export policy and the regulator that applies to it, rather than assuming one category.

Does it cover GST refunds and RoDTEP claims?

Yes. Chapter 10 covers both refund routes — LUT with Form GST RFD-01 under Rule 89, and IGST paid with the shipping bill as the application under Rule 96 — including the exact GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B fields that block a refund when they do not match. Chapter 11 covers RoDTEP and duty drawback, with the full 2026 notification timeline and the 31 December 2026 end date.
