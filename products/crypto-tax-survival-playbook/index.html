Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Is this guide up to date with the new Income-tax Act 2025?

Yes. Every rule is mapped to both the old and new section numbers, so exchange statements citing Section 194S and department notices citing Section 393 both make sense. The book is verified against the Income-tax Act, 2025 (in force from 1 April 2026), the Income-tax Rules, 2026, CBDT circulars and notifications, and the Union Budget 2026-27, as of September 2026.

I am a small investor with only a few thousand rupees in crypto. Is this for me?

Yes. There is no minimum size for the reporting duties: the exchange reports your trades and TDS whether you invested Rs. 5,000 or Rs. 5 crore. The book shows the ten-step filing process that takes a simple single-exchange portfolio about an evening to file correctly.

I traded on foreign platforms like Binance or Bybit. Does the book cover that?

Yes, in detail. Chapter 7 covers worldwide taxation, the self-deduction TDS duty with Form 26QE, Schedule FA and the Black Money Act risk, and the CARF data-sharing that starts delivering your offshore transaction history to the CBDT from April 2027 — plus how to fix past returns before the data arrives.

I already received a crypto notice from the tax department. Will this help?

Chapter 10 is written for exactly that situation: the notice types, what each one means, the first-seven-days response plan, the penalty provisions, and the updated-return (ITR-U) route with its 25% and 50% additional-payment windows. For a live scrutiny notice, the book tells you when to bring in a CA.
