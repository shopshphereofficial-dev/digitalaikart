Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Is this guide specific to Navratri 2026?

Yes. Every date, tithi, colour and muhurat window is for Sharadiya Navratri 2026, which runs from Sunday 11 October to Monday 19 October, with Vijayadashami (Dussehra) on Tuesday 20 October. The rituals and planning framework stay useful every year, but the dates and timings are the verified 2026 ones.

Are the muhurat timings correct for my city?

The muhurat windows in the guide are given for New Delhi as a planning reference, with a few other cities noted, because tithi timings shift with your city's sunrise. The guide tells you to confirm the Ghatasthapana, Sandhi Puja and Parana windows on your local panchang or with your family priest before you begin.

Do I need to fast for all nine days?

No. The guide explains that a partial vrat — vrat food once a day, or fasting only on Ashtami and Navami — is perfectly acceptable and common, and it carries a clear health-first note. Anyone who is pregnant, unwell, elderly or managing a condition such as diabetes should follow a doctor's advice rather than a strict fast.
