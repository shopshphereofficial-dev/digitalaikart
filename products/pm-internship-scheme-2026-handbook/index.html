Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Is the PM Internship Scheme really paying Rs. 9,000 a month?

Yes. Since the revised guidelines of 12 March 2026, interns receive Rs. 9,000 a month — Rs. 900 from the host company (from CSR or own funds) and Rs. 8,100 from the government via Direct Benefit Transfer — plus a one-time Rs. 6,000 grant paid in two tranches of Rs. 3,000. Older guides quoting Rs. 5,000 are out of date.

Who is eligible for the PM Internship Scheme?

Indian citizens aged 18 to 25, not in full-time employment or full-time education, who have passed at least Class 10 or hold an ITI certificate, diploma, degree or master's degree, with family income under Rs. 12 lakh a year. Final-year students can apply with a NoC. MBA/CA/CMA/CS/MBBS/BDS/PhD holders and graduates of IIT/IIM/NLU/IISER/NID/IIIT/IISc are excluded.

Does this handbook come from the government?

No. It is an independent, plain-English guide built from official sources — the PMIS portal, Ministry of Corporate Affairs guidelines including the 12 March 2026 revision, UGC guidelines, PIB releases and Parliament replies. The official portal at pminternship.mca.gov.in remains the final authority on your application.

Is the content up to date?

Yes. It reflects the revised PMIS guidelines of 12 March 2026 and Round III of the pilot, which is live until December 2026, with data as of 15 July 2026. The handbook tells you to confirm anything time-sensitive on the official portal.
