Yes. The rules, the No Claim Bonus ladder, the surveyor threshold and the claim process are the same for two-wheelers and cars. Where the two differ — long-term third-party cover, engine protection, theft of a two-wheeler — the book says so.

No. It is educational content that explains how motor insurance works and how the rules protect you. It is not a substitute for your own policy document or for professional advice. Where the book and your policy differ, your policy governs.
