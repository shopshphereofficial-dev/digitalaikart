Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

I am not good with technology. Can I still use this pack?

Yes. Every prompt is designed to be pasted into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, and the free versions of all three are enough. You replace the words in square brackets with your own facts and press send. If you can send a WhatsApp message, you can use this pack. Page 9 has a Master Context block you paste once, so the assistant already knows your school, fees and rules.

Is this only for big preschools or franchises?

It is written for the independent centre — one or two branches, an owner who also teaches or supervises, and a chain with a bigger signboard two streets away. The prompts assume a small team and no marketing department. Every template is one you can fill in yourself in an afternoon.

Are the facts in the appendices current?

The appendices were checked in November 2026 against the NCPCR Regulatory Guidelines for Private Play Schools, the Delhi Directorate of Education circulars on the NEP foundational stage and the 6+ age rule, the CBSE assessment composition for 2026-27, and each vendor's own pricing page for India. Preschool regulation is a state subject, so the pack states the scope of every rule and tells you to confirm your own state's position with the district education office.

Will AI write things about my school that are not true?

It will if you let it, which is why almost every prompt carries a hard rule against inventing fees, facilities, ratios, reviews, certifications or state rules. Appendix F is a ten-point verification checklist, and the three-minute test on the same page is the habit the pack is really teaching. AI drafts; you verify before anything reaches a parent.

Does this replace a consultant or an admissions agency?

No. It replaces the blank page. The prompts, the visit flow, the fee sheet, the register and the follow-up sequence are the same work an agency would charge you a retainer for, but you run them yourself. What it cannot do is answer your phone within the hour, which is the single biggest factor in whether an enquiry converts.
