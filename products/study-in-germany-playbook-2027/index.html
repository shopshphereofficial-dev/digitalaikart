Is this guide current for the 2027 intakes?

Yes. Every fee, deadline and rule was checked against German government, DAAD, APS India, uni-assist, TUM, Techniker Krankenkasse and university sources between 1 and 3 November 2026. Where a rule is still a proposal, such as the BAföG reform that may raise the blocked account amount, the book says so in plain words.

What is dMAT and does it apply to me?

dMAT is the Digital Master Test from g.a.s.t., added to APS documentation for listed master's subject groups. It applies if your APS registration and document shipment were on or after 29 June 2026, your previous degree is in engineering, commerce, accounting, finance, economics, business or management, and you are applying for the summer semester 2027 master's intake or later. Chapter 11 covers the fee, the 16 January and 13 March 2027 test dates and the sequencing that saves two months.

Does this book help if I am still deciding whether to go to Germany?

That is what Part One is for. It puts the real three-year cost sheet in front of you, corrects the "Germany is free" claim with the exact places you pay — Baden-Württemberg at 1,500 euros a semester and TUM at 2,000 to 6,000 — and gives you a ten-question filter to decide before you spend a rupee on APS.
