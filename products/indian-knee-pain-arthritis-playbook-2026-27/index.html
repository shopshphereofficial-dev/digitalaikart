Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Is this book only about arthritis, or does it cover other knee pain?

Both. Chapter 1 maps the eight patterns that explain most knee pain — osteoarthritis, patellofemoral pain, meniscal tears, IT band syndrome, bursitis, patellar tendinitis, referred hip pain and inflammatory arthritis — and shows how each is treated differently. The rest of the book is built around osteoarthritis, because that is what most Indian knee pain turns out to be.

I am in my thirties with knee pain. Is this book for me?

Yes. Chapter 1 helps you work out what kind of knee pain you have, Chapter 7 is the strength programme, and Chapter 18 is written specifically for people under 40 who want to keep the knee they have. Several chapters assume nothing about your age.

Will this book replace my doctor?

No, and it does not try to. It is an educational guide. What it does is put the same guidelines your doctor works from — ICMR, NICE, OARSI — into plain language, with Indian prices attached, so that you ask better questions and stop paying for treatments that do not work. It never prescribes doses, and it tells you repeatedly when to see someone.

Is the information current and India-specific?

Yes. It draws on the ICMR Standard Treatment Workflow for knee osteoarthritis (July 2024), NICE NG226 (2022), the OARSI 2019 guidelines, the ICMR-NIN Dietary Guidelines for Indians (2024), NPPA implant price ceilings in force to 15 November 2026, and Indian community prevalence studies published in 2024 and 2025. Every figure is traceable to the source appendix at the back.

Does it cover surgery costs and insurance?

Chapter 15 covers when surgery is actually indicated, using the ICMR's own list of indications and contraindications, plus the eight questions to ask a surgeon. Chapter 16 covers the money: the NPPA implant ceiling of Rs 51,563, the four private cost bands, PM-JAY and state schemes, CGHS and ESIC, and what IRDAI's 2024 rules mean for a pre-existing condition.
