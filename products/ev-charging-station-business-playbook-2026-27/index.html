Frequently Asked Questions

Everything worth knowing before you buy

I have not bought anything yet. Is this the right starting point?

Yes — that is exactly who it is written for. The first five chapters are meant to be read before you spend anything: they tell you what the business actually is, where India's electric fleet really sits, and what the median charger in the country earns. Part Two then gives you a way to test a specific site in three visits.

Is the content current?

Everything was checked in the first week of November 2026. That includes the Ministry of Power guidelines of September 2024, PM E-DRIVE's operational guidelines of September 2025, CEA regulations of 2023, the battery swapping guidelines of January 2025, Vahan registration data through October 2026, the Unified Bharat e-Charge launch of August 2026, and the state EV policies notified between 2022 and 2025.

Will this tell me exactly how much I will earn?

No, and you should distrust anything that does. It gives you the arithmetic — units sold, margin per unit, fixed costs — and runs it at four utilisation levels so you can see how violently the answer moves. You then put your own site's traffic count and your own vendor quotes into the worksheets.

Does it cover the subsidy I can actually claim?

It covers both. PM E-DRIVE's Rs 2,000 crore for public charging is open only to government and public sector entities, and the book says so plainly rather than implying otherwise. The state table covers the notified terms in Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh and Karnataka, and gives you the five questions to ask your own state nodal agency.

Is this useful if I want to put a charger at my shop or society rather than build a station?

Yes. Part Two covers the host model and the destination site in detail, and Chapter 28 works through why a mall or society installation usually should not be an owned asset — and what to negotiate instead.

Does it recommend a particular charger brand?

No. It lists what Indian sellers were charging in November 2026 so you know the range, and gives you nine questions to ask any vendor, including the type test certificate, the OCPP version and the response time. There is no dealer relationship behind this book.
