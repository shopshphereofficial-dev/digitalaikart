Do I need to know anything about AI to use this pack?

No. Every prompt is written to be pasted into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any modern assistant. You fill in the square brackets with your client's details and edit the result. Will AI invent prices, brands or materials for my clients?

It will if you let it — which is exactly the problem this pack is built around. Every prompt carries a hard rule: never invent prices, brands, project statistics or client quotes, and leave a placeholder instead.
