The Problem It Solves

You are good at coaching. Nobody taught you the business.

Here is how the fourth month usually goes. The phone goes quiet. Two clients stop replying without ever saying they have stopped. The diary is full but the bank account is not, because you priced a session instead of pricing an hour that includes travel, prep and WhatsApp. A client asks for a refund and you realise there is no agreement to point at. None of that is a fitness problem, and no certification course covers it. It is pricing, retention, paperwork and tax — four things that can be written down, and in this playbook they are. A Look Inside: The Three-Tier Offer

One rate is a trap. Three tiers turn a yes-or-no into a choice.

The playbook gives you the full table with what to include at each level, the per-session equivalent of each package, and the reason the middle tier is the one you want them to buy. This is the shape of it, before you adjust the numbers to your own city and cost floor.

Starter · 8 sessions, 4 weeks

Indicative ₹6,000 (₹750 a session). Two sessions a week, one written four-week plan, one WhatsApp check, replies within 24 hours. Its job is the low-risk first yes.

Transform · 24 sessions, 12 weeks

Indicative ₹16,000 (₹667 a session — the cheapest per session, which is exactly the story you tell). Three progressive blocks, weekly check-ins, monthly measurement, travel within 5 km.

Elite · 36 sessions, 12 weeks

Indicative ₹26,000 (₹722 a session). Three sessions a week, twice-weekly check-ins, fortnightly measurement. It anchors the middle and serves the serious client.

Part 2 also gives the adjustment rule: in Delhi NCR, Mumbai or Bengaluru multiply the middle tier by 1.5 to 2 if you have proof; in a smaller town, cut what you deliver rather than what you charge — and never take the middle tier below your cost floor.
