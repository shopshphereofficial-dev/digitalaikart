Is any of this actually free?

Yes. Gemini Notebook Standard is free with any Gmail account, and ChatGPT's free tier covers the study loop in this book. The Google AI Plus year for college students is free to claim until 31 December 2026, but converts to Rs 399 a month after the trial unless cancelled.

Do I need a laptop, or is a phone enough?

A phone is enough to start. Gemini Notebook and ChatGPT both work in a mobile browser and as apps. A laptop is more comfortable for long practice sets, but the loop works on the smallest screen.

Will AI make me lazy or dependent?

Only if you let it produce your answers. Used as the book describes - to generate practice, mark your attempts and explain your errors - it builds independence, because the work of retrieval stays with you.

Can my teacher tell if I used AI?

Institutions increasingly run AI-detection alongside plagiarism checks, and undisclosed AI text can be treated as plagiarism under existing UGC regulations. The safe path: use AI to prepare and practise, write submissions yourself, and disclose any AI help honestly.
