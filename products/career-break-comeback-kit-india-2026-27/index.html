Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

My break was three years long. Is this kit still useful?

Yes. Long breaks are the exact case the kit is built for. Every programme in Part 2 needs a minimum break of six to twenty-four months, and the gap story method in Part 3 is written for breaks measured in years, not months.

I am not in IT or a metro city. Will this work for me?

The method is function-agnostic: accounts, HR, operations, sales, support, marketing and data all appear in the examples. Part 6 gives five lanes with the exact first three steps for each, and the returnship map lists where each programme actually operates, so you can see which ones are open to your location before you spend an evening on an application.

Are the returnship details current?

Every programme row was taken from the employer's own careers page in November 2026 — Amazon rekindle, Myntra Phoenix, TCS Rebegin, Salesforce India Return to Work and Goldman Sachs India Returnship. Intakes open and close, so the kit also tells you how to re-check each one in twenty minutes, and Part 12 lists every source with the date it was checked.

Do I need a paid AI subscription to use the prompts?

No. The 24 prompts are written to work in any current assistant — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot — and the free tiers are enough for every one of them. Each prompt is plain text you copy, paste and fill in.

How is this different from a general job-search guide?

A general guide assumes a continuous career. This one assumes a break: the dated break entry, the six approved gap-story framings, the hostile versions of the gap question, the salary answer when your last drawn salary is years old, PF and gratuity continuity, and maternity rights on return.
