Do I need a paid ChatGPT subscription to use this kit?

No. Every prompt works with the free tiers of ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude. The kit even tells you which tool to use for which task and how to work around free-tier message limits by switching between them.

I'm a fresher with no work experience. Will this help me?

Yes — freshers are a primary audience. You get a dedicated fresher resume builder, a project-section builder that turns college work into professional bullets, HR-round rapid prep, walk-in drills, and internship application prompts.

Will recruiters know my resume is AI-written?

Not if you follow the kit. Prompt 09 (the De-Robotizer) strips the exact words and patterns recruiters recognize as generic AI output, and the kit's one golden rule — AI is your editor, not your author — keeps everything sounding like you.

Is it okay to use AI on job applications at all?

Using AI to present your real experience clearly and to practise interviews is smart preparation — presenting your real self at your best. What's wrong is inventing qualifications you don't have, and the kit strictly guards against that, including what data you should never paste into an AI chat.
