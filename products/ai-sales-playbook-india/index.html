Is this for experienced salespeople or beginners?

Both. It assumes you already know how to sell and gives you the AI instructions for the repetitive half of the job. Beginners get a structure to follow; experienced sellers get their hours back.

Do I need a paid AI tool to use this?

No. Every prompt works in any capable assistant — ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot or your company's tool. Start free and upgrade only when a limit pinches your week.

Does the WhatsApp section tell me to message people cold?

No — the opposite. It explains the 2026 rules: Meta requires opt-in before you message, and India's DPDP Act requires free, specific, informed consent. Cold WhatsApp can get your number restricted. The prompts are for warm, opted-in contacts only.

Is the content India-specific?

Yes. It uses the India findings of Salesforce's 2026 State of Sales report, the India WhatsApp and DPDP rules, Indian B2B channel data, and Hinglish outreach examples.

Whichever you already have. The prompts are tool-agnostic and written in plain language. A paid tool earns its money only if you send hundreds of messages a week; a free tier is fine for a dozen.
