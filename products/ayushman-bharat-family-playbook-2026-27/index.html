Everything you need to know before buying

Is this guide for me if my parents are 70 or older?

Especially for you. Every Indian citizen aged 70 and above is eligible for free health cover up to ₹5 lakh a year under the Ayushman Vay Vandana Card, regardless of income. The guide shows exactly how to enrol a parent step by step — Aadhaar is the only document needed.

Is the information verified from official sources?

Yes. Every figure and rule comes from official sources — the National Health Authority, the PM-JAY beneficiary portal, Press Information Bureau releases and government guidelines — and each number carries its date so you know how fresh it is.

My Ayushman card application was rejected. Will this help?

Yes — Part Four of the guide is written exactly for that. It covers the four real reasons cards get rejected (database, name mismatch, family ID error, eKYC failure) and the specific fix for each, plus how to escalate a complaint that gets action.

Is the Ayushman card really free?
