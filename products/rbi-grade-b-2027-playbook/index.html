The Clock Is Already Running

The RBI published its calendar for panel year 2027 on 22 October 2026

Notification: February 2027

Grade B (DR) General, DEPR and DSIM, per the Annual Recruitment Calendar for Class I posts. Phase I in April 2027, Phase II in May or June 2027. Dates are month-wise and indicative; the advertisement governs.

108.75 of 200 was the 2026 floor

The official General-category Phase I aggregate cut-off from the examination of 13 June 2026, with every sectional minimum beside it. The book shows what the gap between the two means for your target score.

Phase I does not carry forward

Final merit is Phase II (300 marks) plus the interview (75). A candidate who perfects Phase I and cannot write a 250-word answer has spent the year in the wrong place. This playbook allocates accordingly.

What You Get

Six working tools, not six chapters of motivation

The cut-off, read properly

The four sectional minimums for the General category add to 97.25; the aggregate is 108.75. That 11.5-mark gap is the cushion you have to build, and Chapter 4 turns it into a per-section target with the accuracy each one needs.

The 120-minute machine

General Awareness is 40 per cent of the paper in 21 per cent of the time, at 18.8 seconds a question. The negative-marking arithmetic is worked out, the exit rules are written, and the mock protocol is set to twelve mocks with sectional timing.

General Awareness from twelve documents

RBI's own publications, the Budget, the Economic Survey and PIB, organised into a six-bucket note system with the 1-7-30 revision loop. GA is the only section of Phase I that is fully buildable.

ESI and FM for the 2026-27 economy

Twenty themes explained in the amount an answer can use, plus a sourced fact sheet: repo at 5.50 per cent, calibrated tightening, FY27 growth projected at 7.1 per cent and CPI at 5.2 per cent, the deficit at 4.3 per cent of GDP and capex at Rs. 12.2 lakh crore.

The answer skeleton, with models

Six parts: frame, define, data, mechanism, both sides, judgement. Five full model answers on monetary policy, fiscal consolidation, digital payments, NBFCs and employment, each with a note on why it scores.

Interview and trackers

Four question families, twelve questions worth preparing, and the practicalities that decide close calls. Plus a twenty-week topic grid, a mock log, a Phase II question bank and exam-day checklists.

Which exam cycle does it cover?

Panel year 2027, for which the RBI calendar dated 22 October 2026 lists the Grade B notification in February 2027, Phase I in April 2027 and Phase II in May or June 2027. The plan is built for a November 2026 start and works from any start date, because the sequence matters more than the dates.
