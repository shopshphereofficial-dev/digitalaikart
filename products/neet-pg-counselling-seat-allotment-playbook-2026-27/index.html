Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

My Round 1 result is already out. Is this still useful?

Yes - it is written for exactly this point in the cycle. Round 2 opens on 6 November, Round 3 on 26 November and stray vacancy in mid-December. The parts that matter right now are Part 4 on Freeze and Float, Part 5 on what it costs to change your mind, Part 6 on building a choice list, and Part 10 on the documents that cancel a seat.

Which counselling systems does this cover?

All four. The 50% All India Quota run by MCC, your state's counselling for the other 50% and for private college seats, the 100% deemed university counselling run by MCC, and DNB. AIQ and deemed rules get the most detail because MCC publishes them in full; the state chapter explains what differs, how to find your portal, and what eligibility usually turns on.

Does it print a state-by-state bond table?

No, deliberately. Published bond figures for the same state disagree by crores - for one state the 2026 figures range from a flat 25 lakh to 1.5 crore depending on which order the author read. You get the four numbers to extract, where each one lives in the state's document, one worked example of how much the branch can cost, and ten questions that get the real answer from the college office.

Are the fees and dates official?

Every fee, deposit, deadline and rule comes from the MCC NEET-PG Counselling 2026 Information Bulletin, MCC's AIQ and state schedule of 8 October 2026, NBEMS result notices of 24 September 2026, the AIIMS INI-CET January 2027 prospectus, or a state counselling notice quoted by name. Nothing is estimated, and where sources conflict the book says so instead of picking a number.

Is it a rank predictor?

No. Nobody can tell you that a given rank gets a given branch, because the seat matrix and the preferences of 2.6 lakh other candidates change every round. What this book does is make sure you never lose a seat to a missed rule, a wrong document or a badly ordered list.

How is this different from free articles online?

Free coverage of this counselling is fragmented across coaching blogs with stale bond tables and recycled schedules. This is one document that follows MCC's own bulletin chapter by chapter, states the 2026 changes explicitly, and prints its source list at the end so you can check every claim.
