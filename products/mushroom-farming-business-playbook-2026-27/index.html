Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Is this useful if I have never grown a mushroom in my life?

Yes. Chapter 4 is a step-by-step oyster protocol with the temperatures, spawn rates, pasteurisation windows and timings written out, and it ends with the whole thing on one page you can pin up. The playbook assumes you have never done this, and it assumes you have no cold room, no lab and no capital beyond about twenty thousand rupees.

Where do the numbers come from?

Temperatures, spawn rates, pasteurisation windows, yields and storage conditions come from ICAR-Directorate of Mushroom Research Solan, ICAR-IIHR Bengaluru and ICAR Krishi Vigyan Kendra reports. Scheme figures come from the MIDH Operational Guidelines 2025, the KVIC PMEGP guidelines, the MoFPI PMFME guidelines and FSSAI orders. Every figure carries its source and its date, and the full source list is Appendix D.

Does it cover the government subsidy in enough detail to actually apply?

Chapter 10 reproduces the four mushroom line items from Annexure V of the MIDH Operational Guidelines 2025 with their cost norms and assistance percentages, plus the PMEGP margin money table by category and area, the PMFME 35 per cent subsidy rules, and which office to walk into for each. It also explains why you must not claim two capital subsidies on the same asset.

Is the licensing information current?

It reflects FSSAI's order of 13 March 2026, which took effect on 1 April 2026 and raised the basic registration threshold from Rs 12 lakh to Rs 1.5 crore of annual turnover, with state licences from Rs 1.5 crore to Rs 50 crore and central licences above that. Most advice still circulating online quotes the old Rs 12 lakh figure.

Which mushroom does it recommend I start with?

Oyster, in a cool month, in a cheap room you can hose down. Add milky mushroom for summer so your room is not idle for half the year. Only consider button mushroom when you can hold 14 to 16 degrees bed temperature, because button is a compost business with a refrigeration bill attached.

Will this work if I farm in a hot region?

Yes, and Chapter 6 exists for you. It covers milky mushroom at 30 to 38 degrees, paddy straw mushroom at 30 to 35 degrees, and a low-cost semi-sunken chamber that held those conditions in published trials, plus a month-by-month species calendar for the plains.

Is this a substitute for training?

No, and the book says so on its second page. Mushroom cultivation is a biological process and you learn it by losing a few bags. What the playbook does is make sure your first hundred bags fail in ways you can diagnose, and stop you signing a bank loan before you have run one batch.
