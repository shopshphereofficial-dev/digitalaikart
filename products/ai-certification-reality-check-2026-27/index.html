Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Are the exam fees in this guide current?

Every fee, exam duration, question count, validity period and retirement date was checked against the vendor's own official exam page on 28 October 2026. Where a vendor publishes a rupee price for India — Microsoft's ₹3,691 for Fundamentals, AWS's ₹8,553 for its Foundational tier — that published figure is used. Where it does not, the US dollar fee is given with a rupee estimate at ₹95 to the dollar, marked as approximate.

Which AI certification is the best value in India right now?

Microsoft Azure AI Fundamentals (AI-901). It has a published India price of ₹3,691, it never expires so there is no renewal arithmetic, and it is proctored and checkable. It is a foundational credential, so it will not impress a senior engineering manager — but for a first AI credential it is the best rupee-per-signal on the market.

Do I need a certificate to get an AI job?

No. In a 2026 conjoint experiment with 1,725 recruiters, AI skills lifted interview invitations by 8 to 15 percentage points, while formal certificates added only a modest amount on top of simply declaring the skill. The guide explains what a certificate is genuinely for, and what to build first.

Is this guide India-specific or generic?

India-specific. It uses published India prices where vendors offer them, converts dollar fees at the current rate and says so, includes Naukri's 2026 recruiter-search data on which AI skills Indian employers are actually searching for, and prices everything for a buyer paying from their own pocket.

What is the recertification trap?

Every fee table quotes the price of one attempt; almost nobody computes what a credential costs to hold. A $200 exam with two-year validity is $600 across six years. A $99 exam that never expires is $99. Part 4 does that arithmetic for all eight credentials, and it changes which one you should buy.

Is the ₹0 route really free, and is it worth anything?

Yes to both, with a limit. The guide maps eight free options and states exactly what each one gives you — a shareable badge, a certificate of completion, or a training record that is not a credential at all. Free badges prove initiative and cost nothing to test whether the subject interests you; they will not pass a screening filter that names a specific exam.
