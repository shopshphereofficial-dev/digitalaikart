Everything you need to know before buying

Who is this prompt pack for?

Insurance advisors, agents and POSPs in India — whether you work with one insurer or several. It is written for the jobs you do every day: finding leads, replying on WhatsApp, saving renewals, handling objections, guiding claims and staying compliant.

Which AI tool do I need?

Any good AI assistant works — ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or similar. Copy a prompt, replace the [brackets] with your real client details, and send. No prompt-engineering experience is needed.

Will the messages sound like AI wrote them?

Not if you paste real facts and give the draft a quick read in your own voice. The prompts are written to sound like a person. The rule throughout the pack is: AI drafts, you verify, you send.

Is this IRDAI-compliant advice?

No. This pack is a drafting and productivity tool, not insurance, legal or tax advice. Every message you send about a specific policy, benefit or claim must be checked against the policy document and IRDAI's rules, and product-specific advertising should be approved by your insurer.
