Indian Coaching Centre & Tuition Business Playbook 2027-28

The Business Side of the Blackboard — Compliance, Cash and the Batch That Stays

A 45-page working playbook for the people who run India's tuition classes and coaching centres — from a one-room Class 10 batch to a 300-seat institute. It opens with the uncomfortable arithmetic: roughly 15 lakh tuition and coaching establishments, seven crore students in private coaching, and a government that announced free online coaching for competitive exams in August 2026. Then it does the work: the nine registrations in order, the Ministry of Education's 2024 guidelines decoded clause by clause, the CCPA's November 2024 advertising rules with six rewrites of copy that would now attract a penalty, GST at 18 per cent on SAC 999293 and where the threshold trap sits, presumptive tax under section 58 of the Income-tax Act 2025, and the four numbers that tell you whether a seat makes money. Then the operating system — batch architecture, the four-test engine, faculty pay models, the fourteen-day onboarding that decides whether a student is still there in November, and the defaulter ladder. Plus a 90-day launch plan, a month-by-month Indian academic calendar, and 40 copy-paste AI prompts for admissions, parent messages, test papers and analysis.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

I run a small 25-student class. Is this too big for me?

No — it is written for exactly that size. Every compliance clause is shown twice, once for a 30-student class and once for a 300-seat institute, because the answer genuinely differs. The unit economics sheet, the defaulter ladder and the fourteen-day onboarding are built for a centre where the owner still teaches.

Are the rules current?

Yes, as of November 2026. That includes the Ministry of Education's January 2024 guidelines, the CCPA's November 2024 advertising rules, the CCPA's May 2026 dark-patterns order, the DPDP Rules 2025, GST at 18 per cent on SAC 999293, and presumptive taxation under section 58 of the Income-tax Act 2025, which took effect on 1 April 2026.

Do the coaching-centre guidelines actually apply to me?

Education is on the concurrent list, so the central guidelines bind you only once your state adopts them — and several states and union territories have. Chapter 6 explains which definition applies at which size, and why the practical answer is to assume they apply.

Is this a teaching book or a business book?

A business book. It will not teach you Physics. It covers the registrations, the money, the compliance, the timetable arithmetic, the retention systems and the marketing — the parts that decide whether the centre is still open in year three.

Will the AI prompts work with any chatbot?

Yes. They are plain-language prompts with bracketed placeholders, written for ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or any assistant you already use. There is no API, no subscription and no tool to install.
