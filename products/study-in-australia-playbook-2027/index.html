Study in Australia 2027

The Complete Playbook for Indian Students — Written After the Rules Changed

A 59-page working playbook on the Australian student visa as it stands in November 2026. It starts with the change nobody has caught up with: from 2 October 2026 most temporary visa holders can no longer lodge a Student visa onshore, and family members can only be included in a narrow set of cases — India is not one of them. Then the money, in the Department's own arithmetic: AUD 29,710 living costs, first-year fees less anything paid, travel, and the AUD 87,856 income alternative, worked through three real profiles. Then the course: the 2027 National Planning Level of 295,000 commencements, the 80 per cent rule that decides how fast your application is looked at, and the published 2027 allocation for every public university in Australia. Then the visa itself — the Genuine Student test with its four 150-word questions, the nine approved English tests with the exact score columns, the document set, the refusal patterns and their fixes. Then the honest version of the permanent residence question: the 2026-27 program where employer sponsorship rose 32 per cent and regional places fell 57 per cent, the 485 with its 92-week and six-month traps, the points test, and the AUD 79,423 salary floor. Every figure sourced and dated.

Yes. Does it cover the new rules on bringing a spouse or children?

Chapter 12 covers it in full: the narrow list of exemptions, why India is not on it, the fact that nobody can add family after the visa is granted, and what the realistic alternatives are. For a married applicant this single chapter changes the entire plan.

Can I still apply for a student visa while I am in Australia?

Only if an exemption applies. Since 2 October 2026 most temporary visa holders cannot lodge a Student visa application onshore, and an application lodged when you were required to be offshore is invalid — not refused, invalid. Chapter 12 lists every exemption.
