The Problem It Solves

A good resume is not a design problem — it is a wording problem, and most tools only sell you the design

“Responsible for daily reporting” tells a recruiter nothing

Most resumes list duties because that is what the job description said. The fix is mechanical: start with a verb, end with a number. The score pushes you to do it bullet by bullet.

You cannot see the keyword gap

The job post says “stakeholder management” and “SQL”; your resume says “dealing with people” and “databases”. Both can be true, but only one gets parsed as a match.

Word templates fight you

Tables for the layout, a text box for the contact details, a photo in the corner — every one of those is a parsing risk, and moving one line breaks the page.

Resume builders charge monthly for one page

You need a resume for a fortnight of applications, not another ₹299 a month. This is one file, one payment, unlimited resumes, no watermark.

Freshers get advice written for people with ten years

“Quantify your impact” is useless when your last job was an internship. Fresher mode scores projects, and the bundled checklist shows how to write a project bullet with numbers you actually have.

You send it, then wonder why nothing came back

There is no feedback loop in a job application. A checklist you run before sending is the closest thing to one — and it takes two minutes.

How the Score Is Built

Ten weighted checks, each with the points earned and a specific fix — no black box

  1. Name, email and phone · 12 points

    All three present in the body of page one. Without them the parser cannot create a candidate record at all.

  2. Target role line · 6 points

    A headline under your name so the resume is filed under the role you actually want.

  3. Summary length · 10 points

    Full marks at 30–80 words. Shorter reads thin; longer gets skipped.

  4. Work experience, or projects in fresher mode · 10 points

    Two roles with title and company score full marks; a fresher's two projects score six.

  5. Education · 8 points

    Degree, institute, years and score or CGPA.

  6. Skills block · 10 points

    Six or more items, in the words the job post uses.

  7. Action-verb bullets · 16 points

    The heaviest single check: how many bullets open with a verb instead of “Responsible for”.

  8. Numbers in bullets · 12 points

    Four quantified bullets score full marks; three score ten. Percentages, rupees, hours saved, team size, counts.

  9. Overall length · 6 points

    250–850 words — the range that fits one to two pages without padding.

  10. Job-description keywords · 10 points

    Added when you paste a job post: how many of its top terms appear in your resume. The missing ones are listed so you can add the ones that are true.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Is this an ebook or a tool?

It is a tool, not an ebook. You get a ZIP containing one index.html file — the builder — plus a README, an example resume, a printable ATS checklist and your licence. Double-click index.html and the builder opens in your browser. Nothing to install, no account to create.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The file has no server, no analytics and no network calls. Switch the internet off and it still builds resumes, scores them, generates cover letters and prints PDFs. Your data is saved in your own browser's local storage on your device.

What exactly does the ATS score measure?

Nine checks: contact details, a target role line, summary length, work experience or projects, education, a skills section with six or more items, bullets that start with action verbs, bullets that contain numbers, and overall length. Paste a job description and a tenth check is added: how many of that post's key terms appear in your resume. Each check shows the points earned and a specific fix.

Will this guarantee I pass every ATS?

No, and no tool can honestly promise that — applicant tracking systems differ. What this does is keep you inside the rules parsers handle reliably (single column, standard headings, selectable text, no tables or images) and show you the gaps in your content. The rest is your experience and how you describe it.

Is it good for freshers with no work experience?

Yes. Turn on Fresher / student mode and the score weights projects instead of jobs. The cover-letter generator has a fresher tone, and the bundled checklist explains how to write project bullets when you have no job history.

How do I get a PDF?

Click Print / Save PDF, then choose Save as PDF in your browser's print dialog with A4 paper, margins set to None, scale 100% and "Headers and footers" switched off. The app prints only the resume, never the editing interface.

Can I use it on my phone?

The editor works in mobile browsers, but the print-to-PDF step is best done on a laptop or desktop. Most people fill the resume on a laptop and keep the JSON backup in Drive to open on another machine.
