Frequently Asked Questions

Everything to know before you buy

Is this book medical or legal advice?

No. It is a navigation manual. It does not diagnose, treat or give legal opinions. It explains what the RPwD Act, the UDID scheme, the ADIP and Niramaya schemes, the CBSE circulars and the income-tax provisions actually say, and exactly how to apply — so that your money goes to clinicians and your time goes to the right counters.

Which disabilities does it cover?

It is written around the 21 specified disabilities in the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016, with the deepest detail on the developmental ones: autism spectrum disorder, intellectual disability, specific learning disability such as dyslexia, ADHD, cerebral palsy, global developmental delay, speech and language delay and hearing impairment.

My child already has a disability certificate. Is it still useful?

Yes — and for exactly the chapters that matter at that stage: Niramaya's sub-limits and claim rules, the ADIP device amounts, the Section 127 and Section 154 deductions, the scholarship windows, the CBSE concessions and the Class 9 and Class 11 registration trap. Most families with a certificate are still paying for therapy that a DEIC or DDRC would provide free.

How current are the facts?

Every amount, section number, scheme rule and portal name was checked against official sources in November 2026: the RPwD Act text, the DEPwD and UDID portal SOPs, the RBSK 2.0 guidelines of 2026, the National Trust's Niramaya pages, the revised ADIP scheme, the DEPwD scholarship guidelines, the CBSE circular and exemption manual, IRDAI's 2023 circular and the Income Tax Department's section mapping. Every source is listed in Appendix A4.
