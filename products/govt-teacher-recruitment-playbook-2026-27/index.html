Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Does passing CTET or a state TET give me a teaching job?

No. The CBSE says so in its own CTET FAQ — qualifying does not confer a right to recruitment or employment, it is one eligibility condition among several. Appointment needs a separate state recruitment exam and then document verification. Chapter 1 lays out all four gates and shows where the competition really sits.

Is this a CTET preparation book?

No. It is not a question bank. It is a guide to the recruitment system: which qualification and which TET paper opens which door, the live 2026-27 drives with their dates and patterns, how negative marking changes your strategy, and what happens at verification. Chapter 26 sets out what the papers test, so you know what to study — but the practice papers are your job.

Which exams does it cover in detail?

Bihar BPSC TRE 4.0 (33,320 posts, Advertisement 15/2026), Uttar Pradesh UPESSC TGT 2026 (17,735 posts, Advertisement 07/2026) and Andhra Pradesh DSC-2026 / APTRT-2026 — plus the central KVS and NVS route, and the TET systems of Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh and Himachal Pradesh.

Are the dates, fees and marks verified?

Yes. Every date, fee, mark, pay level and eligibility condition was taken from an official notification, commission advertisement, board prospectus or regulator circular, and verified on 30 October 2026. Where a figure came from press reporting rather than an official document, the text says so. Chapter 31 names every source.

I passed my TET years ago — is it still valid?

Almost certainly. NCTE extended TET certificate validity from seven years to lifetime in June 2021, with retrospective effect from February 2011, and directed states to revalidate certificates that had lapsed. Chapter 9 explains how to confirm it with your issuing board.

Will it help if I am applying outside my home state?

Yes. Chapter 27 covers what travels with you — your degree, your TET, your age — and what does not, including domicile-linked reservation and Andhra Pradesh's district cadre. The age differences alone (Bihar 37, UP 62, KVS 30 to 40) make this worth reading before you give up on a state.
