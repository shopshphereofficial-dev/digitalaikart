Six Facts That Change What You Do This Week

Most of what is published about Indian company registration is out of date. These are verified to November 2026.

Government fee is nil

SPICe+ charges no incorporation filing fee where authorised capital is up to Rs 15,00,000. You pay stamp duty, Rs 131 for PAN and TAN, and your DSC — not a Rs 7,000 fee.

Director KYC is now every three years

From 31 March 2026 the annual DIR-3 KYC is gone. Compliant directors next file by 30 June 2028, in a new single form. Guides saying 30 September are a year out of date.

A small company is much bigger

Since 1 December 2025, paid-up capital up to Rs 10 crore and turnover up to Rs 100 crore qualifies as a small company and files the shorter MGT-7A.

INC-20A is the deadline that hurts

Within 180 days you must declare subscribers have paid for their shares. Miss it: Rs 50,000 on the company, Rs 1,000 a day per officer, and no legal trading.

Filing fees waived right now

MCA General Circular 05/2026 lets FY 2025-26 AOC-4 be filed to 31 December 2026 and MGT-7 or 7A to 31 January 2027 without additional fees.

A company is audited every year

No turnover threshold, no exemption. An LLP with turnover under Rs 40 lakh and contribution under Rs 25 lakh needs no audit at all — which changes the arithmetic.
