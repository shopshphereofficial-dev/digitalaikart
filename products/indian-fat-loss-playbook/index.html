Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Do I have to give up rice and roti?

No. Carbohydrate is not the enemy; portion and context are. Keep one to two rotis or a small bowl of rice, but build the rest of the plate from vegetables and protein. Most Indian diets fail from too much refined carb and too little protein, not from rice itself.

How fast will I lose weight?

At a 400-500 calorie daily deficit, expect roughly 0.5 kg of fat per week, with normal water fluctuation on top. Weigh monthly for the true trend and track your waist weekly.

Is this book medical advice?

No. It is general education built from official sources such as NFHS-6, ICMR-NIN and the WHO. It does not replace a doctor. If you are pregnant, under 18, on medication, or managing a chronic condition, consult a qualified professional before changing how you eat or move.

Do I need protein powder or a gym?

No. ICMR-NIN advises getting protein from food and avoiding supplements for healthy people building muscle. The plan uses dal, curd, paneer, eggs, soya and chicken, and a no-equipment home workout library.

Should I consider a weight-loss medicine like Ozempic or Mounjaro?

That is a decision for you and a qualified doctor, not a self-service purchase. The guide explains the 2026 India landscape honestly, including costs, muscle-loss risk and weight regain after stopping.
