Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Is this an ebook or a software tool?

It is neither. You get a Microsoft Excel / Google Sheets workbook (.xlsx) with live formulas — nine sheets covering a dashboard, invoices, clients, expenses, a monthly summary, a tax planner, settings and a reference sheet. There are no macros, nothing to install and no account to create.

What do I need to open it?

Microsoft Excel 2016 or later, Microsoft 365, Google Sheets or LibreOffice Calc, on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android or iOS. There are no macros, so nothing needs to be enabled.

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. Upload the .xlsx to Google Drive, open it with Google Sheets and save it as a Google Sheet. The formulas, charts and dropdowns carry over. The README has the step-by-step steps, including what to do if a dropdown needs re-pointing.

Does it calculate my income tax?

It estimates it. The Tax Planner compares regular books against the presumptive schemes (44ADA 50% for professionals, 44AD 8% for business) and applies the FY 2026-27 new-regime slabs, including the ₹60,000 rebate under section 87A (nil tax up to ₹12 lakh taxable income), marginal relief just above ₹12 lakh and the 4% health and education cess. It then nets off your TDS credit and any advance tax you have already paid. The figures are indicative — verify them with your chartered accountant or at incometax.gov.in before you file or pay.

Does it handle TDS and GST?

Yes. Each invoice carries a TDS percentage and works out the TDS deducted and the amount that actually reaches your bank. There is an input-GST column on expenses and a GST quick view (output GST, input GST and net). It does not file GST or income-tax returns, and it does not generate e-invoices.

How many invoices and expenses can I track?

Two hundred invoice rows and two hundred expense rows, with a client book of thirty. That is comfortably more than a full financial year for most freelancers, and the ranges are easy to extend if you need more.

Is it a subscription?

No. It is a one-time purchase. There is no monthly or yearly fee, no per-invoice charge, and you keep the file forever.
