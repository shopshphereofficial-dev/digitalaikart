The Problem It Solves

HR paperwork is not hard. It is just always due today.

Someone accepts a job on Saturday and starts on Monday. You need an offer letter, and what you have is a Word file from three jobs ago with another company's name in the footer. Six months later a probation period quietly ends and nobody writes it down, so the notice period becomes an argument. Then there is the month when a delivery assistant keeps coming late, and the warning you send is three lines long with no dates, no policy and no consequence — which is worth nothing if it is ever questioned.

The other half of the problem is arithmetic. Leave is tracked in a notebook, the leave balance is whatever someone remembers, and the full and final settlement is worked out on a calculator at the counter while the person waits. StaffKit HR is the boring, complete version of all of that: letters that already say the right things in the right order, and a workbook that calculates instead of remembering.

The Workbook That Keeps the Register

Five working sheets, formulas included, no macros

👥 Staff Register

One row per employee with designation, department, joining date, monthly gross and basic, PF and ESI status, PAN, UAN, bank details and status. Annual CTC and years of service are calculated; the total row adds up active staff and their cost.

🗓️ Leave Register

One row per application. Type the employee code and the name fills in; the days calculate from the from and to dates. Leave type and status come from dropdowns.

📊 Leave Balance

Entitlement, used and balance for earned, casual and sick leave per person, with SUMIFS pulling only approved applications. A negative balance turns red and the total balance runs on a colour scale.

🧮 FnF Calculator

Salary for days worked, leave encashment on basic plus DA, notice shortfall recovered, gratuity if five years are complete, and the net payable — plus a service-length check that tells you whether gratuity applies at all.

📚 Lists & Read Me

Every dropdown value lives on one Lists sheet — departments, employment types, leave types, statuses — so you can extend them without touching a formula. The Read Me explains the colour key in three lines.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Is this an ebook or actual files?

Actual files. The ZIP contains 18 Word documents (.docx) and one Excel workbook (.xlsx), plus a start-here guide, a read-me and the licence. You open them in Word, LibreOffice or Google Docs and edit the text directly — nothing to install and nothing to sign up for.

Can I edit these in Google Docs?

Yes. Upload a .docx to Google Drive, open it with Google Docs, edit it, then download it back as a PDF to send. The headings, tables and letterhead carry across. Very long tables can reflow slightly, so check the page breaks before you send anything important.

Do the templates include my company letterhead?

Each document starts with a letterhead block holding your company name, address, phone, email and GSTIN or CIN. Replace it once and save that copy as your master, or paste your three letterhead lines into each file as you use it. A logo goes in with Insert → Pictures, about 3 cm high.

How do I know which field to fill in?

Every field you must complete is written inside square brackets and prints bold in dark gold, so it is impossible to miss. The guide has a placeholder cheat sheet, and suggests using Find & Replace to change the company name, the signatory and the HR email across a whole document in one go.

Are these legally checked documents?

They are drafted around how Indian small businesses actually hire, and are aligned to the 50 per cent wage rule under the Code on Wages, 2019 and the two-working-day settlement requirement for wages. They are templates, not legal advice. For a termination, a disciplinary matter or anything a lawyer may later read, have your own adviser check the wording first.

What does the Excel workbook calculate?

The Staff Register works out annual CTC from the monthly gross and the PF status, and years of service from the joining date. The Leave Register calculates days from the from and to dates and pulls the name from the employee code. Leave Balance adds up approved leave by type with SUMIFS and shows what is left. The FnF Calculator works out salary for days worked, leave encashment, notice recovery, gratuity if five years are complete, and the net payable.

Does it file PF, ESI, professional tax or Form 16?

No. This pack is documentation, not filing software. It does not connect to the EPFO, ESIC, any state portal or the income tax portal, and it does not generate ECR, challans, 24Q or Form 16. The staff register gives your accountant the headcount, salary structure and joining dates they need for those filings.

Is the settlement calculator accurate?

It uses the standard arithmetic: salary and notice recovery at monthly gross divided by 30, leave encashment at basic plus DA divided by 30, and gratuity at 15/26 of basic plus DA for each completed year after five years of service. Your policy, your state rules and the facts of the case decide the rest — check the figures with your accountant before you pay, and remember that wages due must be paid within two working days of the last working day under the Code on Wages, 2019.

How many employees can I manage with the workbook?
