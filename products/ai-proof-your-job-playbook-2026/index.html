Who is this playbook for?

It is for salaried Indian professionals who want to stay valuable in their current job while AI changes how work is done — mid-career employees, specialists and team leads. It is not a job-hunting guide; it assumes you already have a job and want to keep and grow it.

Is the information current?

Yes. Every number is from a named source dated 2025–2026 — Nomura, the ICRIER study with OpenAI, Gallup, SHRM, Quess Corp, the World Economic Forum and Indian business press. The AI tool price sheet was checked in October 2026.

No, and the book says so honestly. What it gives you is the evidence on which work is exposed, the capabilities Indian employers are paying a premium for right now, and the exact weekly habits that make a professional expensive to replace. You still have to do the work.
