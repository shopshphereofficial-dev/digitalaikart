What's Inside

Eight parts, thirty-five numbered sections, nothing padded

🏛️ The clinic's licence

Level 1 means IUI only. Level 2 means IVF. How to read the level code in a registration number on the national registry, download the certificate, and compare it with the one on the wall.

💰 2026 price bands

IUI at ₹8,000-30,000 a cycle; a fresh IVF cycle at ₹1.5-3.3 lakh with medicines; ICSI, freezing, frozen transfers, PGT-A and donor cycles priced separately, with the sources named.

📉 Honest odds

Live birth per cycle by age from the 2024 US national data, the UK regulator's 2024 report, and the arithmetic of what two or three cycles really buys you.

⚖️ Your legal rights

Section-by-section: counselling before consent, written consent, a cap of three embryos per cycle, one donor per couple, records kept ten years, a discharge certificate, a grievance cell.

🚩 Nine red flags

Guaranteed pregnancies, instant "fresh donors", PGT-A pushed on a 30-year-old, add-on stacking — and the one offer that is a criminal offence with a five-year minimum sentence.

📋 Five worksheets

Two-quote budget planner, clinic scorecard, twenty-question consultation sheet, cycle tracker, and a decision log with a stop rule you sign before cycle one.
