Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Who is this playbook for?

Every Indian household with a phone and a bank account — and especially anyone with elderly parents. It covers the 12 scams emptying Indian accounts in 2026, how to lock your money down in about an hour, and exactly what to do in the first 60 minutes after a fraud.

Is the content up to date?

Yes. Every figure, rule and helpline is drawn from official Indian sources as of October 2026 — the Ministry of Home Affairs, I4C, the RBI, NPCI, the Department of Telecommunications and Supreme Court directions.

Is there really no such thing as a digital arrest?

Correct. No Indian law allows anyone to be arrested or investigated over a phone or video call, and no agency asks you to transfer money to avoid arrest. The playbook explains the full four-stage script and the one line that ends the call.

Can I actually recover money after a cyber fraud?

Sometimes — and it depends almost entirely on speed. The playbook shows the Golden Hour steps, the 1930 and cybercrime.gov.in walkthrough, the RBI's liability rules, and the four-rung recovery ladder that gets frozen money back to you.

Does it help if I already lost money?

Yes. Part 4 is a full recovery manual: the first-60-minutes checklist, ready-to-send templates for your bank, the police and the RBI Ombudsman, and the exact legal route to release frozen funds.

Is this legal or financial advice?

No. It is an educational guide that explains published Indian rules and official procedures. It is not legal or financial advice — for your specific case, speak to your bank and, if needed, a lawyer.
