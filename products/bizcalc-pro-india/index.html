What You Can Do With It

Eight calculators, each built for the way India actually borrows, bills and prices

🏦 Loan EMI & Prepayment

Reducing-balance EMI, total interest and total payment — plus a full month-by-month amortization schedule and a prepayment box that shows the interest and months you save.

⚖️ Loan Comparison

Put up to three loan offers side by side on amount, rate and tenure. The lowest total cost is flagged, and you see exactly how much more the others cost.

📋 Loan Eligibility (FOIR)

Enter your net income, existing EMIs and the lender's FOIR to estimate the loan amount you may be sanctioned at a given rate and tenure.

🧾 GST Add / Remove

Add GST to a base price or pull it out of an inclusive price, with the CGST/SGST split and the single IGST figure shown for every standard slab.

🏷️ Margin & Markup

Turn a cost and selling price into profit, margin and markup — or work out the price you must charge to hit a target margin.

📊 Break-even

Contribution per unit, break-even units and revenue, and how many units you need for a target monthly profit.

💹 SIP & Compound Growth

Project a monthly SIP or a one-time lumpsum — invested amount, estimated gain and total value, compounded monthly.

🛍️ Discount & Price

Work out the final price from an MRP and a discount, or the original price from a discounted one — useful for sales and offers.

Everything you need to know before buying

Is this a software tool or a book?

It is a small software tool, not an ebook. You get a folder with an index.html file; you double-click it and it opens in your browser as a working set of calculators. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once the folder is on your device, every calculator works with the internet switched off. Nothing is uploaded and your inputs stay in your own browser.

What exactly can I calculate?

Eight calculators: loan EMI with a full month-by-month amortization schedule and prepayment savings; a side-by-side loan comparison for up to three offers; loan eligibility using your income, existing EMIs and FOIR; GST added to or removed from a price with the CGST/SGST split; margin and markup; break-even units and revenue; SIP and lumpsum compound growth; and discount pricing.

Can I save my working?

Yes. Save scenarios by name in your browser, export and import them as a small JSON file, and print any calculator's results or save them as a PDF from your browser's print dialog.

Are the EMI and GST figures exact?

The maths uses the standard reducing-balance EMI formula and the GST add/remove formulas, so the numbers are accurate for the inputs you give. But lenders add processing fees, insurance and their own eligibility rules, and GST slabs can change. Treat the results as estimates and confirm the current position with your bank or CA.
