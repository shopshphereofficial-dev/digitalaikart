Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

My cashless request was denied at the hospital. Is my claim rejected?

No — and this is the most expensive misunderstanding in Indian health insurance. A cashless denial is only a refusal to pre-authorise, not a repudiation. Take the denial note in writing, pay the hospital, collect the discharge summary and itemised bills, and file the same claim as a reimbursement claim, which the insurer must decide within 30 days of your last document. Many cashless denials on thin grounds are paid on reimbursement.

Is the content current and verified?

Yes. Every rule, deadline and statistic was verified in September 2026 against the IRDAI Master Circular on Health Insurance Business dated 29 May 2024, the IRDAI (Insurance Products) Regulations 2024, the Cashless Everywhere circular of 31 January 2024, IRDAI's official policyholder pages, the Council for Insurance Ombudsmen, Finance Ministry data presented in Parliament, and the Insurance Ombudsman framework. The full source list is printed in the book.

My claim was already rejected. Will this book still help?

That is exactly what Parts 4 and 5 are built for. You get the counter to each of the 12 most common rejection grounds, a fill-in appeal letter that demands the policy clause and the Claims Review Committee approval date, and the complete free escalation ladder — Grievance Redressal Officer (15 days), Bima Bharosa portal, and the Insurance Ombudsman, which is free and passes awards within three months.

Is escalating to the Ombudsman expensive or complicated?

It is completely free — no fees, no lawyer needed. You file a written, signed complaint (a fill-in template is in the book) at the Ombudsman centre with jurisdiction over your address, within one year of the insurer's rejection or unsatisfactory reply. Mediation recommendations can come within a month and awards within three months; ignoring an award costs the insurer ₹5,000 per day.

Does the book cover buying a policy, or only claims?

Both. The 30-minute Day One Audit worksheet and the non-disclosure trap section prevent disputes before they start, and Part 7 shows how renewals, the grace period and portability protect the waiting periods, no-claim bonus and the 60-month moratorium you have already earned when you buy, renew or switch policies.
