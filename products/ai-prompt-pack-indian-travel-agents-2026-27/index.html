Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Do I need to know anything about AI to use this pack?

No. Every prompt is written to be pasted into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any modern assistant. You fill in the square brackets with your client's details and edit the result. Page 1 explains the five habits that make every prompt work, and there is a Master Context block you paste once and reuse all season.

Will AI invent hotels, prices or visa rules for my clients?

It will if you let it — which is exactly the problem this pack is built around. Every planning prompt carries a hard rule: do not invent attractions, timings, drive times, visa fees or permit rules, and write 'confirm locally' instead of guessing. Appendix E is a verification checklist to run before anything reaches a client. AI drafts; you verify.

Are the GST, TCS and WhatsApp figures current?

Yes. The WhatsApp figures are Meta's published India rates effective 1 October 2026, and the GST and TCS positions reflect the law as checked in October 2026 — including the flat 2% TCS on overseas tour packages from 1 April 2026. Tax rules change, so every figure is presented as a starting point and the pack tells you to confirm it with your chartered accountant.

Is this only for winter, or can I use it all year?

The calendar in Appendix A is built for the 2026-27 winter season, because that is when the bookings are. But all 100 prompts work all year — enquiries, itineraries, quotes, supplier emails, marketing and the paperwork do not change with the season.
