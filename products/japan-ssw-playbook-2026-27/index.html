What Makes This Different

Six things no agent will tell you, each traced to an official source

🚫 Food Service Is Closed

The Immigration Services Agency's own page says it is "not accepting new applications at this time". If an agent offers you a restaurant placement today, the conversation is over.

🏦 Rs 35,400, Not Rs 4 Lakh

Under the Emigration Act 1983 a registered agent may not collect more than Rs 30,000 plus 18% GST, with a receipt. The gap between that and the quoted packages is the fraud.

📈 The 33% Pass Rate

In the August–September 2026 window, 535 candidates sat the JFT-Basic in India and 180 reached A2.2. The language test is the real filter, and it is a speed test.

💵 77%, Not 100%

Gross to net, line by line, with the 2026 Japanese social insurance rates. About 82% reaches your account in year one and about 77% from year two.

🔒 The Family Rule

On SSW (i) you cannot bring your spouse or children to Japan for five years. Only SSW (ii) allows it — and only 11 of the 19 fields reach SSW (ii).

📅 April 2027 Changes Everything

A new three-year training visa replaces the old intern programme on 1 April 2027, with an A1 language bar instead of A2.2. Chapter 18 explains who should use it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Do I need to know Japanese to work in Japan on this visa?

Yes. A2.2 equivalent Japanese is a condition of the Specified Skilled Worker (i) visa, proven by the JFT-Basic test or JLPT N4 and above. Chapter 4 explains the scoring bands, and in the August–September 2026 window in India only 33.6 per cent of candidates who sat the test reached A2.2.

How much can I legally be charged by a recruitment agent?

A recruiting agent registered in India may not collect more than Rs 30,000 plus 18 per cent GST under the Emigration Act 1983 and the rules made under it — Rs 35,400 in total — and must issue a receipt. Chapter 11 sets out the full legal ceiling, the six verification checks, and what to do if you have already paid.

How much does a Specified Skilled Worker actually earn?

Japan's Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare recorded monthly contractual cash earnings of JPY 254,100 for Specified Skilled Worker staff in its 2026 survey. First jobs typically range from JPY 180,000 to 260,000 gross per month depending on field, prefecture and overtime. Chapter 12 works through the deductions to show what actually reaches your bank account.

Is this book useful if I have already spoken to an agent?

Especially then. Chapter 11 gives you the exact legal ceiling, a two-minute check on the agent's registration, the payment route that keeps a receipt meaningful, and the complaint channels — the Protector General of Emigrants, the MADAD portal and the Overseas Workers Resource Centre — if money has already been taken.

How current is the information?

Every figure was checked against official sources in November 2026: the Immigration Services Agency, the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare, the Japan Foundation, Prometric Japan, the Ministry of External Affairs, NSDC International and the two embassies.
