Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan for Digitalaikart AI?

Yes. The Free plan costs ₹0 and includes 50 credits every month — and you get 5 free messages as a guest before you even sign up. Paid plans start at ₹199 per month.

How do credits work?

Every action spends credits. A chat message costs 10 credits on Astral Lite or Core, 20 on Astral Pro and 50 on Astral Max. An image costs 300 credits on Astral Image, 550 on Image Pro and 1,100 on Image Ultra. Your allowance refills automatically every month.

What are the Astral tiers?

Astral is Digitalaikart's own assistant. Lite and Core are fast and cheap for everyday questions; Pro and Max think harder for complex work. Image, Image Pro and Image Ultra create pictures, with higher tiers giving better quality. Higher tiers cost more credits because they take more computing power.

Which plan includes digital products free?

The Starter and Pro plans include the AI Job Hunt Kit free. The Ultimate plan (₹999 per month) includes all Digitalaikart digital products free — including the GPT-6 Astra — Zero to Hero Guide and every future product.

Can I cancel my plan anytime?

Yes. All plans are month-to-month with no long-term contract, so you can cancel anytime.

What payment methods can I use?

Payments are processed securely by Razorpay, so you can pay with UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking and popular wallets. All prices are in Indian rupees.
