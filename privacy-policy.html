Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Digitalaikart ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website digitalkartai.shop or purchase our products.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Payment Information: When you make a purchase, payment is processed by Razorpay. We do not store your card details, UPI ID, or bank account information. Razorpay handles all payment data securely on their servers.
  • Contact Information: If you email us at lonefaisal977@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp, we receive your email address, phone number, and message content.
  • Technical Data: We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website — which pages are popular, how long people stay, and where they come from. This data is anonymous and aggregated, and cannot be used to personally identify you. Basic information like your browser type and pages visited may also be collected by our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) for security and performance purposes.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders (digital downloads and physical books)
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • To send order confirmations and shipping updates (for printed books)
  • To improve our website, products, and services
  • To prevent fraud and protect against unauthorized transactions

Payment Processing

All payments are processed through Razorpay, a PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway. Your payment information is encrypted and transmitted directly to Razorpay's servers. We never see or store your full card number, CVV, or net banking credentials.

Cookies

We use Google Analytics cookies to understand how visitors use our website — which pages are popular, how long people stay, and where they come from. This data is anonymous and aggregated. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies for personalized ads. Third-party services like Razorpay may use their own cookies during the payment process.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We only share data with:

  • Razorpay — for processing payments
  • Google Analytics — for anonymous, aggregated website usage statistics
  • GitHub Pages — for hosting our website
  • Courier services — for delivering printed books (name, address, phone only)

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of any future communications

To exercise these rights, email us at lonefaisal977@gmail.com.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data. Our website is served over HTTPS with SSL encryption. Payment data is handled by Razorpay's secure infrastructure.

Children's Privacy

Our website and products are not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.
