Last updated: September 15, 2026

Digital Products (PDF Ebooks, Templates, Digital Downloads)

No refunds are offered for digital products. Due to the nature of digital products — they can be instantly downloaded and duplicated — all sales of digital products are final and non-refundable.

Please review the product description, features, and FAQ carefully before making a purchase. If you have any questions about a product before buying, contact us at lonefaisal977@gmail.com or WhatsApp at +91 96826 00301.

Exceptions for Digital Products

We will provide a replacement or resolution in the following cases:

  • The downloaded PDF file is corrupted, damaged, or incomplete
  • You were charged incorrectly (e.g., charged twice for the same product)
  • The product you received is materially different from what was described on the product page

To request a replacement, email lonefaisal977@gmail.com within 7 days of purchase with your order details and a description of the issue.

Printed Books (Physical Products)

For the Digital + Printed Edition, the following refund policy applies to the physical book component:

  • Damaged or Defective Book: If your printed book arrives damaged or defective, contact us within 7 days of delivery with a photo of the damage. We will send a replacement at no extra cost.

  • Wrong Book Delivered: If you receive the wrong book, contact us within 7 days for a replacement.

Note: The digital PDF portion of the Digital + Printed Edition is non-refundable regardless of any physical book issue.

Cancellations

  • Digital Products: Orders are processed instantly and cannot be cancelled once payment is completed.
  • Printed Books: If the book has not yet been shipped, you may request cancellation by emailing lonefaisal977@gmail.com. If the book has already been shipped, cancellation is not possible.
